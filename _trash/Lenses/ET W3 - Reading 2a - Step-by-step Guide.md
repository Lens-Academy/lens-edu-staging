\# 2. Step-by-step Guide: How to Generate and Select Your Top Question

Now that you've seen how a problem becomes a research question, here's the step-by-step process you'll follow this week. Each step matches a step in your Week 3 Worksheet, so feel free to move between the reading and the worksheet as you go!

- **Step 1: Bottlenecks to Progress on your problem area.** Map what's already being tried in your problem area and diagnose its key bottleneck.
- **Step 2: Generating a Wide Set of Candidate Questions.** Brainstorm around 5 to 10 impactful candidate questions.
- **Step 3: Iterative Narrowing.** Narrow down your list step by step.
- **Step 4: Choosing Your Top Question(s) with a Weighted Factor Model.** Use a Weighted Factor Model (WFM) to select your top 1-2 questions to carry forward.
