\# Post-Program Feedback & Certificate of Completion

You did it! 🎉 Thank you so much for going through the Facilitator Training Program. Over these five modules, you've worked on the shift from teaching to facilitating, how people learn through discussion, how to prepare and open a session, the in-the-moment craft of running one, and how to handle hard moments, coach one-on-one and close well. That's real work, and we're so grateful you brought your curiosity and care to it!

---

\# Help us make this program even better

Your honest reflections, about what worked, what didn't, and what you wish had been different, help us improve the training for the next facilitators. There are no right or wrong answers, and we read every response!
