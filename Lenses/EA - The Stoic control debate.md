#### Text
content::
Epictetus divides things into what is up to you and what is not. Most things you care about, such as a job, a race or a relationship, seem to be partly up to you. The philosopher William Irvine proposed a third category for these in a 2009 book. Michael Tremblay, who writes about Stoicism, argues against it. He writes from inside the Stoic community.
