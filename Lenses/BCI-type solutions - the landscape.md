#### Text
content::
Most AI-safety plans work on the *AI*: constrain it, align it, govern it. This family bets on a different bottleneck — the **human-AI capability and bandwidth gap**. Humans think slowly, communicate slowly, tire, coordinate badly, and give low-bandwidth feedback; as AI outpaces us, we lose the ability to supervise, specify values, and stay in control. **BCI-type solutions try to change the *human* side** — enhance, merge with, emulate, or better-interface human minds — so people stay competitive with, in control of, or fused into AI.

This reading (a distillation of the Foresight neurotech-and-alignment workshop) is the best single **map** of the space. Watch for two things: the **six clusters** it identifies, and the **soft-to-hard spectrum** — from cyborgism and wearable sensors that ship today, to invasive high-bandwidth BCIs and whole-brain emulation that remain speculative.

#### Text
content::
The sub-threads you'll meet in the rest of this module:

- **Merge** — high-bandwidth BCI coupling humans and AI (Neuralink; Urban's "tertiary layer").
- **Cyborgism / symbiosis** — software-only human-in-the-loop tools that amplify human agency without handing off to autonomous agents.
- **Cognitive enhancement** — make humans (especially alignment researchers) smarter/faster via pharmacology, genetics, or BCI-as-prosthesis.
- **Whole-brain emulation** — scan and emulate brains to produce human-derived digital minds that inherit human values by construction.
- **Feedback / value-learning** — use neural signals as richer-than-language data to improve oversight.

The unifying hope is **differential progress**: reorder the race so human-aligned emulations or cyborg teams arrive *before* unaligned de novo AGI.
