#### Text
content::
You have a shortlist. A weighted factor model is how you stop circling it.

The idea is simple. Choose the factors you will judge each question against. Weight them by what matters most to you, splitting 100 points across them. Score each question against each factor from 1 to 10. Multiply each score by its weight, sum across factors, and see which questions rise.

The grid below starts with the Accelerator's suggested factors and weights. **Change them.** The weights are the part of this exercise that is actually about you, and the default split is a starting point rather than an answer.

#### Text
content::
⭐ If your top-scoring question is not your gut favourite, sit with that tension for a moment before deciding. Do not just override the model, and do not just override your gut.

Most of the time that gap is telling you something specific: a factor you genuinely care about is either missing from the list or weighted lower than you actually feel it. Adding it, or moving its weight, is a better response than picking a winner by feel.
