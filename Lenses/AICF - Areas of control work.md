Ryan Greenblatt's list of research areas is a map drawn from inside the field, from Redwood's point of view, in April 2025. The next lessons add other views. #### Question: Open

content::
Without looking back, name as many of Greenblatt's areas as you can. Which one does he think deserves the most effort, and why does he think work on it is useful now, when today's models are not yet dangerous?
force-feedback:: first
feedback-instructions:: The learner recalls Greenblatt's areas. The eight: (1) settings and control evaluations, (2) control-relevant capabilities and model properties, (3) countermeasures developed in isolation, (4) control experiments on actual AI usage, (5) software infrastructure and security measures, (6) human processes such as auditing and escalation, (7) conceptual research, (8) nearer-term applications such as misuse. He gives settings and control evaluations the most, perhaps a third of current control resources. Work on it is useful now to practise the methodology, find better techniques, and build settings that will be useful once models are smarter, since current models are not actually risky. Reply in at most four sentences: say how many areas they got, name one or two they missed, and correct any error. Missing areas is normal. One turn, no follow-up question, no generic praise. Tell them to move on.

#### Question: Open
id:: a8b23e08-e64a-4df4-947e-201cd5b2e4ff
content::
Greenblatt says some of this work is probably most doable at AI companies. Which areas would you expect to need access to a company's own systems or staff, and which could an outside group do? Give one example of each, with a reason.
force-feedback:: first
feedback-instructions:: From the reading: human processes for control are "probably most doable at AI companies", and for software infrastructure and security measures "it's unclear how much of this prototyping work could effectively happen outside of frontier AI companies", and getting measures implemented may need politicking inside the company. Experiments on actual AI usage are most interesting with participants inside an AI company. Settings and control evaluations, capability studies, countermeasures in isolation and conceptual work can largely be done outside. A good answer gives one of each with a reason about access to real systems, data or staff. Accept other reasoned answers. Reply in at most four sentences. Up to two turns. No generic praise.
