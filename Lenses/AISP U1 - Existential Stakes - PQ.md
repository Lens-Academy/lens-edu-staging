\## Before reading

A government has enough money to fund only one of two safety programmes. **Programme A** reduces the chance of a catastrophe that would kill 100 million people. **Programme B** reduces a much more uncertain risk that could permanently end humanity's future. You do not yet know the exact probability of either catastrophe, and you do not know how effective either programme is.

Spend about two minutes on this. **What information would you want before deciding which programme deserves more resources?** Do not try to solve the whole problem. List the considerations you think could change the answer.

feedback-instructions::
The learner has not yet seen the unit's argument about existential risk. Respond once, in 50 to 90 words. Reflect back one or two considerations they identified. If their response focuses only on the number of immediate deaths, note neutrally that this is one possible dimension and tell them the next section introduces another. Do not explain existential risk, longtermism, Bostrom, Parfit, future generations, or the intended answer. Do not grade. Do not praise generically. End by sending them to the reading.
