#### Question: Open
id:: 88d3b984-c6f8-4fc8-aefc-74fde0e5d49b
content::
Assume future AI systems become much more capable than today's systems.

List as many *different causal routes* to global catastrophe as you can in three minutes. Try to avoid giving the same story several names.

feedback-instructions::
Respond once in 60 to 100 words. Reflect how many genuinely different mechanisms the learner generated. Do not reveal the course taxonomy. If all examples are takeover variants, simply note that they share a common mechanism and send the learner onward.
