summary_for_tutor: "Reports the 2023 Expert Survey on Progress in AI (ESPAI), which surveyed 2,778 researchers who published in six top AI venues about the pace and impacts of AI. Aggregate forecasts give 50% odds of many milestones by 2028, a 50% chance of unaided machines outperforming humans at every task by 2047 (10% by 2027), and full automation of all occupations at 50% by 2116. On risk, 68.3% thought good outcomes more likely than bad, yet between 37.8% and 51.4% gave at least a 10% chance to extinction-level outcomes, and a majority favored prioritizing research to minimize AI risks. Methods include framing-effect controls (fixed-years vs fixed-probabilities) and gamma-distribution aggregation of respondents' year-probability estimates."
