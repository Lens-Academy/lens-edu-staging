#### Question: Open
id:: d6c4d8b9-faee-455e-8219-acaf7f3eb7d1
content::
Name two or three approaches to happiness or to understanding the mind that you already know something about. They can be anything: a therapy, a meditation tradition, a philosophy, a book's programme. For each, guess two things: which kind of happiness from module 2 it aims at (or whether it aims at insight instead), and its main method.
feedback-instructions:: This is a pre-reading question. The learner names two or three schools they already know and guesses for each its goal, using the kinds of happiness from module 2 (positive feeling, life satisfaction, eudaimonia, ataraxia or apatheia, sukha, contentment, fundamental wellbeing, equanimity) or insight, and its main method. Next they see a map of 25 schools.

Acknowledge their guesses in one or two sentences, using their words. If a guess mixes up kinds of happiness (for example calls equanimity a pleasant feeling, or eudaimonia a feeling), say so in one sentence, because module 2 covered it. Otherwise do not say whether the guesses are right. Tell them to check their guesses against the map in the next lens. 40 to 80 words. One turn. No generic praise.
