#### Text
content::
Field-building advice from a few years ago said "make more junior researchers." The 2026 evidence says that's now the wrong target. Drawing on interviews with hiring managers and funders across the ecosystem, the MATS team argues the **binding constraint has shifted from junior-talent supply to absorptive and mentorship capacity** — the field can mint juniors faster than organizations can mentor and employ them.

As you read, track what this implies for where field-building effort should go next (senior hires, management, mentorship, absorptive orgs — not just more intro courses).
