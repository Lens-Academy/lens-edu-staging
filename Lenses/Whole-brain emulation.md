#### Text
content::
A different strategy accepts that we may not fully control a de novo AI, and instead tries to build advanced digital minds **from humans**. Whole-brain emulation (WBE), or "mind uploading," aims to scan, map, and simulate a specific brain at high enough resolution to reproduce its function — the hope being that a human-derived mind is more likely to share human values than an AGI built from scratch.

#### Text
content::
The catch is timing. If brain-emulation research mostly teaches us how to reverse-engineer neural algorithms, it may deliver **neuromorphic AGI before it delivers WBE** — accelerating exactly the uncontrolled-AI risk it was meant to sidestep. (Workshop estimates put ~85% on brain-inspired AI arriving first.) So WBE's value as a safety strategy hinges on a differential-progress question, not just on whether uploading is technically possible.
