\## The Effective Thesis Accelerator

\## A Warm Welcome! 💛

Welcome to the Effective Thesis Accelerator! 🎉 We’re so excited to have you here and are grateful that you’ve chosen to spend these 8 weeks with us.

Over the next 8 weeks, we’ll be working with you to turn your thesis or research project into more than just an academic requirement - but a meaningful step towards real-world impact and the kind of career & life you want to build!

We’ll explore questions like: What problems are most worth working on? Where can my research actually make a difference? What does this mean for my own career? And how can I use the thesis I’m already doing to start experimenting with this!

Our hope is that you leave the Accelerator not only with a stronger thesis, but with more clarity, confidence, skills, relationships, and agency to keep moving towards impact long after these 8 weeks are over.

**By the end of the program, we hope that you will leave with:**

- **Higher agency** towards defining your own vision for impact, and **building skills** towards executing on it.
- **A high-impact research project with a clear Theory of Change (ToC),** which is aligned with your career goals
- **Understanding** of how to conduct high-impact research (i.e., ITN, ToC)
- Engagement with relevant real-world stakeholders for your research

Most importantly, we hope these 8 weeks give you a space to **experiment, ask questions, learn from each other, and make meaningful progress on something that matters to you.**

We're really looking forward to learning alongside you. 💛

\# 📌 Key Information ^key-info

Here's everything you need to know about how the Accelerator runs!

\## 📅 Program Structure

Each week, you'll work through the week's reading and worksheet on your own, then come together with your cohort for a weekly discussion.

\### Weekly 1.5h Cohort Discussions (Required)

These weekly sessions are the central component of the Accelerator experience. During these sessions, you’ll engage deeply with the weekly readings and worksheets, and participate in structured peer discussion, feedback, and mutual support.

*Please confirm your weekly discussion time directly with your assigned facilitator.*

\### 💡 Mastermind Sessions

The Mastermind is the core format of our weekly peer-group discussions. From Weeks 2–7, two people each week will be “in the Mastermind.” When it’s your turn, the group focuses on you and supports you with something real that matters to you - related to the worksheet of the week in relation to your thesis, impact direction, or a decision you’re stuck on.

- **What you can bring in:** The goal of each Mastermind is for you to bring in something connected to the week’s worksheet, but you can also bring in anything that feels important to you in relation to your thesis, top uncertainties, or career direction!
- **How it works:** When you’re in the Mastermind, you get focused time where you first share your situation or question. The group asks clarifying questions, they reflect, offer perspectives, and suggest options. Lastly, you can decide and reflect back what’s useful and what you would like to take forward.
- **Why we do this:** Through the Mastermind, we are able to create a space where we are each heard and where we are able to solve problems and get “unstuck” much faster than we can achieve alone!

---

\## 📚 Three Key Components of This Course

- **Reading:** Each week, you’ll find carefully selected readings that introduce key ideas, frameworks, and evidence to support your high-impact research and career planning journey. These readings form the foundation for our discussions - please complete them before your session and come ready to share your thoughts and reflections!
- **Worksheet:** The worksheets are your space to actively apply what you’re learning to your own thesis, interests, and career goals. They help you reflect, analyze, and make tangible progress. We encourage you to work on them before your discussion so you can get the most out of your peer group conversations.
- **Journaling (Optional):** Journaling prompts offer a personal space to explore your thoughts, values, motivations, and challenges. This is a chance to slow down, reflect, and deepen your self-awareness, helping you integrate what you’re learning week by week into your own life.

\## 🗓️ Overview of the 8-Weeks ^overview

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations to Impact — clarify your values and motivations, explore your Ikigai, and get a first look at the landscape of impactful career paths |
| 2 | Selecting a High-Impact Problem — tour the landscape of high-impact problem areas, then use the ITN framework to narrow in on one worth pursuing |
| 3 | Generating & Prioritising Research Questions — map your problem area, diagnose its key bottleneck, generate a wide set of candidate questions, and narrow to your top 1-2 with a Weighted Factor Model |
| 4 | Stakeholder Mapping and Outreach — figure out who actually needs your research question answered, and start reaching out to them (plus working through the emotional side of outreach) |
| 5 | Developing Your Theory of Change — synthesise everything so far into a clear ToC linking your research to real-world impact |
| 6 | Reverse Engineering Your Thesis from Your ToC — work backwards from your ToC to define a concrete thesis output and the research question that would produce it |
| 7 | Unblocking Your Impactful Career — use the Head, Heart, and Hand framework to diagnose what's actually blocking your career direction, and take real next steps on it |
| 8 | Final Presentations — present your 8 weeks of work to your peers, give and receive feedback, celebrate progress, and set next steps beyond the program |

\### 🌟 Before We Begin ^before

We wanted to share that you don't need to arrive with everything figured out. You don't need to have the perfect research question, a perfectly defined career plan, or a clear picture of exactly where you want to end up. That's what these 8 weeks are for!

Come with your questions, especially the ones you don't know how to answer yet. Bring your half-formed ideas, the things you're excited about, the things you're unsure about, and the questions you think others might not have thought to ask yet. Share your ideas generously, listen deeply to others, and let yourself be surprised by what you learn from the people around you.

And throughout the program, we encourage you to keep coming back to something even bigger than your thesis: why do you want to make the world a better place? What drew you towards impact in the first place? What kind of contribution feels meaningful to you? Your thesis is one really exciting opportunity to explore those questions.

We’re so grateful that you’ve chosen to spend these 8 weeks thinking seriously about how you can contribute to a better world. The work you’re doing matters, and we hope this program helps you feel more capable of turning that motivation into action!

Our hope is that you leave with more than a stronger thesis - but also with skills, ways of thinking, relationships, and a greater sense of agency that you can carry with you throughout your career.

And remember: this is just the beginning. Your thesis is one step, these 8 weeks are one step, and the questions you explore here may continue to evolve for years to come!

We’re incredibly excited to learn alongside you, to see what you discover, and to see what you go on to build. Let’s get started! 🚀
