\## Emotional Barriers to Outreach

Take 10–15 minutes to pause, observe, and write - there are no right or wrong answers. Think of it as getting curious about the voice that shows up when you're about to hit send, rather than trying to silence it.

#### Question: Open
id:: 053f6cb0-cab4-466d-9387-34d0ea1a200e
content::
**Noticing the Voice of Self-Doubt**

When you imagine reaching out to a stakeholder or expert this week, what comes up? Is there a specific voice, thought, or feeling in your body? What does it actually say to you, word for word? Where do you notice it physically, tight chest, racing thoughts, an urge to close the laptop?

#### Question: Open
id:: 900031bd-0bb0-4a56-81a3-beeec5014fef
content::
**Exploring Its Purpose with Gratitude**

Oftentimes, voices we find challenging are trying to protect us from something important. If this voice is trying to protect you from something, what might that be, embarrassment, rejection, being "found out"? What is it so afraid would happen if it let its guard down? Even if its strategy doesn't feel helpful right now, what was it originally trying to do for you?

See if you can genuinely thank it for that, this part has likely been trying to keep you safe for a long time, even if outreach isn't really the danger it thinks it is.

**Connecting Reflection to Action**

What might this voice or part need from you in order to soften, even slightly? And what's one small way you could act alongside this feeling this week, rather than waiting for it to disappear first (i.e. sending an email while still a little nervous, asking a friend to look at your draft first, or simply reminding yourself why this work matters to you)?
