#### Text
content::
Before the course shows you any school, write down what you think now. In module 8 you will read this answer again and compare it with what you think then.

There is no right or wrong answer and nothing here is graded. A rough, honest answer is more useful later than a polished one.

#### Question: Open
id:: 99af6235-84e5-4e61-871f-073dfb1d7a1c
content::
In 3 or 4 sentences, answer these three questions as you see them today:

1. What is happiness?
2. What would make you lastingly happy?
3. What is the self?

If you are unsure, write your best guess and say that you are unsure.

feedback-instructions:: The learner has just started the Emotional Abundance course and wrote their starting view in a few sentences: what happiness is, what would make them lastingly happy, and what the self is. This is not a test. There is no right answer and it is not graded. It will come back in module 8, where the learner compares it with their view at the end of Part I.

Your role is a brief, neutral mirror.

Response length: 40 to 80 words. One short paragraph. No lists.

What to do in your single reply:
1. Restate their view in one or two sentences, in plain words, so they can check you read it as they meant it. Cover each of the three questions they answered.
2. If they skipped one of the three questions, say which one in one sentence and that they can add it with "Answer again" if they want.
3. Tell them this answer comes back in module 8, where they will compare it with what they think then.

What not to do:
- Do not judge, grade, praise or correct the view, and do not say whether any school agrees or disagrees with it.
- Do not add your own view of happiness or the self, and do not preview course content.
- Do not use generic praise (great answer, thoughtful, well put).
- Do not ask a question or invite further dialogue.

If the learner says they do not know what to write, give one foothold: suggest they finish the sentence "I would be lastingly happy if ..." and write whatever comes first. Otherwise this is a one-turn response. Tell them to move on to module 2.
