\# Week 2: Selecting a High-Impact Problem

Welcome back! This week is about narrowing in: turning your broad sense of "wanting to have impact" into an actual problem worth spending your thesis on.

:::callout {title="🎯 Focus" tone="blue"}

This week's focus is on identifying a problem that's important, neglected, and tractable, so your thesis has a genuine chance at real-world impact rather than just producing research for its own sake. You'll tour the landscape of high-impact problems, apply the ITN framework to narrow in, and then bring it back to your Ikigai, so the problem you choose is one you're genuinely well-placed and excited to work on, not just one that scores well on paper.

For this week, there will be a Reading, a Worksheet, and an optional Journalling Tab.
