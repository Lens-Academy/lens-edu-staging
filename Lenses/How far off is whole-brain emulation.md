#### Text
content::
Whole-brain emulation is the most ambitious BCI-type bet: scan and simulate a brain to get a **human-derived digital mind that inherits human values by construction**, runnable fast and copyable to form alignment-research teams. But how close is it?

This 2025 report is the sober reality check against the hopeful 2008 Sandberg-Bostrom roadmap: no whole-brain single-neuron recording exists in *any* organism, the field has fewer than ~500 researchers, and human WBE remains generations away. Read it to calibrate how much weight the hard end of this plan can actually bear — and recall the extra worry that WBE research may accelerate unsafe *neuromorphic* AI before it delivers safe uploads.
