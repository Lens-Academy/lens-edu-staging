This extended discussion traces "wantlike" behavior from thermostats through wasps to beavers to humans, showing how increasingly sophisticated prediction and steering naturally produce goal-directed behavior. It also warns against two thinking errors: anthropomorphism (assuming AI will be like humans) and mechanomorphism (assuming AI will be like stereotypical rigid machines).
