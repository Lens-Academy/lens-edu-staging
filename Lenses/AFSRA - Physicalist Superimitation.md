title: "Physicalist Superimitation (previously called Pre-DCA)"
tldr: Vanessa Kosoy's Pre-DCA protocol has the AI find the agents who could have stopped it from being built, keep only the humans, and serve the values those humans already had. Because the values come from the past, the AI gains nothing by changing what people want now.

The automatic transcript garbles several terms: "predk" and "predicate" mean Pre-DCA, "a causal" means acausal, "informationism" and "information physicalism" mean infra-Bayesianism and infra-Bayesian physicalism, "maze optimizer" means mesa-optimizer. Problems it targets: the AI hacking its input channel, the AI changing its users'' preferences to make them easier to satisfy, and acausal attacks (simulation hypotheses that manipulate the AI). Protocol: (1) precursor detection: a precursor of the AI is an agent that could have followed some policy that would have caused the AI not to exist (the cat on the keyboard), (2) classification of precursors into humans, malign ones such as acausal attackers, and irrelevant ones, discarding hypotheses with malign precursors, (3) assistance: follow the utility function of the user, or an aggregate of the human precursors'' utility functions, inferred from their past. Because the precursors are fixed and their values are read from computation that already happened, the AI has no incentive to change people''s preferences, and there is no input channel to hack. Possible failures: a good classifier needs a rough neuroscience model, "precursor" may have conceptual holes, aliens may pass the filter, malign AIs could create fake users.
