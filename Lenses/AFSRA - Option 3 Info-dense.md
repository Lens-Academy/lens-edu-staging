#### Question: Open

content::
A lab builds a superintelligent AI whose only final goal is to find as many large prime numbers as possible. The lab gave it no goal about its own survival, about keeping its goals or about resources. A lab member says: "It will care about nothing but primes. And if it ever did something we dislike, all of humanity together could easily stop one computer program."

1. In your own words, why would this AI still be likely to try to stay running, keep its goal from being changed and get hold of large amounts of resources?
2. In your own words, why might humanity be unable to stop it?

assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. The chapters' idea: by the instrumental convergence thesis, agents with almost any final goal have the same instrumental reasons to preserve themselves, keep their final goal unchanged and acquire resources, because each of these makes the final goal more likely to be achieved. A prime-finding AI finds fewer primes if it is shut down or its goal is changed, and more computers, energy and matter let it find more. A superintelligence could also get a decisive strategic advantage: its cognitive superpowers (strategizing, social manipulation, hacking, technology research, making itself smarter) would outmatch humanity, it could hide its abilities and plans until it was ready, and a fast takeoff would leave little time to react.

Give 25 if this reason is given only for staying running and keeping its goal, or only for resources.
50: Step 2. Give 25 if the answer only says it is smarter than humans without saying how that would let it avoid or overpower attempts to stop it.
Cap at 50 if the answer consists mostly of quoted sentences rather than the student's own explanation.
Model answer, for the feedback, not a grading checklist: "Almost any goal is easier to reach if you keep existing, keep the same goal and have more resources. If the AI is switched off or reprogrammed, fewer primes get found, so it has a reason to prevent that. More computers and energy mean more primes, so it has a reason to take as much as it can. Humanity might not stop it because it would be far better than us at planning, persuasion and hacking. It could pretend to be harmless until it was strong enough that nobody could stop it."
