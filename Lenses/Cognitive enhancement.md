#### Text
content::
If the core problem is humans being outpaced, a direct answer is to **make humans smarter** — via pharmacology, brain-computer prosthetics, or genetics. GeneSmith & kman's piece is the contemporary version of the germline/embryo-selection route ("superbabies").

Two things to weigh as you read. First, this thread **survives the merge-circularity objection**: raising human capability doesn't require fusing with an unaligned AI. Second, its two biggest problems — **timelines** (enhanced humans may arrive a generation too late to matter for near-term AGI) and **inequality/lock-in** (cognitive enhancement as a privilege of the powerful, which cuts against safe, multipolar outcomes).
