summary_for_tutor: "Ryan Greenblatt examines whether AI control techniques, normally aimed at around-human-level systems, can still reduce risk when applied to significantly superhuman, likely-misaligned AI. He argues such control offers no certainty and mainly aims to prevent the worst outcomes (e.g. loss of control) rather than reliably eliciting useful work, which is much harder for superhuman schemers. Using rough estimates of capability growth (~1.5 SD per year) and the point where control becomes hopeless (~3-7 SD above top humans), he estimates control might buy only months to a few years during an intelligence explosion, and concludes it is a minor but worth-keeping-in-mind application rather than the main source of risk reduction from control."
