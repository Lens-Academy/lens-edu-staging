\## 1. Introduction to the Program

The Effective Thesis Accelerator is designed to guide and support you through the process of developing a thesis that is not just “good enough for graduation,” but something that can genuinely have a meaningful impact - for your personal life, your community, and the wider world. At the end of our 8 weeks, we hope that you will leave with:

- **Thesis:** A well-developed, high-impact thesis proposal with a clear **Theory of Change (ToC),** ideally with expert collaboration, connections, or interviews.
- **Career:** A clear high-impact career plan that aligns with your thesis.
- **Self:** Greater motivation, agency, and self-awareness towards your vision for impact.

\### Introduction to Theory of Change

One key outcome of this program is that you will leave with a clear Theory of Change (ToC) for your research - a roadmap showing how your work can create real-world impact. Before drafting your ToC, we’ll first focus on identifying a high-impact problem (Week 2) and then mapping key bottlenecks and top stakeholders (Week 4). This ensures your ToC is grounded in reality: you’ll understand what needs to change to make progress and who needs to act differently, allowing you to shape your thesis to influence the right people.

A Theory of Change is a step-by-step plan that links your research outputs to the outcomes and impact you hope to achieve. Developing a ToC helps you move beyond the assumption that “impact happens automatically” by making you think strategically about how your work can actually create change.
