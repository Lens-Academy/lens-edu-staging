#### Text
content::
Might the AIs themselves matter morally? The field went from fringe to Anthropic hiring for it in about two years.

**The stakes**
- [The stakes of AI moral status](https://joecarlsmith.com/2025/05/21/the-stakes-of-ai-moral-status) — Carlsmith on why getting this wrong could be catastrophic in either direction.

**Foundations (mostly Eleos AI)**
- [Insights from the Science of Consciousness](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08708) — what consciousness science can actually say about AI.
- [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.00986) — the paper that made the case respectable.
- [Key concepts and current beliefs about AI moral patienthood](https://eleosai.org/post/key-concepts-and-current-beliefs-about-ai-moral-patienthood)
- [Key strategic considerations for taking action on AI welfare](https://eleosai.org/post/working-paper-key-strategic-considerations-for-taking-action-on-ai-welfare/)
- [Research priorities for AI welfare](https://eleosai.org/post/research-priorities-for-ai-welfare)
- [Project Ideas: Sentience and Rights of Digital Minds](https://www.forethought.org/research/project-ideas-sentience-and-rights-of-digital-minds#develop--advocate-for-lab-policies-ml-governance-advocacy-writing-philosophicalconceptual)

**Empirical work on introspection and self-reports**
- [Why model self-reports are insufficient — and why we studied them anyway](https://eleosai.org/post/claude-4-interview-notes/)
- [Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection/index.html) — interpretability meets introspection.
- [Looking Inward: Language Models Can Learn About Themselves by Introspection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13787)
- [Could AI models be conscious?](https://youtu.be/pyXouxa0WnY) — Kyle Fish (Anthropic) interview.
- Model welfare sections of various Anthropic system cards — primary-source snapshots of how a lab operationalizes this.
