summary_for_tutor: "Ryan Greenblatt's framework organizing misalignment-risk strategies by the level of political will available, from most to least: Plan A (strong international agreement eliminating race dynamics, ~10 years to work, focus on moonshots), Plan B (US government buys 1-3 years of lead, prosaic methods), Plan C (leading company spends a 2-9 month lead on a rushed handoff with janky control), and Plan D (company mostly unconcerned; ~10 committed insiders with ~3% compute focus on extracting non-sabotaged safety research and evidence of risk). Also notes a Plan E where essentially no one is positioned to act. Gives rough probability estimates for each scenario and the corresponding expected takeover risk. Recurring theme: quickly handing off safety work to barely-superhuman, hopefully-aligned AIs."
