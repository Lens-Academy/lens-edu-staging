\# Week 8 Peer Group Discussion: Final Presentations

This section is **optional, but strongly recommended!** If you've formed a peer group (3-5 people works best), use this guide to run a 1h session together, no facilitator needed. Not in a peer group yet? You can find one through our WhatsApp community (see the Welcome page), or use these questions to reflect on your own, with peers in your course, or with your thesis supervisor.

**Roles:** rotate a **host** (opens the session and keeps the flow) and a **timekeeper** each week.

:::callout {title="🗓️ Week 8 session plan (1h)" tone="blue"}

**5m · Check-in:** What are you proud of yourself for over the last 8 weeks?

**40m · Presentations:** Each person presents for about 5 minutes using the Week 8 presentation template, followed by about 4 minutes of reflections. Each listener offers:

- One thing to celebrate
- One question to take forward
- One resource, person or organisation that could help

With a larger group, shorten the times or split into two smaller groups.

**15m · Close:**

- What's your biggest insight or "aha" moment from seeing everyone's projects?
- What's one concrete action you'll take in the next month to move your thesis or project forward?
- How can you keep supporting each other after the course ends?

Presentations don't need to be polished or finished! Share where you're genuinely at: the insights you're excited about, what didn't work, and the questions still alive for you.

:::
