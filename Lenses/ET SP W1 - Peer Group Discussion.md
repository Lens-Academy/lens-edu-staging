\# Week 1 Peer Group Discussion: Foundations to Impact

This section is **optional, but strongly recommended!** If you've formed a peer group (3-5 people works best), use this guide to run a 1h session together, no facilitator needed. Not in a peer group yet? You can find one through our WhatsApp community (see the Welcome page), or use these questions to reflect on your own, with peers in your course, or with your thesis supervisor.

**Roles:** rotate a **host** (opens the session and keeps the flow) and a **timekeeper** each week.

:::callout {title="🗓️ Week 1 session plan (1h)" tone="blue"}

**20m · Introductions:** Go around and share your name, what you're studying, and what drew you to doing an impactful thesis.

**30m · Group discussion:** Pick 2-3 of the questions below that feel most alive for your group.

**10m · Set up your group:**

- Agree a few group norms (e.g. confidentiality, everyone gets airtime, it's okay to be unsure!)
- Pick a regular weekly time
- Decide who hosts and keeps time next week
- Set up a shared doc for commitments

:::

\## 💬 Week 1 discussion questions

- What did you learn about yourself through this week's worksheet? This could be what energises you, what drains you, what you're good at, or what you care about more than you realised.
- What dimensions of impact might be missing, neglected, or under-considered in how you usually think about your thesis?
- Which career path was new or surprising to you? For your top career hypothesis, what's your biggest uncertainty?
- If you think of your thesis as an Ikigai experiment, what might it help you explore or test?

:::callout {title="💡 Tip" tone="amber"}

There's no right answer this week. The goal is reflection and getting to know each other!
