\## Lee and colleagues at Microsoft Research (2025)
Lee and colleagues at Microsoft Research (2025) surveyed 319 knowledge workers across 936 real examples of generative-AI use. The more confident a worker was in the AI, the less critical thinking they reported using; the more confident they were in their own ability, the more they reported. The study also found AI shifted work toward verification, integration, and stewardship, and away from initial production. Read the study's own account below.
