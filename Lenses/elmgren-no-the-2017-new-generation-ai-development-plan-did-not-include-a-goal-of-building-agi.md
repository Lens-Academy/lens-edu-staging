summary_for_tutor: "Argues that the widely repeated claim that China's 2017 New Generation AI Development Plan (NGAIDP) set a goal of building AGI rests on a misreading. The claim hangs on one appearance of the characters tongyong rengongzhineng ('general-purpose AI') within the phrase 'data-driven general-purpose AI mathematical models and theory.' The article makes four points: (1) the phrase refers to scientific theory, such as architectures or scaling laws, not an engineered AGI system; (2) tongyong rengongzhineng usually means general-purpose AI rather than AGI and is often conflated with LLMs; (3) Chinese grammar and style suggest 'general-purpose' ending up next to 'AI' may be near-coincidental rather than a deliberate term of art; and (4) national and provincial follow-up implementation documents never repeat the term. It stresses that the phrase sits deep in a 'Basic Theory' box among dozens of minor goals, not in the plan's 'Strategic Objectives,' and concludes that any Chinese AGI ambition does not date to 2017."
