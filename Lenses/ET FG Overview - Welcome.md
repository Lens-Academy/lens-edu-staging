\## Effective Thesis Accelerator: Campus Director HQ

We are so excited to have you join us as a Campus Director for the Effective Thesis 8-Week Accelerator! 🎉 Thank you for being a part of our mission to empower more incredible students in their journey towards impact. From mid Oct to mid Dec 2026, you’ll be guiding a cohort of motivated students as they develop high-impact theses as a way to build towards their high-impact career path.

\### Overview of the Program

The Effective Thesis Accelerator is designed to help students utilise their thesis/research as a meaningful step toward real-world impact and their future high-impact career path.

As a campus director or facilitator, your role is not to prescribe answers, but to create and hold a space in which students can explore their curiosities, build confidence in their skills, and develop the agency to tackle complex problems in ways that are meaningful to them.

By the end of the program, participants should be able to:

- Develop a clear Theory of Change (ToC) for their research, articulating how their thesis could contribute to real-world outcomes.
- Ideally, engage relevant real-world stakeholders in their research, helping move their work out of abstraction.
- Use their thesis as a stepping stone toward a high-impact career path.

\### Overview of the 8-Weeks

| Week | Focus | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ikigai & Foundations to Impact | Use the Ikigai framework to think big-picture about the impact you want to have, reverse-engineer your Theory of Change from that, and explore a menu of impactful career paths worth exploring. |
| 2 | Landscape of High-Impact Problems | Tour the landscape of cause areas using ITN (importance, neglectedness, tractability) as a lens, then apply it more rigorously to land on one specific problem worth researching. |
| 3 | Generating & Prioritizing Research Questions | Learn how to ideate and prioritize research questions - using our research question database, AI tools, research agendas, and a quick prioritization exercise - to land on your top candidate question. |
| 4 | Stakeholder Mapping and Outreach | Identify where progress is currently blocked on your chosen problem and the relevant stakeholders necessary for making progress. Reach out to the top stakeholders and learn how to conduct high-quality stakeholder interviews. |
| 5 | Refining Your Theory of Change | Build out your Theory of Change, mapping realistic pathways from your research to impact and testing the assumptions behind them. Also, continuing stakeholder outreach! |
| 6 | Reverse Engineering Your Project | Turn your impact goals into concrete research questions, methods, and a feasible project plan. |
| 7 | High-Impact Career Planning | Connect your thesis to longer-term high-impact career paths, and identify concrete next steps and opportunities beyond graduation. |
| 8 | Presentations & Reflections | Presenting your thesis/ research projects, celebrate your learning and growth, and consolidate your direction going forward. |
