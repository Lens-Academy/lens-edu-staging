\# Week 4 Peer Group Discussion: Stakeholder Mapping and Outreach

This section is **optional, but strongly recommended!** If you've formed a peer group (3-5 people works best), use this guide to run a 1h session together, no facilitator needed. Not in a peer group yet? You can find one through our WhatsApp community (see the Welcome page), or use these questions to reflect on your own, with peers in your course, or with your thesis supervisor.

**Roles:** rotate a **host** (opens the session and keeps the flow) and a **timekeeper** each week.

:::callout {title="🗓️ Session plan (1h)" tone="blue"}

**5m · Check-in:** One word or one sentence on how you're arriving this week.

**30m · Group discussion:** Pick 2-3 of the questions below that feel most alive for your group.

**20m · Peer accountability:** About 4 minutes each:

- **What I committed to last week**
- **What actually happened**
- **My one next step for this week**
- **One thing I'd love help with**

One person notes everyone's commitments in a shared doc so you can check in on them next week.

**5m · Close:** Each person says their one commitment for next week out loud, and confirm your next session time.

:::

\## 💬 Week 4 discussion questions

- Who are the people or organisations that could most influence progress on your problem?
- When you imagine hitting send on an outreach email, what does that inner voice say? If it's trying to protect you from something, what might it be?
- How can this group support you in reaching out to stakeholders this week?

:::callout {title="💡 Tip" tone="amber"}

Celebrate every email sent, not just replies! And go gently with the inner-voice question: invite, don't push. Most people feel this.

:::
