Hi, I'm Elias. I lead course creation for the Context Accelerator at Lens.

If you are reading this, you probably want to contribute to making AI safe. Maybe you already do.

What we noticed: the people who are truly impactful have deep strategic context and understanding of the field. Many people think this only grows passively over years. We don't believe that is the only way.

Small gaps are mostly fine. Sometimes it is embarrassing in a conversation when you don't know a basic concept. But gaps are a real problem when you have to make decisions, for example about your career, or when you want to get hired.

\## What the course is for

This course aims to be the fastest way for you to build strategic context in AI safety. Strategic context means:

- who does what, and why
- the agendas, the arguments and the history
- the people
- a deep understanding of the problem
- the main ideas
- knowing which projects are likely valuable, and how to pick what to work on yourself

\## Who it is for

It is built for people who only recently started in AI safety, for example after a BlueDot Impact course. It is also useful if you have been in the field for a while, for example if you finished MATS (a research programme for new AI safety researchers), but still feel you have gaps.

\## How we made it

We asked people who have been in the field for a long time what they think everyone in AI safety should know. We turned their answers into a list of skills. You can test yourself on each skill, find your gaps and fill them.

\## What you will do

- Work on the skills you don't have yet
- A 2-hour group meeting at the end of each unit
- Optional activities, for example:
    - Meet people in 1-on-1 conversations, like the ones at EA Global (EAG), the main conference of the effective altruism community
    - Lightning talks
    - Mastermind groups
- Take a test that shows your skills

\## This is a beta

The course is a beta. Expect some things to change, and please tell us what does not work for you.
