#### Text
content::
At the opposite, **soft** end from merge-and-upload is **cyborgism**: no implants, no uploading — just software-only, human-in-the-loop tooling that amplifies human agency. janus & Nicholas Kees' founding statement frames LLMs as *simulators-as-tools* that extend human cognition while keeping a person firmly steering, rather than delegating to autonomous agents.

Its appeal is that it's **live and low-regret**: commercializable and testable today, it tightens alignment-research feedback loops and preserves human agency. But hold the obvious objection: isn't this just "AI for AI safety" with a neurotech label? What, if anything, does the *brain*/neural-data angle add beyond ordinary tool use?
