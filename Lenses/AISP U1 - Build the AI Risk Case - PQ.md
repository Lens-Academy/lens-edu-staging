\## Before reading

Imagine AI systems that can perform most cognitive work a highly skilled human can perform from a computer. Do not assume they are conscious, hate humans, or are omniscient.

What additional facts would have to be true before systems like these could create a civilisation-level threat? Spend three minutes building the strongest chain you can.
