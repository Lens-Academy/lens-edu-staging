Optional extras for governance/strategy week.

**Superintelligence strategy / deterrence**
- [Superintelligence Strategy (MAIM)](https://www.nationalsecurity.ai/) — Hendrycks, Schmidt & Wang's "Mutual Assured AI Malfunction": deterrence as the alternative to a Manhattan Project.
- [AI Deterrence Is Our Best Option](https://ai-frontiers.org/articles/ai-deterrence-is-our-best-option) — MAIM's authors answer their critics.
- [Evaluating the Risks of Preventive Attack in the Race for Advanced AI](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PEA3691-13.html) — RAND takes the "would anyone actually strike a datacenter?" question seriously.

**Frontier lab safety policies (primary sources — skim one or two)**
- [Anthropic — Responsible Scaling Policy](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/17310f6d70ae5627f55313ed067afc1a762a4068.pdf)
- [OpenAI — Preparedness Framework v2](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/18a02b5d-6b67-4cec-ab64-68cdfbddebcd/preparedness-framework-v2.pdf)
- [Google DeepMind — Frontier Safety Framework v3.0](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/DeepMind.com/Blog/strengthening-our-frontier-safety-framework/frontier-safety-framework_3.pdf)
- [xAI — Risk Management Framework](https://data.x.ai/2025-08-20-xai-risk-management-framework.pdf)
- [Meta — Frontier AI Framework](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/meta-approach-frontier-ai/)

**Lab governance scrutiny**
- [Anthropic is Quietly Backpedalling on its Safety Commitments](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/HE2WXbftEebdBLR9u/anthropic-is-quietly-backpedalling-on-its-safety-commitments) — how binding are "commitments" when the race tightens?
- [Holden Karnofsky on 80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/holden-karnofsky-concrete-ai-safety-frontier-ai-companies/?t=2620) — "Can we trust Anthropic, or any AI company?" (from 00:43).

**US AI policy/regulation**
- [Should Governments or Markets control AI?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wkPsbwzyOa8) — Dean Ball x Daniel Kokotajlo "anti-debate"; the best single conversation across this divide.
- [Ensuring a National Policy Framework for AI](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/12/eliminating-state-law-obstruction-of-national-artificial-intelligence-policy/) — the executive order targeting state AI laws.
- [What is California's AI safety law?](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-is-californias-ai-safety-law/) — Brookings explains SB 53.
- [America's AI Action Plan](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Americas-AI-Action-Plan.pdf) — the administration's full strategy document.
- [IAPS compute policy explainers](https://www.iaps.ai/compute-policy-hub) — export controls and compute governance, explained piece by piece.

**China AI policy/regulation**
- [State of AI Safety in China (2025)](https://concordia-ai.com/research/state-of-ai-safety-in-china-2025/) — Concordia AI's comprehensive survey.
