content::
Whatever your view on advocacy, it helps to know where the movement has already stumbled. This piece synthesizes the views of ~17 named experts on the AI safety movement's **mistakes** — insularity and echo-chamber dynamics, neglecting policy and coalitions for too long, off-putting extremeness, over-concentration in a few orgs and funders, and communication missteps.

Read it as a diagnostic checklist: which mistakes are about *strategy*, which about *communication*, and which about *movement structure*?
