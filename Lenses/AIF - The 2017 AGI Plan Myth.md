#### Text

A case study in how AI-race narratives get built from mistranslation: no, China's 2017 New Generation AI Development Plan did not declare a goal of building AGI. Worth reading for the correction *and* for the epistemics lesson.

#### Chat
instructions::
TLDR of what the user just read: A close reading showing the widely-cited claim that China's 2017 New Generation AI Development Plan set an AGI goal is wrong. The plan targets AI industry leadership and applications by 2030, not artificial general intelligence; the myth spread via loose paraphrase and repetition in Western policy discourse.

Discussion topics to explore:
- How did this myth propagate, and what does that say about the evidence base of "China is racing to AGI" claims?
- What DOES the 2017 plan actually target, and does the correction change any policy conclusion the learner previously held?
- What other load-bearing race-narrative claims deserve the same source-checking treatment?

Ask the learner to name one belief about the AI race they hold on secondhand evidence and how they'd verify it.
