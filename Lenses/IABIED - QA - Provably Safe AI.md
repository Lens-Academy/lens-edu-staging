#### Text
content::
The authors are not demanding mathematical proofs of safety -- they say such proofs are probably impossible even in principle, citing the "rowhammer attack" as an example of how physical reality can bypass theoretical guarantees. Their argument is not about tail risks or abundance of caution: the car is careening toward a cliff, and the right response is to stop.
