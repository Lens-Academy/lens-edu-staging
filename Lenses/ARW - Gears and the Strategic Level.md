#### Question: Open
id:: a5929468-76cb-48d1-bb82-eac521f7ee30
content::
Take one mistake of yours from the past month, small or large.

1. Level 1: what will you do now about this situation?

2. Level 2: what way of thinking would have caught the problem before it happened?
feedback-instructions:: The learner read the CFAR Handbook's flash class on the strategic level (level 0: blaming yourself for a past decision, level 1: a tactical update, a change in what you plan to do, level 2: a strategic update, a change in how you generate plans) and applied levels 1 and 2 to a recent mistake. Ungraded. Reply in 2 to 3 sentences, one turn. Check only whether their level 2 answer is really a change in how they make plans (it would apply to other situations too) or another tactical fix for this one case. If it is tactical, ask what it would look like in a different situation. If it is strategic, say so in one sentence. If the answer reads as self-blame, say gently that the post calls that level 0. No praise.
