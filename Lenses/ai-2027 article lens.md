summary_for_tutor: "The full AI 2027 scenario by Daniel Kokotajlo, Eli Lifland, Thomas Larsen, Romeo Dean, and Scott Alexander. It is a concrete, quantitative, month-by-month forecast of how superhuman AI could develop over the next few years, informed by trend extrapolation, roughly 25 tabletop wargames, and feedback from over 100 experts. Starting from 'stumbling' AI agents in mid-2025, it traces escalating capabilities and US-China geopolitical dynamics and branches into two endings, a 'race' ending and a 'slowdown' ending, from roughly the same premises. The authors frame it as a predictive exercise rather than a recommendation and invite readers to debate and counter it."
