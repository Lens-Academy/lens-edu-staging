Ord situates effective altruism in the history of ideas (the scientific revolution, the Enlightenment, utilitarianism, and the application of evidence to medicine and global poverty) and recounts the founding of Giving What We Can, 80,000 Hours, and the Centre for Effective Altruism. MacAskill then reviews the movement's recent growth, projects its possible future, and introduces 'Cause X': a currently unrecognized major moral problem, framing EA as an open-ended intellectual project of figuring out how to do the most good."
