summary_for_tutor: "Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's essay confronting the risks of powerful AI, written as a companion to his optimistic 'Machines of Loving Grace.' He frames humanity as entering a turbulent 'technological adolescence' and argues that risk should be discussed carefully: avoid doomerism, acknowledge uncertainty, and intervene as surgically as possible. Defining 'powerful AI' as a 'country of geniuses in a datacenter' (smarter than Nobel laureates across most fields, autonomous, and runnable as millions of fast-thinking copies), he analyzes five categories of risk: (1) autonomy or misalignment risks, (2) misuse for destruction, (3) misuse for seizing power, (4) economic disruption, and (5) destabilizing indirect effects, together with his thoughts on how to address each. He believes the risks can be overcome if humanity acts decisively."++}
