\## Welcome

This is a demo of a group meeting held inside Lens. Everyone is in the main room now.

In a moment the navigator opens breakout rooms. You will move into a small room with a few other people. The page on the right then changes to that room's questions, and everyone in your room types into the same answer boxes together.

When the breakouts end, everyone comes back here, and you can see what each room wrote.
