\## Appendix: Email Outreach Templates

\### 1) Template for Expert Interview Request

**Subject: Expert Interview on \[Your Thesis Topic\]**

Dear \[Expert's Name\],

I hope this message finds you well. My name is \[Name\], and I am currently studying \[Degree Program\] at \[University/Institution\] and am a current mentee at Effective Thesis. I am deeply interested in \[specific field/topic of interest\], and I have come across your work on \[mention specific article, research, or project they’ve done\] that aligns with my thesis on \[topic\].

Given my thesis research on \[topic\], I would love to ask if you would be open to **a 30-minute expert interview in the next 2 weeks.** I would love to learn from your work on \[specific question or topic you'd like guidance on\]. Your expertise in \[specific area of expertise\] would be incredibly valuable as I continue to explore this area and refine my research.

If you have limited capacity, I would also appreciate the opportunity to ask you a few questions via email.

Thank you so much for considering my request. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,

\[Name\]

\[University/Institution Name\]

\### 2) Template for Collaboration without a specific idea

#### Callout: ⭐ Recommended:
tone:: amber

#### Text
content::
If there is an org/person that you’re interested in working with, we recommend several options:

- **⭐ Recommended:** (a) asking about their current research needs or priorities, with the goal of aligning your thesis to meet that need;
- (b) options to intern or volunteer for their organisation/research project for your thesis; or
- (c) if they are open to external thesis mentorship/supervision.

#### End Callout

#### Text
content::
**Subject: Research Collaboration Opportunity - \[Their Organization/Research Area\]**

Dear \[Expert's Name\],

I hope this message finds you well. My name is \[Name\], and I am currently studying \[Degree Program\] at \[University/Institution\] and am a current mentee at Effective Thesis. I am deeply interested in \[specific field/area\] and have been following your work at \[organization\] on \[mention specific project/initiative they're involved in\]. Your approach to \[specific aspect of their work\] particularly resonates with my commitment to \[relevant impact area/mission\].

I am currently working on my thesis in \[problem area\] and would love to explore opportunities to contribute directly to your organization's research priorities, rather than pursuing research in isolation. My goal is to ensure my academic work translates into real-world impact and addresses genuine needs in the field.

I would like to ask if you would be open to **a 30-minute expert interview in the next 2 weeks.** I would love to discuss your current work and potential research questions that would be genuinely valuable for the field and your organization. I'm particularly interested in tackling problems that might be challenging for your team to address due to constraints, but where more research could make meaningful progress.

If you have limited capacity, I would also appreciate the opportunity to ask you a few questions via email. I **have also attached my CV to this email.**

Thank you so much for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,

\[Name\]

\[University/Institution Name\]

\### 3) Template for Collaboration on a Specific Idea

#### Callout: 3) Template for Collaboration on a Specific Idea
tone:: amber

#### Text
content::
If you have a specific idea in mind to collaborate on: we recommend sharing your specific research idea and tailoring it as much as possible to their current research needs and priorities. If you can, reference their work to show that your ideas are grounded in a deep understanding of what they do and why.

#### End Callout

#### Text
content::
**Subject: Research Collaboration Opportunity - \[Their Organization/Research Area\]**

Dear \[Expert's Name\],

I hope this message finds you well. My name is \[Name\], and I am currently studying \[Degree Program\] at \[University/Institution\] and am a current mentee at Effective Thesis. I am deeply interested in \[specific field/area\] and have been following your work at \[their organization\] on \[mention specific project/initiative they're involved in\].

Your approach to \[specific aspect of their work\] particularly resonates with my commitment to \[relevant impact area/mission\] and has inspired a specific research project that I believe could directly advance your work.

I have developed a thesis proposal focused on \[specific research idea/question\] that builds on your research in \[specific area\]. Based on your work showing \[reference their findings\], I propose to investigate \[your specific research question/approach\]. This research would specifically address \[challenge/gap they've identified\] by \[your proposed method/solution\].

**As such, could I ask if you would be open to a 30-minute expert interview in the next 2 weeks?** I would love to discuss your current work and how I may better align my thesis with you or your organisation’s research priorities.

If you have limited capacity, I would also appreciate the opportunity to ask you a few questions via email. I have also attached my CV and research proposal to this email.

Thank you so much for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,

\[Name\]

\[University/Institution Name\]
