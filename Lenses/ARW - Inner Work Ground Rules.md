\## This week works on feelings

This week's techniques work on what goes on inside you: feelings in your body (Focusing), beliefs you flinch away from (Bucket Errors), stress (Againstness) and inner conflict (Internal Double Crux). That can bring up more than you expect. So, before you start:

- **You choose the material.** Each practice asks you to pick a bug from your list. Pick a small or medium one, at least the first time. "I keep putting off one email" works as well as anything deep. You never have to tell the tutor what a bug is about. A code word is enough, and the tutor will work with that.
- **You can stop at any point.** Stopping halfway, or skipping a practice, is a fine result. Tell the tutor and go on to the next part of the page.
- **This is not therapy.** The tutor is an AI that asks questions. It is not a therapist, and neither is this course.
- **Know where to stop.** Stop the exercise and do something calming if a practice starts to feel overwhelming, if old pain or trauma comes up, or if you are going through a hard time right now (grief, a crisis, strong anxiety or low mood). We think those belong with a friend, a doctor or a therapist, not with a course exercise.
- **In a crisis,** contact your local emergency services or a helpline. [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, confidential helplines by country.

CFAR says that its workshops, like meditation retreats, may trigger mania or psychosis in people who are prone to it, and it advises people with a history of mania, hypomania or psychosis not to come, or to talk to their psychiatrist first.[^cite-salamon-2025-update] If that applies to you, we suggest the same for this week: talk to your psychiatrist before doing the practices, or read the pages and skip the practices.

Each technique comes with the handbook's own caveats, and Internal Double Crux also with a warning from CFAR. You will meet them on the pages. As in every week, the techniques are scaffolding. If one does not fit you, adapt it or drop it.

[^cite-salamon-2025-update]: Anna Salamon (2025), *CFAR update, and New CFAR workshops*. [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/AZwgfgmW8QvnbEisc/cfar-update-and-new-cfar-workshops)
