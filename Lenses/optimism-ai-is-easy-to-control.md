summary_for_tutor: "An optimistic counterargument to AI takeover fears by Nora Belrose and Quintin Pope. It contends that neural networks are 'white boxes', with full read/write access to their internals, gradient descent, and fully controllable inputs, making them far more controllable than 'black box' human brains, and that control research is easier to advance thanks to reproducibility, low cost, scalability, and the absence of legal constraints. It further argues that human values are simple and pervasive in training data, so AIs learn ethics before dangerous capabilities and 'alignment generalizes further than capabilities', leading the authors to estimate catastrophic AI takeover at roughly 1 percent."
