#### Question
content:: Before you read: can humans lose control over important decisions without any AI ever rebelling, and without one dramatic takeover moment? If yes, how could it happen, step by step? If no, what stops it? Write your reasoning down before you read on.

assessment-instructions:: The student is about to read the gradual-disempowerment article by Jan Kulveit and colleagues (Unit 3 of "Thinking about Thinking"). They have just put their own step-by-step story of how humans could lose influence on the record.

Acknowledge their reasoning in 1 to 2 sentences, specifically, without judgment. Do NOT preview the article's six claims, the causal chain used later in the unit, or the historical analogy (advisors and regents). Do not correct their story; it is a setup, not a test.

Close with something like: "Hold that story as you read. Notice where the article agrees with it, where it goes further, and where you want to push back. Head to the reading when you are ready."
