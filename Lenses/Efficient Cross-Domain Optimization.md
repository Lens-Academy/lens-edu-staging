What do we actually mean by "intelligence"? Before reasoning about superintelligence, we need a precise definition — one that works for bees, beavers, humans, and hypothetical AIs alike. Yudkowsky's 2008 post proposes one: intelligence is *efficient cross-domain optimization*.

content::
Yudkowsky distinguishes narrowly optimizing systems (Deep Blue, bees, beavers) from cross-domain optimizers (humans, evolution). He then adds the crucial qualifier: efficiency — optimization power divided by resources used. With that definition in hand, consider: what would it mean for an AI to be a *more* efficient cross-domain optimizer than a human?
