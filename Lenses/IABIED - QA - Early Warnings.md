#### Text
content::
The authors use the OceanGate Titan disaster as a detailed analogy: OceanGate had sensors measuring hull integrity but lacked the theoretical understanding to interpret the data or act on it. Similarly, AI companies can measure outputs and monitor chain-of-thought, but without a mature theory of intelligence, they cannot distinguish real warning signs from noise -- and even if they could, the field is too immature to know what to do about them.
