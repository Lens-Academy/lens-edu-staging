summary_for_tutor: "Epoch AI's documentation for the Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI), a composite metric that combines scores from over 50 AI benchmarks into a single general-capability scale so models can be compared even across timespans long enough for individual benchmarks to saturate. It explains that ECI infers relative benchmark difficulty from cases where models are evaluated on multiple benchmarks, and that models score higher by performing well on harder benchmarks. It points to the methodology section, the companion paper 'A Rosetta Stone for AI Benchmarks' (arXiv 2512.00193), the public code repository, the interactive leaderboard, and domain-specific ECI scores for software engineering and math."
