summary_for_tutor: "Introduces superforecasting as a method for making reliable forecasts on questions that lack enough historical data for statistical models, such as geopolitical events. The method has three steps: measure forecasters' accuracy, identify consistent top performers (the top roughly 0.1% across multiple years), and aggregate their predictions. It cites the U.S. intelligence community's tournament over 500-plus questions showing these forecasts are well-calibrated (events given 20% happen about 20% of the time) and outperform typical forecasters. It notes the open question of long-range accuracy and that organizations can hire existing superforecasters or build the process internally."
