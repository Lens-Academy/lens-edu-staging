summary_for_tutor: "Sections 1 to 6 of nostalgebraist's essay 'the void'. A base model is trained to predict how real-world text continues. Even a perfect one never sees the author's mind, so its 'writing' is really inference about a hidden author (the made-up 'dclamont' forum comment example). Instruction tuning, and then the chat assistant format, were invented to make base models easier to use. The assistant first appeared in Anthropic's 2021 paper 'A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment', whose HHH prompt was a role-play of a future AI written with sci-fi robot tropes (the spaghetti and salt example). Post-training then teaches the model a character that appears nowhere in its data about reality, so it has little to go on about what the assistant wants or is. This follows on from the Janus' Simulators lens before it."
