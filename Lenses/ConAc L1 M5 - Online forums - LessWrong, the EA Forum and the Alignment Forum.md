AI safety research is distributed across academic publications, online forums, organisational blogs and social media. Different sources provide different kinds of information.

Online forums have played an unusually important role in the development of AI safety as a research field. Many concepts, threat models and research agendas were first formulated and discussed in forum posts. For some research directions, these discussions constitute a substantial part of the available literature.
