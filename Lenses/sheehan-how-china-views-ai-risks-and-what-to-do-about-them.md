summary_for_tutor: "A Carnegie Endowment analysis of China's AI Safety Governance Framework 2.0, a nonbinding standards document released in September 2025 by TC260 and CNCERT-CC under the Cyberspace Administration of China. It explains who produced the framework and what changed from version 1.0: deeper treatment of risks from open-source models, labor-market and broader social impacts, chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear (CBRN) weapon misuse, and loss of human control over advanced AI, plus a new risk categorization and grading system. It argues the document is aimed primarily at a domestic audience as a roadmap for future technical standards and regulation, while the party continues to weigh AI development more heavily than safety and keeps control of politically sensitive content as its core concern."
