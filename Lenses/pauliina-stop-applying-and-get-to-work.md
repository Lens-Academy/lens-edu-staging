summary_for_tutor: "A short, practical post arguing that for a specific subset of people (short AI timelines, struggling to land a role, able to self-motivate, enough of a financial safety net), the standard advice of applying to jobs and fellowships trades real impact for the appearance of competence. Recommends instead: identify the risk scenario and interventions you'd most want to help with, find who's already working on them, and reach out with concrete offers to help rather than requests for a role. A candid, first-person counterpoint to 'Apply for a program or fellowship' and 'Talk to people in the field' in the module's closing Next Steps lens: useful to place right after readings on AI's trajectory, since it's squarely about what a learner does with short timelines rather than what might happen to the world."
