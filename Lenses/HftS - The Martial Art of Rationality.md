#### Question: Open
id:: 03f62a28-e98d-4e95-99c7-383b49ead600
content::
A friend says: "I'm smart and I've read a lot about cognitive biases, so I'm already rational. Rationality training is for people who aren't as bright."

1. Using the essay, explain in your own words why being smart does not make training unnecessary.
2. Using the essay, explain in your own words why having read about biases is not the same as having the skill.
force-feedback:: first
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. The essay compares rationality to a martial art. You do not need big muscles to learn to make a fist, only a hand. Likewise rationality is about training brain machinery everyone has. A fast learner may learn faster, but that is not what the art is about. It is about fixing, or working around, the systematic errors that human brains tend to make. Minds respond to our will less readily than muscles, and we cannot rewire them, only compensate. Knowledge from heuristics and biases research, probability theory and psychology does not do this by itself: it has to be applied to your own intuitions and connected to practice in your daily mental life, because it is your own mental limbs you must move.
