#### Text
content::
Most schools on the last pages aim at some kind of calm toward feelings. The rest of this module is about how that calm can go wrong. First, your own guess.

#### Question: Open
id:: 4cd70e19-5d91-4fcd-93b0-aab27dd46bbd
content::
Two friends of yours hear that a close relative is seriously ill. Both stay calm. One is at peace with it, the other has shut it out. What would you watch for, over the following weeks, to tell which is which?
feedback-instructions:: This is a pre-reading question. Restate in one or two sentences the signs the learner would watch for. Do not say whether the signs are good, do not add your own, and do not mention any teacher, study or term (such as near enemy, avoidance or bypassing). If they gave no observable sign, only a feeling ("you can just tell"), ask once what they would actually see. Say that the next pages give the traditions' and researchers' own answers, and that they can compare. 40 to 80 words. One turn.
