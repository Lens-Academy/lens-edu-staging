#### Text
content::
The loudest version of BCI-type solutions is the **merge**: couple human brains to AI at high enough bandwidth that a human stays "in the loop at machine speed," or the AI becomes part of the person. Tim Urban's Neuralink piece is the fullest popular articulation — Musk's framing that, faced with superintelligence, humans should *merge* rather than end up as "house cats."

Read it as the **vision statement**, not a safety proof. As you go, hold onto the sharpest objection (developed later in this module): merging may not *solve* alignment so much as *presuppose* it — you need an already-trustworthy AI to safely fuse with, and once the AI outclasses the human, the human is the bottleneck, not the safeguard.
