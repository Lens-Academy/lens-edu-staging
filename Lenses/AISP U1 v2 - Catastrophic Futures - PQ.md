#### Question: Open
id:: 095c6c4d-5cf6-4025-8a74-03eca9bb22ae
content::
Imagine that advanced AI becomes extremely capable and human extinction never happens.

What outcomes would still make you say the transition went catastrophically wrong? List two or three. Then say what, if anything, makes extinction different from those failures.

feedback-instructions::
Respond once in 60 to 100 words. Reflect the dimensions the learner uses, such as welfare, autonomy, political control, irreversibility, or suffering. Do not introduce the course's categories yet. Do not grade.
