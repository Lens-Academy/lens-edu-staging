#### Text
content::
Most AI safety work is *object-level*: alignment research, evals, policy. **Field-building** (or "capacity-building") is the *meta* layer — growing the people, skills, and institutions that do all that object-level work. The case for it rests on a simple premise: **AI safety is talent- and capacity-constrained**, so anything that reliably increases the supply of skilled, well-supported people is a force-multiplier on every other plan.

Read Asya Bergal's funder-level argument for why this meta-work is worth serious investment — and note that "capacity" means more than junior researchers: it includes founders, managers, mentors, and the organizations that can absorb new talent.
