\## Before reading

Two groups study the same unprecedented global risk. Group A consists of domain experts. Group B consists of forecasters selected partly for a strong prediction track record. After months of structured discussion, Group A assigns a probability several times larger than Group B.

You must make a decision today. What should the disagreement itself make you do differently? Spend two minutes on your answer.

Do not reveal the Existential Risk Persuasion Tournament results and do not recommend mechanically splitting the difference. Respond once in 50 to 90 words. Reflect the learner's proposed response and send them to the reading. Do not grade.
