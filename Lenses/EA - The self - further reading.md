#### Text
content::
None of these is required. Each line says what you get from it.

- **Thomas Metzinger, [*The Elephant and the Blind*](https://thomasmetzinger.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Metzinger_MIT_Press_2024-1.pdf)** (MIT Press 2024, free). A book built around more than 500 reports by meditators of "pure awareness". Chapters 25 to 29 cover ego dissolution, nondual experience and the "true self". The leading no-self theorist treats reports of a true self as data.
- **Thomas Metzinger, ["The Ego Tunnel"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFjY1fAcESs)** (TEDx talk, 18 minutes). His view of the self-model with the rubber hand and out-of-body examples.
- **Anil Seth and Manos Tsakiris, ["Being a Beast Machine"](https://osf.io/6snfm_v1/)** (Trends in Cognitive Sciences 2018, accepted manuscript). The technical argument behind Seth's talk: body ownership and emotion as predictions about the body's inside.
- **Robert Wright and Evan Thompson, ["Why I Am Not a Buddhist"](https://www.nonzero.org/p/why-i-am-not-a-buddhist-robert-wright-5a4)** (2020, about 2 hours 20 minutes). The two disagree about whether science supports the Buddhist view of the self.
- **Evan Thompson, ["Clarifying Why I Am Not a Buddhist"](https://indianphilosophyblog.org/2020/04/25/clarifying-why-i-am-not-a-buddhist-a-response-to-amod-lele-guest-post-by-evan-thompson/)** (blog post). A shorter written statement of his position.
- **Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, ["Advaita Vedanta"](https://iep.utm.edu/advaita-vedanta/)**. The scholarly background to Ramana's teaching.
- **Wikipedia, ["Anatta"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatta)**. How different Buddhist schools read the not-self teaching.
- **Raphaël Millière and colleagues, ["Psychedelics, Meditation, and Self-Consciousness"](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01475/full)** (Frontiers in Psychology 2018). A scientific map of the different things "loss of self" can mean, and why brief states of self-loss should not be confused with selflessness as a trait.
- **Dahl, Lutz and Davidson, ["Reconstructing and deconstructing the self"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4595910/)**, which you met in module 4. It places self-enquiry in the "deconstructive" family of meditation.
- The library module on **fundamental wellbeing research** goes deeper into Jeffery Martin's work and its critics.
