title: "Deep Learning is Not So Mysterious or Different"
tldr: "Deep learning's strangest generalization behavior may be less alien than it looks. Flexible models can still generalize when training softly prefers simpler or more compressible solutions instead of forbidding complex ones outright."
summary_for_tutor: "Andrew Gordon Wilson argues that phenomena often treated as unique mysteries of deep learning, including benign overfitting, double descent, and overparameterization, also appear in simpler models and fit established generalization frameworks. The paper develops soft inductive biases as preferences among hypotheses rather than hard restrictions on the hypothesis space."
