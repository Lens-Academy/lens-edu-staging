\## Unblocking your Impactful Career

Career uncertainties usually show up as one vague problem: *"I don't know what I should do."* But that sentence can be hiding three different problems tangled together, and each one needs a different response to actually resolve. This week is about discovering what you're stuck on, and taking small actions on the thing that will actually make progress on it!

\## 1. The Head, Heart and Hand Framework for Impactful Careers

Creating your impactful career isn't simply a matter of choosing the "right" cause area on paper. It requires a balance of three genuinely different categories of questions - which can fall into the dimensions of your Head, your Heart, and your Hand.

![1. The Head, Heart and Hand Framework for Impactful Careers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lens-Academy/lens-edu-staging/staging/attachments/et-w7-reading-01.png)

**🧠 Your Head** asks: will working on this problem actually lead to real-world impact? These uncertainties are resolved through research and analysis. Questions here may look like:

- Is this problem important, neglected, and tractable?
- Do I know enough about the real-world state of this problem?
- Does this approach target the right bottleneck?

**❤️ Your Heart** asks: does this path motivate, energise, and sustain me? These uncertainties are resolved through honest self-reflection. Questions here may look like:

- Do I enjoy this day-to-day, or just the idea of it?
- Does this align with who I want to become?
- What belief about myself might be limiting my options?

**🤝 Your Hand** asks: have I actually tested this in reality? These uncertainties can only be resolved through action and experimentation. Ask yourself:

- Who is doing this work that I could talk to?
- What's the smallest version of this I can try?
- What would a real mini-experiment of this look like?

\#### Why all three dimensions matter

Having just one or two out of three can often be a key reason why people are stuck in their impactful journeys:

- **Head ∩ Heart, no Hand** - you know what matters and feel genuinely aligned, but you haven't tested any of it in reality yet. You do a ton of thinking and planning, but nothing actually happens out in the world. The risk here is staying theoretical indefinitely, mistaking a good plan for real progress.
- **Head ∩ Hand, no Heart** - you're doing rigorous, genuinely impactful work, but it may be quietly draining you. The risk here is burnout that looks like success from the outside, but isn't sustainable in the long run.
- **Heart ∩ Hand, no Head** - you're motivated and active, but not actually sure any of it is impactful. The risk here is staying busy without it actually making an important difference, and many well-intentioned people fall into exactly this trap.
- **All three together** - you're doing impactful work with tangible impact, and energised by both *what* you're doing and *how* you're doing it!

A key thing to remember is that your impactful career isn’t a fixed destination - it’s something you can continue to shape, refine, and build throughout your journey. At different points, you may be blocked on different things!

\## 2. What is my actual blocker?

If you feel stuck on your career direction right now, it's worth asking: is this actually a Head problem, a Heart problem, or a Hand problem? From the inside, all three can feel identical, they all just show up as "I don't know what to do." But they benefit from completely different responses.

- A **Head** blocker may benefit from more research, reading, and analysis. Ask: *what do I not yet understand?*
- A **Heart** blocker responds to reflection, and sometimes simply to naming a feeling out loud rather than trying to solve it. Ask: *what do I need to reflect on? What emotion am I avoiding?*
- **A Hand** blocker responds to actually doing something - rather than purely thinking or feeling alone. This may be actions that can give you more information that you lack, and can’t gain from your desk.

\### Why finding the right blocker matters

It's easy to default to whichever response feels most comfortable or familiar to you (i.e. just reading more or doing more research), and mistake that for progress. A common trap: treating a Heart or Hand blocker as if it were a Head blocker, doing more research when what you actually need is a conversation, or an honest hour of reflection. If you've been "researching" the same decision for weeks without it resolving, it's worth asking honestly whether you're actually stuck somewhere else entirely.

Here are a few examples of what this can look like and why it matters:

- **Applying Head to a Hand blocker:** you've read three articles on what it's like to work in AI policy and feel like you understand the field reasonably well, but you've never actually spoken to someone doing that work or tried a small piece of it yourself. Reading about a job is not the same information as doing five hours of it, and the most beneficial next step here may be to take on a small project or speak to someone in the field!
- **Applying Heart to a Head blocker:** you journal for an hour about whether biosecurity "feels right" for you, but your actual open question is a factual one, whether biosecurity policy work is more talent-constrained than technical biosecurity research right now. This uncertainty would be best served by researching and asking people who'd know.
- **Applying Head to a Heart blocker:** you've read every 80,000 Hours career review on offer, built spreadsheets comparing cause areas, and you're still stuck, because the real issue isn't a lack of information but may be an emotional blocker, such as imposter syndrome or not feeling good enough. No amount of additional research or reading would be able to solve this, and this may be best explored by exploring your limiting beliefs, talking honestly with a close friend or therapist, or reflecting on what is really making the decision feel difficult.
