#### Text
content::
Everything so far treats BCIs as a *solution*. This reading flips it: brain-computer interfaces may be an existential-risk **factor**. The sharpest mechanism is **entrenched totalitarianism** — neural surveillance and coercion could enable durable, unshakeable control that locks in a bad future permanently — alongside attack-surface and "neuraljacking" risks from wiring brains to advanced AI.

Read it as the counterweight to the optimistic threads: a technology pitched as keeping humans in control could, in the wrong hands, do the opposite.
