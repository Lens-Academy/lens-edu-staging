#### Text
content::
You've reached the end of the course. The question now is whether this is a field where your own actions could matter.

There are many ways in. Some people do technical research. Others work on governance and policy, build evaluations, fund projects, run communities, or explain the problem to others. Education, translation and communications all count.

You don't need a career plan yet. If you're unsure, ask yourself what's missing: information, skills, confidence, people to work with? "I don't want to work on this" is a fine answer too, if you've thought it through.
***
Here's a menu of next steps: ways to learn more, meet people, test your fit and start contributing.

**Learn more**

Visit AISafety.info's [How can I help?](https://aisafety.info/how-can-i-help) guide, which walks through concrete next steps based on your situation and interests.

**Talk to people in the field**

[AISafety.com](https://aisafety.com/events-and-training) keeps an up-to-date directory of conferences, training programs and online events. EA Global conferences (EAGs) are especially good: you'll meet experts and plenty of newcomers in your position, with career fairs and structured one-on-ones to make it easy.


**Help spread understanding**

Share the link to this course or other safety-related materials with your friends, your university group, or on your channel.

**Apply for a program or fellowship**

If you want a structured way to test your fit for research, apply to a fellowship or bootcamp. Applications open and close through the year, so sign up to [AISafety.com's events and training newsletter](https://aisafety.com/training) to hear when they open.

**Engage politically or institutionally**

If you lean toward governance or policy, learn what the work looks like before diving in. [AISafety.info](https://aisafety.info/) has an overview of AI governance, and MIRI lists [actions you can take right now](https://ifanyonebuildsit.com/act). For grassroots advocacy, [PauseAI](https://pauseai.info/) runs public campaigns and local groups pushing for a global pause on frontier AI development.

**Donate**

If you can't give time yet, you can give money. [Manifund](https://manifund.org/) hosts AI safety regranting and a range of AI safety projects.
