#### Text
content::
The most tractable BCI-for-safety idea needs no merge and no uploading: **use neural signals as feedback for AI**. Language is a low-bandwidth, lossy channel for human values; brain signals — neural correlates of approval, confusion, or moral emotion — could give richer supervision data for RLHF, debate, imitative amplification, and scalable oversight. Robbo's piece makes this case and offers a useful Enhancement / Merge / Alignment-Aid taxonomy.

Note the key claim: even **coarse** signals (EEG, affect, skin response) might improve oversight for some value dimensions — you don't need superintelligent humans or full WBE for this thread to pay off.
