summary_for_tutor: "Appendix examining data as a target for AI governance. Assesses data against three governance criteria — measurability, controllability, and meaningfulness — concluding it is highly meaningful but hard to measure and control because it is non-rival, easily copied, and extractable via model distillation. Surveys the key data-governance concerns: training-data quality and safety, data poisoning and security, provenance and accountability, consent and rights frameworks, bias and representation, and access and sharing protocols. Explains why, despite its importance, data governance offers fewer concrete control points than compute governance, which the main text prioritizes."
