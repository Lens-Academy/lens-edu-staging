summary_for_tutor: "Presents the launch of the Lead Exposure Elimination Project (LEEP), a Charity Entrepreneurship-incubated charity advocating for lead paint regulation in high-burden, low- and middle-income countries. Applies the Importance-Tractability-Neglectedness framework: lead poisoning affects roughly 815 million children and accounts for about 1% of global disease burden and $1 trillion in yearly economic losses (importance); 61% of countries lack lead paint regulation and many high-burden countries are unaddressed (neglectedness); legislation is technically viable, low-cost, and faces virtually no industry opposition (tractability). Outlines LEEP's plan to pilot advocacy in Malawi, its cost-effectiveness comparable to GiveWell's top charities, and concrete ways to help."
