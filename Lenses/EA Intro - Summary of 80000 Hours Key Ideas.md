summary_for_tutor: "Condenses 80,000 Hours' framework for high-impact careers. It treats impact as a product of three factors: choosing a pressing problem (compared on scale, neglectedness, and tractability), finding solutions and roles with high leverage, and personal fit (since top performers vastly outproduce average ones). It then lays out an iterative career strategy across three overlapping stages: explore (roughly ages 18 to 24), invest in career capital (25 to 35), and deploy (36 and beyond), while emphasizing sustainable work, character, and community."
