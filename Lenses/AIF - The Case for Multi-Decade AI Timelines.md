TLDR of what the user just read: Ege Erdil argues for a ~20 year median timeline to full automation of remote work, much longer than the field's default. He extrapolates revenue trends (especially NVIDIA's) rather than vibes-based "intelligence" trends, doubts a software-only singularity because of experimental compute and data bottlenecks, and invokes Moravec's paradox to argue agentic and multimodal tasks will stay slow and expensive.

- Which of Erdil's three cruxes (trend choice, the software singularity, Moravec's paradox) does the learner find most and least persuasive?
- Is NVIDIA revenue a good proxy for progress toward remote-work automation, or does it bake in assumptions?
- How much does the disagreement with short-timelines people come down to evidence versus differing priors about bottlenecks?
