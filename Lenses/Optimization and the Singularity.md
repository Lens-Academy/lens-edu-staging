title: Optimization and the Singularity
# tldr: "Jumble a car's atoms at random and you almost never get anything that travels. A mind's power is its ability to hit targets that small in a search space that large, whether the space is possible futures or possible designs."
summary_for_tutor: "Short extract from Eliezer Yudkowsky's 'Optimization and the Singularity' defining an optimization process. The power of a mind is its ability to hit small targets in a large search space: the space of possible futures (planning) or of possible designs (invention). Example: take all the parts or atoms of a car and jumble them at random, and you almost never get a travel artifact at all, let alone one that ranks as high in your preferences as the car. Relative to those preferences the car is extremely improbable, and an optimization process is what produces that kind of improbability. This gives the module its framing of intelligence as power."
