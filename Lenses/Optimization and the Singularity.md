# tldr: "Jumble a car's atoms at random and you almost never get anything that travels. A mind's power is its ability to hit targets that small in a search space that large, whether the space is possible futures or possible designs."
summary_for_tutor: "Short opening extract from Eliezer Yudkowsky's 'Optimization and the Singularity' defining an optimization process. Core idea is that the power of a mind is its ability to hit small targets in a large search space (planning over futures, invention over designs), illustrated by the car example. A car is an extremely improbable artifact relative to the owner's preferences, and optimization is what produces that improbability. Sets up the Cognitive Superpowers module's framing of intelligence as power."
