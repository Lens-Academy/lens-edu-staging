#### Text
content::
In the previous lens, Habryka and Cale worried that control speeds up AI progress. Richard Ngo, who used to work at DeepMind and OpenAI, makes a wider version of this argument about alignment research in general: much of it, he says, helped AI companies race toward AGI, AI as capable as humans across the board, "under the banner of safety". His post is not about control. But control's main pitch is that it lets companies get useful work, including alignment research, out of AIs they cannot yet trust. Read his opening, then his short passage on automating alignment research.
