title: "Pain is not the unit of Effort"
summary_for_tutor: "Full text of alkjash's LessWrong post 'Pain is not the unit of Effort' (2020). It opens with the author's own content warning (self-harm; parts 'may be actively counterproductive for readers with certain mental illnesses or idiosyncrasies'), so take care if a learner raises personal struggles. Thesis: in the author's social bubble, 'pain is the unit of effort', meaning 'how hard you are trying is explicitly measured by how much suffering you put yourself through.' Part I gives five anecdotes (his mother increasing study hours when he looked happy, a sports coach humiliating slow runners, overachieving classmates' complaining contests, Chinese webnovel heroes defined by 'superhuman pain tolerance', a friend who heard 'try harder' as 'hurt yourself more'). Part II argues this belief explains why few people are 'actually trying', and that people 'optimize for the appearance of suffering'; it includes a parable of people throwing themselves at doors instead of trying the doorknob. Two proposed antidotes: '1. If it hurts, you're probably doing it wrong.' and '2. You're not trying your best if you're not happy.' A closing edit says the antidotes 'are not primarily to be interpreted as truth claims', pointing to a comment by Anna Salamon."
