#### Text
content::
For years AI safety was an **inside game**: researchers, quiet policy influence, elite persuasion. Holly Elmore's essay is the canonical case that this is not enough — that AI safety needs an **outside game** too: visible public advocacy that builds salience, pressure, and political will. It launched much of the subsequent pause-and-advocacy debate.

As you read, pin down the theory of change (public movement → political will → policy) — and notice the objections it invites, which the next lenses take up directly.
