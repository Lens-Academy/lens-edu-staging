title: "Another Way to Be Okay"
summary_for_tutor: "Full text of Gretta Duleba's 'Another Way to Be Okay' (LessWrong, 2023), written in parallel with Duncan Sabien's 'A Way to Be Okay'. Addressed to people 'who are pretty freaked out' about existential risk; it is explicitly 'not to debate whether there's doom around the corner' but 'how to cope if you already believe in that doom'. Six sections: Agency and Acceptance (sort things into a can-control bucket for agency and a can't-control bucket for acceptance; most 'Big Bad Dooms' fall in the latter); Actually Grieve (grieving a lost future as an active, repeated practice, with the author's own steps); Keep Striving (pick achievable goals; explicit permission to find meaning in work unrelated to the 'Actual Problems'); The Good Hours ('You can have good seconds and minutes and hours even while you are having bad months and years'; 'Joy is okay'); Connection and Community (avoid isolation; co-regulate with others); More Grasshopper, Less Ant ('Do an even mix of work and play all the time from now on'). Closing reading of the course's final module."
