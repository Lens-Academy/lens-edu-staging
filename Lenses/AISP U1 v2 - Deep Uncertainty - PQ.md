#### Question: Open
id:: a00daeb4-0203-4bd4-8daf-ec96dfaf723c
content::
A researcher tells you: "There is a 3 percent chance that advanced AI causes a global catastrophe in the next twenty years."

Before deciding whether 3 percent is high or low, what would you want to know about how that number was produced?

feedback-instructions::
Respond once in 60 to 100 words. Reflect the learner's questions about data, model structure, assumptions, expert judgment, or calibration. Do not teach Bayesian priors or Pascal's Mugging yet. Do not grade.
