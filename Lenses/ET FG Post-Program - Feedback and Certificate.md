\# Post-Program Feedback & Certificate

You did it! 🎉 Thank you so much for guiding a cohort through the Effective Thesis Accelerator. Over these 8 weeks, you've held space for students as they explored their Ikigai, chose a high-impact problem, prioritised research questions, reached out to stakeholders, built a Theory of Change, and designed theses that can be real stepping stones toward impactful careers. None of that happens without you, and we're so grateful!

\# Help us make this program even better

Your honest reflections, about what worked, what didn't, and what you wish had been different, help us improve the Accelerator and better support the next Campus Directors and Facilitators. There are no right or wrong answers, and we read every response!
