content::
Most competitive AI safety programmes and employers expect applicants to be familiar with recent developments in the field. As the pace of AI development and safety research accelerates, keeping up becomes increasingly demanding. It is worth developing your own way of staying informed: finding sources that are relevant to your interests and establishing a routine you can sustain alongside your work. The resources below offer a starting point.

\### Blogs and newsletters

[**Don't Worry About the Vase**](https://thezvi.substack.com/) (Zvi Mowshowitz)

Extensive weekly summaries of developments in AI, covering new research, model releases, safety incidents, regulation and public debates. Zvi draws together material from papers, podcasts, company announcements and Twitter discussions, adding his own analysis.

[**Astral Codex Ten**](https://www.astralcodexten.com/) (Scott Alexander)

The successor to *Slate Star Codex*. The blog covers a wide range of subjects, including AI risk, forecasting, scientific methodology and rationality. Its AI-related essays often examine competing arguments and disagreements about technological development and existential risk.

[**AI Safety Newsletter**](https://newsletter.safe.ai/) (Center for AI Safety)

A relatively concise overview of developments in AI capabilities, safety research and governance, written for readers without an extensive technical background.

[**AI Safety at the Frontier**](https://aisafetyfrontier.substack.com/) (Johannes Gasteiger)

A selection of recent technical AI safety papers, accompanied by summaries of their findings and significance. Especially useful for following research outside your immediate specialisation.

[**AI Safety in China**](https://aisafetychina.substack.com/) (Concordia AI)

Updates on Chinese AI safety research, policy, institutions and public discussions. A useful complement to sources predominantly covering developments in the US and Europe.

\### Videos and podcasts

[**Robert Miles AI Safety**](https://www.youtube.com/@RobertMilesAI)

Accessible explanations of technical and conceptual problems in AI alignment, including instrumental convergence, reward hacking, corrigibility and specification gaming. Particularly valuable for developing intuitions about foundational concepts.

[**Rational Animations**](https://www.youtube.com/@RationalAnimations)

Animated explanations of ideas from AI safety, rationality, forecasting and effective altruism.

[**AI X-risk Research Podcast (AXRP)**](https://axrp.net/) (Daniel Filan)

Extended conversations with AI safety researchers about their work. The discussions often explore the motivation behind a research agenda, its underlying assumptions and how particular results might contribute to reducing existential risk.

[**The 80,000 Hours Podcast**](https://80000hours.org/podcast/)

Long-form interviews covering AI safety, governance, technological progress and career decisions. Many episodes offer detailed discussions of researchers' worldviews and proposed interventions.

For a more extensive selection, see the [AISafety.com media channels directory](https://aisafety.com/media-channels), which also includes specialised newsletters, podcasts and curated lists of social media accounts.
