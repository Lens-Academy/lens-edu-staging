#### Text
content::
One human-side strategy keeps flesh-and-blood humans in the loop rather than replacing them with uploads. Two variants:

- **Brain-computer interfaces / the "merge."** High-bandwidth BCIs (the Neuralink pitch) aim to keep humans cognitively coupled to AI, so we are not simply left behind as capabilities scale.
- **Human cognitive augmentation.** Enhance human reasoning and coordination so that human oversight can keep pace with increasingly capable AI.

Both intervene on the **human** side of the problem — unlike most AI safety work, which keeps the focus on aligning the AI itself.

#### Text
content::
Common skeptic objections worth being able to state:

- **Capability, not control.** A BCI plausibly *raises* human (and AI) capability faster than it improves our ability to control AI.
- **Whose values?** "Merging" blurs rather than resolves the question of whose values are in charge.
- **Timing.** Like WBE, meaningful human augmentation may arrive *after* transformative AI, so it can't help when it's most needed.
