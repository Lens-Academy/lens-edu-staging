#### Text
content::
Common skeptic objections worth being able to state:

- **Capability, not control.** A BCI plausibly *raises* human (and AI) capability faster than it improves our ability to control AI.
- **Whose values?** "Merging" blurs rather than resolves the question of whose values are in charge.
- **Timing.** Like WBE, meaningful human augmentation may arrive *after* transformative AI, so it can't help when it's most needed.
