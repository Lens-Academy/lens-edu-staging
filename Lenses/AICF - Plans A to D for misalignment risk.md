#### Text
content::
Every route in this unit depends to some degree on how much AI companies and governments want to act on misalignment risk. Ryan Greenblatt, whose dialogue with Habryka you read earlier in this unit, sorts the possible worlds by how much AI companies and governments want to act and asks what the best plan is in each. Two questions follow the reading.
