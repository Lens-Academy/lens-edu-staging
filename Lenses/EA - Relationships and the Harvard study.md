#### Text
content::
This talk makes the case that one circumstance, close relationships, matters a lot. The speaker directs the study he describes. As he says in the talk, it has followed 724 men for 75 years. In our view, a study that follows people over time but does not assign them to conditions is not an experiment, so it can show that relationships and happiness go together but not, on its own, that one causes the other. While you watch, note what was measured (happiness, health, or both) and whether the design can show cause and effect.
