#### Text
content::
For you, the Tutor is a prep assistant and a second pair of eyes on your group.

**Time:** 10 minutes
**Format:** 1 short reading + 1 practice run with the Tutor

\## What the Tutor is for you (1 min)

It is the same Tutor your learners use. Because you lead a group, it also gives you navigator tools. It helps in two ways:

- **It sends you things.** Reminders and updates arrive around each session, mostly as Discord DMs.
- **It answers when you ask.** Chat with it on the web or in your Discord DMs.

\## What it sends you, week by week (2 min)

The training nudge comes before your first session. After that, the same rhythm repeats every session week, from the prep DM to the survey.

**Training week.** If your training is not finished 5 days before your first session, you get a DM listing what is left.

**Before each session.**

- **The prep DM.** Weekly groups get it 3 days before. Intensive and twice-weekly groups get it 23 hours before. It has run-sheet highlights, the session doc link and the Zoom steps, starting with signing in to the shared navigator Zoom account. That account should make you the host and bring in the note-takers.
- **About a day before.** A reminder with the Join Zoom link. Intensive groups do not get this one.
- **The attendance forecast.** A separate DM about a day before, like "we expect about 5 of your 7 learners". Intensive groups get this one too.
- **One hour before.** A reminder with the Join Zoom link and the session doc link, also shown in your group channel.
- **Before you click Join,** sign in to Zoom as the shared navigator account, not your own (the prep DM has the steps).
- **15 minutes before.** An RSVP digest of who is coming, skipping or late. Later RSVP changes reach you as an update.

**During each session.** If your breakout rooms are not being recorded, you get a DM 20 to 45 minutes in. It tells you to open Breakout Control from the navigator account.

**After each session.** About 90 minutes after the start, you get the facilitator survey link by email and DM.

**Covers.** When you ask for a cover, the Tutor asks possible covers and keeps you updated by DM and email. You get a DM when a cover is set or cleared. If you are covering and can't see the group's channel, write in #9-nav-team.

**Anytime.** Check-ins you asked for arrive at the time you set.

\## What you can ask it (2 min)

Some examples:

- **Check-ins.** "Check in with me on Sunday evening about my prep."
- **Meeting docs.** "Show me the session doc for my next meeting." It reads the whole doc and gives you the link.
- **Articles and sections.** "Pull the main reading for my Week 3 session." It searches the course and reads you the section.
- **Covers and absences.** "I can't make Thursday's session. Please find me a cover." Ask at least 2 hours before the session. You can also check on it or cancel it.
- **Your group's progress.** "How is my group doing? Who has RSVPed?" It shows progress, RSVPs and attendance.
- **Rehearsing a session.** "Read my session doc and walk me through the breakout." It reads the whole doc. Ask it to talk you through it or quiz you.
- **Your own account.** "What are my deadlines?" or "Report a bug: my doc link is broken."

\### Coming later

Not ready yet, so do not count on these:

- **Reading a spreadsheet.** It cannot open a Google Sheet, so group progress comes from the platform.
- **The Zoom link in the Tutor's own answer.** Your day-before and hour-before reminders carry it, but the Tutor's answer about your /navigate page may not list it.
- **Reading your /navigate page for you.** That page (lensacademy.org/navigate) lists your groups, next sessions and onboarding steps. The Tutor reading it for you is built, but maybe not switched on for you.

\## When to come to a person (1 min)

Write in #9-nav-team for help. Come to a person for these:

- **The Zoom host key.** Your prep DM says to claim host with it. The keys are pinned in #9-nav-team. You get access to that channel after your onboarding call, so until then email team@lensacademy.org.
- **A cover emergency.** Inside 2 hours the Tutor can't run a cover search, so post your own call for help in the Session Cover Requests thread in #9-nav-team right away (the Tutor may call this channel #navigation-team, but it is the same one). Do the same if your session is further off and the Tutor's cover search hasn't found anyone yet.
- **Access problems** you cannot fix, like a channel you can't open.
- **Anything the Tutor says it cannot do.**

**How to reach a person.** If the Tutor offers to hand your question to the team, say yes. If it does not offer, write it in #9-nav-team yourself. If you can't open #9-nav-team yet, email team@lensacademy.org.

\## Practice with the Tutor (4 min)

Try three real asks with the Tutor below. They only read, so nothing changes and nobody gets a message.

1. "Show me the session doc for my next meeting."
2. "How is my group doing?"
3. "Pull the main reading for my next session."

No group yet? The first two may come back empty, so spend the time on the third.

Not for practice: asking it to find, withdraw or answer a cover, skip or move a session, pass a question to the team, send feedback, or send an email. Those change real things or message real people.

#### Chat
instructions::
A short **practice run** (about 4 minutes), not a quiz. Help the navigator try three read-only asks on their real data:

- **Session doc:** read the doc for their next meeting and give its link.
- **Group progress:** show their group's progress, RSVPs and attendance.
- **A section:** search the course and pull a section for a coming session.

How to run it:
- Let them type each request in their own words. Answer with their real data.
- Only read. Run no tool that changes, books or sends anything. If they ask for one, say it is not for practice.
- If a tool returns nothing because they have no group yet, say so and move to the section ask.
- After each answer, ask how it would help their prep. Keep it to 3 or 4 exchanges.

Probe (if there's room): "Which of these will you ask me before your first session?"
