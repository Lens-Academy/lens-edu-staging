#### Text
content::
Anything in square brackets needs to be swapped out for your own details before sending, for example:

- **\[Link\]**: the Lens Academy portal link for your cohort
- **\[Insert date/time in participant time zones\]**: your session date and time
- **Location/ Zoom Link**: your room or call link
- **\[Paste in based on the week\]**: the focus line for that week (from the table in the Weeks 2-7 template)
- **\[Name\] and \[Name\]**: this week's Mastermind participants
- **\[Your name\]**: your name!

Quick tip: do a final scan for any "\[" before you hit send.

\### Weeks 2-7

#### Callout: Weeks 2-7 Message Template - weekly reminder + Mastermind participants (send each Friday before)
tone:: neutral

#### Text
content::
Hi everyone!

**Focus of this week:** \[Paste in based on the week\]

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| Week 2 | **Selecting a High-Impact Problem:** We’ll be identifying high-impact problem areas for your thesis using the ITN framework. |
| Week 3 | **Generating & Prioritising Research Questions:** We’ll be mapping your problem area, diagnosing its key bottleneck, generating a wide set of candidate questions, and using a Weighted Factor Model to narrow down to your top 1-2. |
| Week 4 | **Stakeholder Mapping and Outreach:** We’ll be translating stakeholder mapping into real-world outreach and expert interviews, as well as addressing the emotional barriers to outreach. |
| Week 5 | **Developing your Theory of Change:** We’ll be developing a Theory of Change (ToC) to connect your thesis research to real-world change. |
| Week 6 | **Reverse Engineering Your Thesis from Your ToC:** We’ll be reverse engineering your thesis from your ToC. Focus on defining tangible outputs and formulating actionable research questions. |
| Week 7 | **Unblocking Your Impactful Career:** We’ll go through the Head, Heart, Hand framework to diagnose your own blockers and take real, concrete next steps |

**📌 Todos**

- Read through the module before our meeting using the Lens Academy Portal \[Link\]
- Begin drafting the Weekly Worksheet and Journal Answers ahead of time, but don’t worry if you don’t finish - we’ll work on it together during the discussion!

**🗓️ Discussion Details**

- Date & Time: \[Insert date/time in participant time zones\]
- Location/ Zoom Link
- Duration: 1.5 hours

**Mastermind Session (Weeks 2-7)**

This week, \[Name\] and \[Name\] will be in the Mastermind session. Please come prepared with your worksheet, questions, and challenges - anything you want the group’s help thinking through.

💡 **Reminder:** Mastermind is practice, not perfection. You don’t need a polished plan - come ready to share, reflect, and learn from each other.

Looking forward to seeing you there and diving in!

#### Text
content::
Hi everyone,

We did it, we’ve officially completed the Effective Thesis Accelerator! 🎉

Thank you so much for being part of this journey, and for continuing to channel your heart for the world into impactful work and research. As you go from here, we hope you keep experimenting and iterating, and that you stay creative in shaping a path that feels truly yours. We hope you continue to support each other too as complex big problems cannot be solved alone. And we hope that you also continue to take moments to really celebrate how far you've come - now, and at each step of your journey!

**📝 One last (super important!) thing**

Please take around 5 minutes to fill in the post-programme feedback form: [https://airtable.com/appcKo5GTmHnUaBlg/pagGHPBww4E31JabV/form](https://airtable.com/appcKo5GTmHnUaBlg/pagGHPBww4E31JabV/form)

Your feedback genuinely shapes how we improve the Accelerator for future cohorts, and it helps us understand what was most valuable for you. If you've already filled it in during our session, thank you!

**🌱 Where to go from here**

This isn't the end of your journey, just the next step! A few things you might like to explore:

- **Keep working with Effective Thesis:** apply for [1:1 Advising](https://www.effectivethesis.org/services) for ongoing support, or the [Effective Thesis Fellowship](https://www.effectivethesis.org/key-services/effective-thesis-fellowship) to work on a high-impact research project with an expert mentor or organisation.
- **Post about your journey on LinkedIn.** We’ll send out your completion certificates in the next few weeks, and encourage you to share what you worked on and what you learned from the program! This can be especially useful if you’re still looking for collaborators and feedback!
- **[Take a (Trial) Giving pledge](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/pledge/effectivethesis):** You don't have to wait until you've landed your ideal high-impact role to start making a real difference, giving effectively is something you can commit to today, as a student. And you don't need to be earning a full salary to start: GWWC's Trial Pledge lets you commit to giving a smaller share of your income now, as a student!
- **Get more support:** [Magnify Mentoring](https://www.magnifymentoring.org/), 1:1 advising with [Probably Good](https://probablygood.org/advising/) or [80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/speak-with-us-o/), and [Rethink Wellbeing's free CBT Lab](https://www.rethinkwellbeing.org/services) for people doing high-impact work.
- **Keep learning and connecting:** the [EA Introductory Program](https://www.effectivealtruism.org/courses/introductory-program), or a [local or university EA group](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/allGroups).
- **Find fellowships and internships:** [80,000 Hours Job Board](https://jobs.80000hours.org/), [Probably Good Job Board](https://jobs.probablygood.org/) and [EA Opportunities Board](https://www.effectivealtruism.org/opportunities).

**💛 Stay connected**

Feel free to keep using our group chat to share updates, wins and questions with each other. I'm so excited to see what we each go on to do!
