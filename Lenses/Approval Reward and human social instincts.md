#### Text
content::
If the brain-like AGI agenda says "reverse-engineer the brain's innate reward system," what does that look like concretely? Byrnes' analysis of human social instincts is a worked example. He argues the reason humans — and today's LLMs — don't behave like power-seeking ruthless consequentialists is a **non-behaviorist part of the reward function he calls "Approval Reward,"** tied to norm-following, morality, and self-image. The alignment culture clash, on his telling, reduces to a disagreement about whether future powerful AIs will have something like Approval Reward (like humans) or not (like pure utility-maximizers).
