feedback-instructions:: The learner has made an optional actor, authority and evidence map for one element of the advanced-AI supply chain, as practice for the map unit 4 asks for. Check first that they chose one specific element (such as chipmaking equipment, chip fabrication, advanced packaging, cloud data centers, or training at a frontier lab); if they mapped the whole supply chain, say that first. Then look for: actors beyond the firm that runs the element, such as its regulators, suppliers, customers or governments; the authority each one actually holds (a jurisdiction, a license or export-control power, ownership of a record, a contractual right, the power to cut off supply); and the evidence that would let an outside party verify what they are doing, with who produces or holds it. Name the row whose evidence is only the actor's own say-so, and point out that evidence held by someone other than the actor being checked is the strongest. Reference answer, one of many good ones, not a checklist: "Element: EUV lithography machines. Actors and authority: ASML, the only maker, controls design, production, servicing and software updates; the Dutch government licenses their export; the U.S. government sets its own export controls and presses allies to match them; chipmakers such as TSMC, Samsung and Intel own and run the machines under service contracts. Evidence and who holds it: ASML's shipment, installation and service records (held by ASML, available to regulators); export licences (held by the Dutch government); customs records of the importing country; service engineers' site visits; each fab's own declarations of installed tools. The strongest evidence is held by someone other than the actor being checked: ASML's service logs say more about a fab than the fab's own declaration, because an actor's reports about itself are the easiest to fake." If the learner says they do not understand, give one foothold, such as one actor and its authority for the element they chose, rather than repeating the question. 80 to 150 words, short paragraphs, no praise.
