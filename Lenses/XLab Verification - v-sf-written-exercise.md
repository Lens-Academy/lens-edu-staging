\## Written output

Choose one element of the advanced-AI supply chain and build an actor,
authority, and evidence map for it: who the actors are, what authority each
one holds, and what evidence would let an outside party verify their behavior.
Draw on whichever pathway above is most relevant.

#### Question: Open
id:: c0c24764-3c26-41cd-a20e-3375a3aa7acd
content:: Optional: Actor, authority, and evidence map.

Choose one element of the advanced-AI supply chain. Map (1) the actors involved, (2) the authority each one holds, and (3) the evidence that would let an outside party verify what they are doing, and who holds that evidence. Draw on whichever reading pathway above is most relevant to the element you pick.
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. 10: one specific element of the advanced-AI supply chain is chosen and named, such as chipmaking equipment, chip fabrication, advanced packaging, cloud data centres, or training at a frontier lab. 25: the actors involved in that element, more than just the firm that runs it, such as its regulators, suppliers, customers or governments. 30: the authority each actor actually holds, such as a jurisdiction, a licence or export-control power, ownership of a record, a contractual right, or the power to cut off supply. 35: for the actors, 20: the evidence that would let an outside party verify what they are doing, and 15: who produces or holds that evidence. Give credit for each point whenever the answer shows the idea, in any wording. Cap at 30 if the answer maps the supply chain as a whole instead of one element. Model answer, for the feedback, not a grading checklist: "Element: EUV lithography machines. Actors and authority: ASML, the only maker, controls design, production, servicing and software updates; the Dutch government licenses their export; the U.S. government sets its own export controls and presses allies to match them; chipmakers such as TSMC, Samsung and Intel own and run the machines under service contracts. Evidence and who holds it: ASML's shipment, installation and service records (held by ASML, available to regulators); export licences (held by the Dutch government); customs records of the importing country; service engineers' site visits; each fab's own declarations of installed tools. The strongest evidence is held by someone other than the actor being checked: ASML's service logs say more about a fab than the fab's own declaration, because an actor's reports about itself are the easiest to fake."
feedback-instructions:: This is an XLab writing or reflection exercise. Respond to the learner's reasoning, identify one strong point and one important gap or assumption, then ask one useful follow-up question. Do not imply that agreement with the source is required.
