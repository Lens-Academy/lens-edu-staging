#### Text
content::
If some future AIs are misaligned, one response is bargaining: pay them to behave. Strange, serious, and increasingly worked-out.

- [Making deals with early schemers](https://redwoodresearch.substack.com/p/making-deals-with-early-schemers) — the core proposal: credible offers to schemers in exchange for cooperation.
- [Notes on cooperating with unaligned AIs](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/oLzoHA9ZtF2ygYgx4/notes-on-cooperating-with-unaligned-ais) — the broader landscape of when cooperation beats conflict. Audio alternative: [Why make deals with misaligned AIs — Lukas on ForeCast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/624xSF4qCInZnF0FxWaQsf?si=POR2q2JkTrWcZCmnCVE7WQ).
- [A taxonomy of barriers to trading with early misaligned AIs](https://blog.redwoodresearch.org/p/a-taxonomy-of-barriers-to-trading) — why the deals might not close: verification, credibility, communication.
- [Being honest with AIs](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/uuikfACQBm4KJZp4w/being-honest-with-ais) — you can't bargain with something you routinely lie to.
- [What Happens When Superhuman AIs Compete for Control?](https://blog.aifutures.org/p/what-happens-when-superhuman-ais) — bargaining among the AIs themselves.
- [Modifying LLM Beliefs with Synthetic Document Finetuning](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/modifying-beliefs-via-sdf/) — the uncomfortable flip side: we can implant beliefs; what does that do to honest dealing?
- Schelling's [Arms and Influence](https://dokumen.pub/arms-and-influence-2008925430-0300143370-9780300143379.html) (Ch. 1) and [Strategy of Conflict](https://www.sackett.net/Strategy-of-Conflict.pdf) (Ch. 1-3) — the bargaining theory everything above quietly relies on.
