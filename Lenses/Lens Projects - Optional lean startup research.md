Remedies include listing ten ideas and asking someone more senior to rank them, noticing when people are genuinely interested, going narrow, and publishing. Most useful for field-track learners, especially technical and policy. \## Why read this

Written by an AI governance researcher for people in their first years of research, this post makes the same argument as this course: start small, find out who actually wants your work, and change course when they don't. Read it with your canvas in mind. Which of its remedies could you try this week?
