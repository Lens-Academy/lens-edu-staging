The AI Futures team rebuilt their forecasting model -- and pushed their own timelines 3-5 years later than AI 2027, mostly by being less bullish on AI speeding up its own development. A look inside how the forecast is built, and why its authors still don't fully trust it."
summary_for_tutor: "Daniel Kokotajlo and the AI Futures Project explain their upgraded unified timelines-and-takeoff model (aifuturesmodel.com), which forecasts when AI reaches milestones like Automated Coder (AC) and superintelligence (ASI). Surveys forecasting methods (expert surveys, argument-informed intuition, revenue extrapolation, brain-compute anchoring, and benchmark trend extrapolation) and justifies extrapolating METR's coding time-horizon trend. Describes the model's three stages -- automating coding, automating research taste up to a Superhuman AI Researcher, and the post-automation intelligence explosion, including the 'taste-only singularity' condition for fast takeoff. Presents the authors' all-things-considered timeline and takeoff views (Eli and Daniel separately) and compares to the earlier AI 2027 model, explaining a roughly 3-5 year lengthening of the median coding-automation timeline driven mainly by more conservative modeling of pre-automation AI R&D speedups and diminishing returns."
