summary_for_tutor: "80,000 Hours' problem profile arguing that the challenges from advanced AI are the most pressing problems facing humanity. It develops a five-step argument: (1) AI could replace human labour in the most economically valuable fields; (2) doing so could trigger the next radical transformation of society; (3) this transformation could be extremely rapid and dramatic, especially with fast feedback loops in AI R&D; (4) it would raise major challenges including existential risks, such as humanity losing control to highly intelligent AI or a group using AI to seize unprecedented power; and (5) this work is tractable but neglected, with only a few thousand people focused on the most serious risks. It supports each claim with current capability examples (PhD-level science benchmarks, agentic coding tools, AlphaFold), addresses common objections, and points toward high-impact career paths. Sequences the learner toward why AI risk is a leading cause area and what they can do about it."
