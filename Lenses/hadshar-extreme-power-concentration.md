Traces three mutually reinforcing routes to concentrated political power -- AI-enabled power grabs (automated military coups, secret loyalties, hacking), economic forces (outgrowing the world, eroded incentives for governments to represent citizens), and epistemic interference -- then covers the potential harms, counterarguments, candidate interventions like law-following AI and alignment audits, and what readers can do."
