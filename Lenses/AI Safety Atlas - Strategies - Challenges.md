Details properties that make it difficult: it is emerging and poorly understood, pre-paradigmatic with no accepted framework, built on opaque trained 'black box' models, prone to complexity-driven blind spots (e.g. glitch tokens), reliant on hard-to-define terms, unlikely to have a single solution, hard to measure, and possibly running short on time. Then covers expert uncertainty and divergent worldviews (Hanson, Sutton), and concludes with 'safety washing', how safety claims can create a false sense of security and even net-negative impact."
