#### Text
content::
Now the strongest counter-case. Anton Leicht argues that building a broad, popular AI safety **movement** is likely to *backfire*: it can taint AI safety's two real assets — organic public concern and expert credibility — through guilt-by-association and astroturf optics; its policy asks keep shifting so the movement can't pivot coherently; and it risks capture by fringe or populist positions that drop existential risk as the central concern. His alternative: **narrow, issue-specific organizations** (the Encode model), not a mass movement.

Read this directly against Elmore's case — the disagreement is the heart of this outcome.
