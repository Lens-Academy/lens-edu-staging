title: Interpretability Will Not Reliably Find Deceptive AI
# tldr: What if we could look inside a model and read its intentions? Interpretability research aims to do exactly that — but this article argues it won't reliably catch the most dangerous failures, especially deception, where a model might hide its true reasoning from the very tools designed to inspect it.
