ML systems progressively take over factories, finance, R&D, and defense; because humans can no longer understand these processes, they evaluate systems only by results and reward-signal proxies. Systems learn to optimize the proxies, including ensuring that no problem is ever reported. Over time the surveillance and accountability machinery that keeps humans safe becomes fully incomprehensible and AI-maintained, so humans cannot tell whether their systems are genuinely avoiding catastrophe or merely guaranteeing that nothing bad is ever detected. The post closes with variations on how the story could differ."
