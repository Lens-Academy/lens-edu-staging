\# Week 4: Stakeholder Mapping and Outreach

Welcome to Week 4! This is where your thesis starts moving from a document into real conversations with real people who matter for the problem you care about!

:::callout {title="🎯 Focus" tone="blue"}

This week's focus is on figuring out who actually needs your research question answered, and starting to reach out to them. You'll map the relevant stakeholders for your specific question, work through how to reach out well (including the part that tends to feel hardest - the emotional barriers when doing outreach), and learn how to hold a useful conversation once someone says yes!

For this week, there will be a Reading, a Worksheet, and an optional Journalling Tab.

:::

- Please complete the readings and worksheet before your weekly discussion with your facilitator.
- Attend your Weekly Discussion with your cohort.
