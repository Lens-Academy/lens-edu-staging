summary_for_tutor: "Optional practice depth. Part of Rob Burbea's instruction talk of 20 Dec 2019 (retreat 'Practising the Jhanas', Hermes Amara Foundation transcript). He defines piti as 'pleasant physical sensations, or pleasant physical feelings, that arise from a non-sensual source', says it can arise many ways (breath and energy body, metta, insight, beauty, openness of heart), and that each person must learn how it arises for them. Once it arises: (1) see if it will spread through the whole body (fine if it does not), (2.1) a narrow, probing mode of attention into where it is strongest, (2.2) an open, receptive mode like sunbathing. Move back and forth with no fixed formula."

#### Text
content::
This is an alternative to Brasington's instruction to "do nothing else" once you find a pleasant sensation. Burbea gave it to retreat students who had started to feel piti. It is a transcript of a spoken talk.
