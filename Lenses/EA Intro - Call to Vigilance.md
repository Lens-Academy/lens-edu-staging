Argues that because it is unclear which actions are helpful versus harmful, readers should respond with vigilance rather than a single one-off action: take robustly good steps now (building knowledge of AI, science and technology, and relevant policy; steering one's career toward relevant directions; connecting with others, including through the effective altruism community) and position themselves to act when opportunities arise. Conveys Karnofsky's mix of urgency, confusion, and hesitance."
