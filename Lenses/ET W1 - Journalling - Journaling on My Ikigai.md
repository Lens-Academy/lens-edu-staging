\## Journaling on My Ikigai

This journaling tab is an optional but highly recommended space for weekly personal reflection alongside the curriculum. Use it as a chance to slow down, check in with yourself, and think about what truly matters to you. Your thesis or project isn’t just something to complete - it can also be a tool for exploring your values, testing your fit, and learning about the life and impact you want to create. Take 10–15 minutes to reflect on the questions below. Write honestly and openly, as there are no “right” answers!

#### Question: Open
id:: e8e8189a-88e3-449e-adea-9f562fd4bb69
content::
**What does your ideal self look and feel like?**

*What might the best version of yourself look like - what values, qualities, skills, and habits define you? How do you show up in your work, relationships, and life?*
optional:: true

#### Question: Open
id:: 00a17ee9-0bd1-4b0e-9457-7f0f9ff3ee18
content::
**What does your ideal day-to-day life look like?**

*Imagine your perfect day five years from now. What does it look like from morning to night? Who are you spending time with? What work or activities make you feel aligned and alive?*
optional:: true

#### Question: Open
id:: 0ef45e5f-db8f-4e53-b274-937426d4438b
content::
**Who is your ideal community?**

*Who do you want to surround yourself with and spend time with - i.e. work peers, friends, family, mentors, collaborators? What kind of energy, values, and expertise do they bring that inspire and motivate you?*
optional:: true
