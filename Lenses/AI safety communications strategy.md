#### Text
content::
Granting that you communicate publicly at all, *how* should you say it? MIRI's 2024 communications strategy is an unusually explicit answer: **say the existential risk plainly and bluntly**, rather than softening it for mass appeal. It's a deliberate bet that candor buys credibility and clarity — and a sharp contrast to moderate, broad-coalition messaging.

Read it as one pole of a spectrum. What does blunt messaging gain (credibility, moral seriousness, clarity)? What does it risk (sounding alarmist or fringe, narrowing the audience)? And what's the opposite bet — moderate messaging for reach — trading away?
