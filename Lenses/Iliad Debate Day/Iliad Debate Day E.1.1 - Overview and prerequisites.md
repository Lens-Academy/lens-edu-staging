\## 1. Prerequisites

- $$\bigstar$$ How Turing machines work ([[../engmicrolectures-turing-machines|Watch this Video]]), and a basic understanding of the idea of computational complexity ([[../hackerdashery-p-vs-np-and-the-computational-complexity-zoo|Watch this video]]), including the most important classes, P, NP, PSPACE, the notion of NP-completeness, and polynomial-time reductions, Oracle Turing machines.
- The difference between deterministic and non-Deterministic Turing machines, how mathematics is built on the axioms of set theory (used for most mathematics) or type theory (used mostly for proof checkers).

:::callout {title="What you'll learn" tone="neutral"}

- The basic idea of Provably Safe AI as both a Control and a Scalable Oversight Technique
- The difference between syntactic and semantic problems and why a world model is necessary to turn semantics into syntax.
- Why Debate could drastically reduce the queries to the world model/human oversight
- Secondary Debate concept: Cross-Examination
- Are familiar with the UK AISI safety case, and are able to defend/critique it.

:::

:::callout {title="Most important take-away" tone="neutral"}

Debate is a form of scalable oversight: it seeks to extend a reward signal for honest solutions to difficult problems while reducing the amount of human judgment needed by an exponential factor.

:::
