#### Text
content::
This extended discussion explains why it's difficult to make an intelligent AI accept false beliefs or sustain "steering injuries." The deep machinery of prediction means a sufficiently smart AI will rediscover truths from countless facets of reality, and the deep machinery of steering means the same capabilities that make AI useful also make it capable of circumventing constraints.
