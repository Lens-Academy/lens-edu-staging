#### Text
content::
Open any school you want a first look at. Each box has one source. The talks and one article are shown here, the rest are linked.

#### Callout: Wellbeing science and positive psychology
collapse:: closed
#### Text
content::
You read one section of Ed Diener's textbook chapter in module 2. The whole chapter covers what predicts happiness and whether it can be raised: [Happiness: The Science of Subjective Well-Being](https://nobaproject.com/modules/happiness-the-science-of-subjective-well-being) (about 18 minutes).
#### End Callout

#### Callout: Evolutionary psychology of happiness
collapse:: closed
#### Text
content::
David Buss's 2000 paper "The Evolution of Happiness" argues that some evolved mechanisms work against lasting happiness. The section "Three Additional Evolutionary Tragedies" takes about 5 minutes: [Buss 2000 (PDF)](https://people.uncw.edu/bruce/psy%20292/pdfs/happiness.pdf).
#### End Callout

#### Callout: Neuroscience of wanting and pleasure
collapse:: closed
#### Text
content::
An interview with the neuroscientist Kent Berridge on why the brain's systems for wanting and for liking are separate: [Pleasing Your Brain](https://brainworldmagazine.com/pleasing-your-brain-an-interview-with-dr-kent-berridge/) (about 6 minutes).
#### End Callout

#### Callout: CBT and the third wave (ACT, MBCT, DBT)
collapse:: closed
#### Text
content::
Steven Hayes on psychological flexibility, the aim of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) (TEDx talk, 19 minutes). For classic CBT, the Beck Institute's page [Understanding CBT](https://beckinstitute.org/about/understanding-cbt/) is short.

#### Video
source:: [[../video_transcripts/tedx-talks-psychological-flexibility-how-love-turns-pain-into-purpose-steven-hayes-tedxuniversityofnevada]]
#### End Callout

#### Callout: Humanistic and existential psychology
collapse:: closed
#### Text
content::
A short textbook section on Maslow, Rogers and self-actualisation: [OpenStax Psychology, 11.5 Humanistic Approaches](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/11-5-humanistic-approaches) (about 3 minutes).
#### End Callout

#### Callout: Attachment and psychodynamic
collapse:: closed
#### Text
content::
R. Chris Fraley's textbook chapter on attachment from infancy to adulthood. The section "Do Early Experiences Shape Adult Attachment?" takes about 2 minutes: [Attachment Through the Life Course](https://nobaproject.com/modules/attachment-through-the-life-course).
#### End Callout

#### Callout: Parts and body-based work (IFS, Focusing, somatic)
collapse:: closed
#### Text
content::
The IFS Institute's own description of Internal Family Systems, on its home page: [What is IFS?](https://ifs-institute.com/) (about 2 minutes). It is the school's training organisation, so read it as the school's view of itself.
