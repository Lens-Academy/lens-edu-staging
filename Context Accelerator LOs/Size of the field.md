Give a rough, dated estimate of how many people work on AI safety and in which areas, say why such estimates are uncertain, and compare them with the AI capabilities workforce and its funding.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[AI safety ecosystem]]

## Why it matters
Knowing how small the field is helps you judge where one more person or dollar can make a difference, and why some problems have nobody working on them. It also guards against assuming that someone else must already be handling a given risk.
