Explain what s-risks are, how advanced AI could bring them about, and why some people rank them alongside or above extinction risk.

## Requires
- [[Existential risk]]

## Helpful first

## Why it matters
https://www.youtube.com/shorts/witfVCwlJAM

Some researchers and funders focus on s-risks, which leads them to different priorities, such as cooperation between AI systems, rather than only preventing extinction. Understanding the idea helps you follow these debates and see that making sure humanity survives is not the same as making sure the future goes well.

## Lenses
- [[../video_transcripts/rational-animations-s-risks-fates-worse-than-extinction|S-Risks: Fates Worse Than Extinction]]: what s-risks are, example scenarios, how suffering can arise from neglect or conflict without anyone wanting it, and the lottery argument for ranking them above extinction risk.
- [Beginner's Guide to Reducing S-risks (Center on Long-Term Risk)](https://longtermrisk.org/research/beginners-guide-to-reducing-s-risks/): why AI is central, the ethical and empirical premises behind prioritising s-risks, incidental, agential and natural routes (including near-miss alignment and conflict between AIs), and proposed fixes such as cooperative AI and surrogate goals; the case from the leading s-risk research group (September 2023).
- [S-risks (80,000 Hours)](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/s-risks/): a short profile of the problem from an organisation that thinks s-risks are much less likely than extinction, covering types of s-risk, why the issue is neglected, and narrow and broad ways to help (September 2022).
