Describe concrete good outcomes advanced AI could bring, such as faster medical science and material abundance, and explain why many thinkers favour preserving the ability to reflect and change course over locking in one vision of utopia.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[AGI and superintelligence]]

## Why it matters
Work on AI risk is easier to direct when you know what success looks like, not only what to avoid. Knowing the range of good outcomes helps you judge proposals by whether they keep the future open, and helps you notice when a plan would lock in one group's values, even with good intentions.
