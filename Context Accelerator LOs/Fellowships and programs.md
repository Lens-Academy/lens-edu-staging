Name the main types of programs that help people into AI safety work as of November 2026, give an example of each, and pick the type that suits a person's background and goals.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[MATS]]

## Why it matters
Many people enter AI safety through a structured program rather than a direct job application. Knowing which programs teach skills, which produce research, and which suit governance or founding helps you choose a next step that fits your stage, instead of applying to whatever you hear about first.

## Lenses
- [Roadmap through AI safety programs for early-career technical researchers (LessWrong)](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/muH6H9i8CtNAubcoo/roadmap-through-ai-safety-programs-for-early-career): a step-by-step path from BlueDot courses to ARENA to part-time remote fellowships like SPAR and then full-time paid ones like MATS, LASR, ERA, Pivotal and Anthropic Fellows, with how selective each step is (May 2026).
- [How to get into AI safety in three months (80,000 Hours)](https://80000hours.org/2026/09/how-to-get-into-ai-safety-in-three-months/): how to build context, pick skills, apply widely to fellowships and jobs, and start real work without waiting for permission (September 2026).
- [Want to upskill in AI policy? Here are 57 useful resources (80,000 Hours)](https://80000hours.org/2026/04/want-to-upskill-in-ai-policy-here-are-57-useful-resources/): read the "Fellowships" section for the main governance and policy fellowships, such as GovAI, IAPS, Horizon and the policy streams of ERA, Pivotal and SPAR, with their format and length (April 2026).
- [Catalyze Impact (Catalyze Impact)](https://www.catalyze-impact.org/): what an AI safety incubator offers people who want to found a new organisation: early funding, coaching, investor introductions and help choosing a problem (2026).
