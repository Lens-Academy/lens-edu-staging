Define takeoff, intelligence explosion, recursive self-improvement and the technological singularity, and explain the basic argument that AI able to do AI research could drive a rapid, self-reinforcing rise in capability, along with its main weak points.

## Requires
- [[AGI and superintelligence]]

## Helpful first

## Why it matters
How fast AI could go from human level to far beyond it decides how much time people would have to respond. Knowing these terms and the argument behind them lets you follow forecasts and safety plans that depend on takeoff speed, and judge what would make a fast takeoff more or less likely.
