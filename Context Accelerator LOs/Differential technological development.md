Explain differential technological development and defensive acceleration (d/acc), give examples of protective technologies they would speed up, and describe why many think the idea is hard to apply in practice.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[Offense-defense balance]]

## Why it matters
Many people choosing what to build or fund use this idea, often without naming it. With it, you can ask whether a project helps protective technology arrive before dangerous technology, and notice when that is hard to tell, for example because the same tool helps both attackers and defenders.

#### Question: Open
id:: 69d2a467-1c25-4946-8e45-a1764fd5296e
content:: Give two reasons why many people think differential technological development is hard to apply in practice.
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. 50 points each for any two of: many technologies are dual-use and help attackers and defenders; it is hard to predict which technologies will turn out protective or their long-run effects; one actor has limited leverage over timing, since others may build the same thing anyway; competition and commercial pressure drive what gets built; protective work can speed up dangerous work too. Accept other well-reasoned reasons.
