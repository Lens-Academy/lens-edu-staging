Explain the main arguments that AGI is still decades away, and the replies that people expecting shorter timelines give to each.

## Requires
- [[Timeline forecasts]]

## Helpful first
- [[Jagged capabilities]]

## Why it matters
Forecasts of when AGI arrives range from a few years to many decades. Understanding the case for long timelines lets you weigh it fairly against the case for short ones, plan for more than one possible future, and tell a well-grounded objection from wishful dismissal.
