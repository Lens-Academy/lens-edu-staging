Weigh AI's benefits against its risks: explain the main arguments that faster development saves lives and that slower development lowers existential risk, and what each argument depends on.

## Requires
- [[Why AI gets built]]
- [[Existential risk]]

## Helpful first

## Why it matters
Most debates about pausing, regulating or speeding up AI come down to this tradeoff. A learner who can separate the cost of delay from the cost of a catastrophe can see where two sides actually disagree, instead of talking past each other.
