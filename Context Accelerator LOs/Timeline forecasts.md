Describe what current forecasts from AI company leaders, forecasting platforms, superforecasters and surveyed researchers say about when AGI might arrive, and explain why they can differ by decades.

## Requires
- [[AGI and superintelligence]]

## Helpful first
- [[Task-length trends]]

## Why it matters
Whether AGI comes in five years or fifty changes which safety research, policies and careers make sense: work that pays off in decades helps little if AGI comes soon. Knowing the real spread of forecasts lets you plan for several timelines instead of anchoring on one confident voice.
