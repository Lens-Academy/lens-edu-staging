Describe the main arguments for and against people concerned about AI risk working at frontier AI companies, including the "build it safely first" case, the "ten people on the inside" case, and worries about racing and incentives.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[AI race dynamics]]
- [[Capabilities externalities]]
- [[AI companies]]

## Why it matters
Many people who take AI risk seriously are offered jobs at frontier AI companies, and these choices shape both their own impact and how the companies behave. Knowing the strongest case on each side helps you judge a specific role on its merits, not on a company's reputation or pay.
