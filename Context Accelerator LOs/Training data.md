Describe where frontier AI models get their training data, how its content shapes model behavior, and the main arguments for and against data running short as a limit on progress.

## Requires

## Helpful first

## Why it matters
A model learns its knowledge, habits and blind spots from its training data, so knowing what goes in helps you predict what comes out. Data is also one input to AI progress: knowing whether it could run short helps you judge forecasts that assume scaling continues.

## Lenses
- [Is AI running out of human-written text? (AP, via PBS NewsHour)](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/ai-gold-rush-for-chatbot-training-data-could-run-out-of-human-written-text-as-early-as-2026): the "gold rush" for training data: licensing deals with Reddit and news outlets, Epoch's estimate that public text runs short between 2026 and 2032, synthetic data and the risk of "model collapse" (June 2024; dataset sizes have grown since).
- [[../Lenses/AIF - Can AI Scaling Continue Through 2030|Can AI Scaling Continue Through 2030?]]: read only the "Data scarcity" section (skip the tables): how much usable web text exists after quality filtering and deduplication, why the Chinchilla rule ties compute growth to a "data wall", and whether video, images, repeated passes and synthetic data could push it back (Epoch AI, August 2024).
- [[../Lenses/anthropic-enhancing-model-safety-through-pretraining-data-filtering|Pretraining Data Filtering for Safety]]: Anthropic removed weapons-related documents from pretraining data and found the model knew less about them without losing other skills; read the TLDR, Introduction, Setup and Conclusions, which also suggest filtering out stories of misbehaving AIs (August 2025).
