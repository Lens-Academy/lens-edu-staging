Describe where frontier AI models get their training data, how its content shapes model behavior, and the main arguments for and against data running short as a limit on progress.

## Requires
- [[Machine learning]]

## Helpful first

## Why it matters
A model learns its knowledge, habits and blind spots from its training data, so knowing what goes in helps you predict what comes out. Data is also one input to AI progress: knowing whether it could run short helps you judge forecasts that assume scaling continues.
