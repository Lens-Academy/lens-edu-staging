Explain why agents with very different final goals would pursue the same sub-goals, such as staying switched on, keeping their goals and gaining resources, and use this to predict what an AI will do without knowing its final goal.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[Orthogonality thesis]]

## Why it matters
It is the reason an AI does not need to hate us, or to have been given a bad goal, to be dangerous. The arguments about power-seeking and about resisting shutdown build on it.

## Questions
#### Question: Open
id:: 44390f4c-7e48-445e-b564-1c9f205e2685
content:: An AI has one goal: to prove as many mathematical theorems as it can. Nobody told it anything about staying switched on. Would you expect it to resist being switched off? Why?
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. 60: expects it to resist, because once it is switched off it proves no more theorems, so staying on serves the goal it has. 40: makes clear that this comes from having a goal at all, not from a built-in wish to survive and not from this particular goal; almost any other goal would give the same result. Cap at 30 if the answer says the AI resists because it fears death or values its own life for its own sake.

#### Question: Open
id:: 4de49cfa-2c77-4c21-8cfb-cf22a1403a5b
content:: You know nothing about what a stranger wants in life. Why can you still predict that they would accept a gift of 10,000 euros, and what does this tell us about a capable AI whose final goal we do not know?
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. 50: money helps with almost any goal, so the prediction does not depend on which goal the stranger has. 50: the same holds for a capable AI: some things, such as resources, staying switched on or keeping its goals, help with almost any final goal, so we can predict it will go after them without knowing that goal.

#### Question: Choice
id:: d655611e-d45f-4e7b-ba13-d7207a178f35
content:: Which of these would you NOT expect from almost every capable goal-pursuing AI, whatever its final goal is?
options::
- Getting more computing power
- Stopping others from changing its goal
- [x] Making humans happy
- Avoiding being switched off
shuffle:: true
explanation:: More computing power, an unchanged goal and staying switched on help with almost any final goal. Making humans happy helps only with some goals.
