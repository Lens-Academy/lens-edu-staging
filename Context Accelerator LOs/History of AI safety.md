Outline the main stages in the history of AI safety, from early warnings through the rationalist and effective altruist years to mainstream attention after 2022, and explain how each stage shaped today's organisations, research and disputes.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[Existential risk]]
- [[Rationalist community]]

## Why it matters
Today's AI safety organisations, funders, research agendas and feuds grew out of a short and specific history. Knowing it helps you see why groups trust or distrust each other, why some safety-minded people work at AI companies, and why political attention to the field has risen and fallen.
