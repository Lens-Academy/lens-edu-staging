Describe the fast or discontinuous and the slow or continuous views of AI takeoff, give the main arguments for each, and explain how takeoff speed changes the time and options for reacting.

## Requires
- [[Takeoff and intelligence explosion]]

## Helpful first
- [[Automated AI research]]

## Why it matters
How fast AI goes from roughly human-level to far beyond decides whether there will be warning signs, time to fix problems in weaker systems, and room for governments to act. If you know which view a plan assumes, you can see where it fails if the other view turns out right.
