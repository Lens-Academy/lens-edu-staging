Describe the main existing international efforts on AI (the summit series, UN processes and the International AI Safety Report), what each has produced, and how far each is from binding rules on advanced AI.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[AI governance]]

## Why it matters
Any future global agreement on AI will build on these forums or replace them. Knowing what they have achieved, and where agreement broke down, lets you judge treaty proposals realistically, read summit news correctly, and see where shared scientific evidence already exists.
