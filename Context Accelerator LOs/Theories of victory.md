Describe the main stories of how humanity could get safely through the transition to advanced AI, tell delaying dangerous AI apart from making AI safe, and say what each published plan needs to go right.

## Requires
- [[AI race dynamics]]

## Helpful first
- [[Risk argument structure]]
- [[AI governance]]

## Why it matters
Many actions sound helpful but only reduce risk if they fit a story that ends safely. With a theory of victory in mind, you can ask what a project needs in order to matter, spot plans with missing steps, and see where your own work could make a difference.

#### Question: Open
