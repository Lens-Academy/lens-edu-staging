Compare the main ways to work on AI safety, inside and outside safety organizations, and explain what employers currently look for, how people typically get in, and which skills the field is shortest of.

## Requires

## Helpful first
- [[Types of safety work]]

## Why it matters
Most people who take AI risk seriously next ask what they could do about it. Knowing which roles exist, what hiring managers screen for and where the field is short of people helps you aim at roles you can get and that are hard to fill, instead of applying widely and stalling.

#### Question: Open

content:: AI safety organizations report being short of people, yet many capable junior applicants can't get hired. Explain how both can be true.
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. 100 for explaining that hiring is limited by senior people's capacity to mentor and supervise: each can supervise only a few juniors, so organizations hire people who need little supervision, and junior supply outpaces what they can absorb. 50 for saying "seniors are scarce" without linking it to absorbing juniors. 0 for "there isn't enough funding" alone.
