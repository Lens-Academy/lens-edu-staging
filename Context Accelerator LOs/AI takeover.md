Explain what AI takeover, loss of control and human disempowerment mean, how they differ from extinction and from human misuse of AI, and what successionists believe about AI replacing humans.

## Requires
- [[AGI and superintelligence]]

## Helpful first
- [[Existential risk]]

## Why it matters
Most arguments about AI risk are about preventing AI takeover, so you need these terms to follow them. Mixing up takeover with robot uprisings, extinction or misuse leads people to dismiss the risk or to back safeguards aimed at the wrong problem.

#### Question: Open
id:: 86e3c8d5-83fe-44c4-a197-b09fcedbe389
content:: Someone says: "AI takeover is science fiction: it means robot armies fighting humans." How do AI risk researchers actually use the term?
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. 100 for saying that takeover means control over key decisions, resources or humanity's future passing to AI systems against human wishes, and that it need not involve robots or open violence (e.g. it could happen through hacking, persuasion, economic control or gradual handover). 50 for only one of these two points.

#### Question: Open

content:: Someone says: "If an AI takeover doesn't kill everyone, it isn't an existential catastrophe." What is wrong with this claim?
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. 100 for explaining that existential catastrophe includes permanent loss of humanity's potential or ability to shape its future, not only extinction, so a takeover that leaves humans alive but permanently powerless can still count. 50 for saying disempowerment is bad without linking it to permanence or lost potential.
