## Glossary

### Module 1

Day-zero model: The five-to-ten-sentence model of the next ten years of AI you wrote before reading anything else. The rest of the course is a series of revisions to it, always restated from memory.
Order of magnitude (OOM): A factor of ten. "+12 OOMs" means multiplying the available compute by 10^12.
TAI (transformative AI): AI that transforms the world at least as dramatically as the industrial revolution did; the arrival Kokotajlo's thought experiment asks about.
Trend: One measured quantity over time, like tallest structure, ship tonnage, or transatlantic message speed.
Step: One new data point in a trend's record: a single new ship, bridge, or bomb.
Jump (large robust discontinuity): A step that moves the metric further than a century of progress at the trend's previous rate would have.
Base rate: How often something happens across all measured cases, used as a starting point before arguing about the case in front of you.
Inside view / outside view: Reasoning from the details of your specific case versus from measured rates across many similar cases; Kokotajlo's inside-view 99% versus his deference-discounted 90% is the pair in action.
R-squared: A 0-to-1 measure of how well a curve fits the data it was measured on. It says nothing about the range where there is no data.
Falsifier: A stated observation that would count against a trajectory; the unit asks for one per trajectory you produce.
