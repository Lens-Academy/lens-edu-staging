#### Question
content:: What is the organizing bet behind the NeuroAI for AI Safety roadmap? State it in your own words and say why it implies safety, not just capability.
assessment-instructions:: A strong answer states the bet: more constraints from human brain biophysics, representations, and behavior raise the probability of landing in the "basin" of safe, human-like solutions. The safety logic: many different paths reach intelligent behavior, but most lack the safety properties (robustness, cooperation, sensible values) we associate with human cognition, so constraining AI toward brain-like solutions biases it toward the rare safe region rather than just any capable one. Award 4-5 for the bet plus the many-paths/most-unsafe reasoning; 3 if the bet is stated but the safety rationale is thin; 1-2 if it's described as merely "copy the brain to be smarter." Accept paraphrase.
max-chars:: 600
#### Question
content:: List at least four of the seven research approaches in the roadmap, and name the one approach that most directly connects this agenda to mainstream mechanistic-interpretability work.
assessment-instructions:: The seven: (1) reverse-engineer sensory representations (robustness/adversarial examples), (2) embodied digital twins, (3) detailed biophysical/connectomic neural simulations, (4) better cognitive architectures (modular/probabilistic theory-of-mind, cooperation), (5) brain-informed process supervision (fine-tune on neural/behavioral data), (6) reverse-engineer brain loss functions, (7) neuroscience-inspired interpretability. Any four earn credit. The interpretability link is approach (7). Award 4-5 for four correct approaches plus correctly naming #7 as the interpretability bridge; 3 for four approaches but missing/wrong on the interpretability link, or three approaches plus the link; 1-2 for fewer than three. Accept paraphrased names.
max-chars:: 600
#### Question
content:: NeuroAI-for-safety and "brain-like AGI safety" both study the brain. What is the key difference in what each assumes about the AI being made safe?
assessment-instructions:: The distinction: NeuroAI-for-safety (this agenda) uses neuroscience to inform whatever AI we build, and that AI need NOT itself be brain-like — we import robustness, cooperation, better objectives, interpretability, etc. Brain-like AGI safety (Byrnes) instead ASSUMES the dangerous AGI is itself built on brain-like learning algorithms and aligns it on those terms. Award 4-5 for clearly capturing "informs the AI we build (not necessarily brain-like) vs the AI itself is brain-like"; 3 for a muddled gesture at the difference; 1-2 for conflating or inverting them. Accept paraphrase.
