**Part 2: Even done well, control makes things worse**

The criticisms in Part 1 said control is not worth the effort. The criticisms in this part go further. They say that even if control works as intended, it has side effects, and those side effects could outweigh the safety it adds.

This part covers:
- Control speeds AI up without making it safer in the long run.
- Control hides the warning shots that would make the world act.
- Optional: control could wrong the AIs themselves, if they matter morally.

For each one, ask what would realistically happen without control, and whether the harm the critic describes would be larger than the safety control adds.
