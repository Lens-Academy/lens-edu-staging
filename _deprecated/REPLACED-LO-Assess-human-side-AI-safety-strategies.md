#### Question
content:: What is whole-brain emulation, and why is it proposed as an AI safety strategy? Then explain the timing tension that could undermine it.
assessment-instructions:: A strong answer: (1) WBE / mind uploading = scanning, mapping, and simulating a specific brain at high enough resolution to reproduce its function (scan-map-simulate). (2) Safety rationale: a human-derived digital mind might inherit human values, offering an alternative route to advanced digital intelligence that is easier to trust/control than a de novo AGI. (3) Timing tension: the research needed for WBE (reverse-engineering neural algorithms) may deliver neuromorphic/brain-inspired AGI BEFORE it delivers WBE (~85% at a 2011 workshop expected brain-inspired AI first), so pushing WBE could accelerate the uncontrolled-AI risk it was meant to sidestep. Award 4-5 for all three; 3 for two; 1-2 for one. Accept paraphrase.
max-chars:: 700
#### Question
content:: Explain the two brain-computer-interface / augmentation ideas for keeping humans in control, and give one reason a skeptic doubts they work as a safety solution.
assessment-instructions:: A strong answer names both ideas: (1) high-bandwidth BCIs / the "merge" (keep humans cognitively coupled to AI so they aren't left behind), and (2) human cognitive augmentation (enhance human reasoning/coordination so oversight keeps pace). For the objection, credit any valid one: a BCI likely raises capability faster than it improves control; "merging" blurs rather than resolves whose values are in charge; augmentation/BCI may arrive after transformative AI (timing). Award 4-5 for both ideas plus a valid objection; 3 for one idea plus an objection, or both ideas with a weak objection; 1-2 otherwise. Accept paraphrase.

#### Question
content:: In one or two sentences, contrast the human-side family's strategy with the other two families (NeuroAI-for-safety and brain-like AGI safety).
assessment-instructions:: A strong answer captures that human-side approaches intervene on the HUMAN side of the problem — keeping humans in control or relevant (via BCI/merge, augmentation, or human-derived uploads) — rather than making the AI itself safe: NeuroAI-for-safety uses neuroscience to build/align/interpret the (de novo) AI we make, and brain-like AGI safety aligns an AI that is itself brain-like. Award 4-5 for a clean contrast that correctly characterizes all three; 3 if the human-side point is right but one other family is fuzzy; 1-2 if the contrast is wrong or missing. Accept paraphrase.
