Whether interpretability can keep up with larger and more alien models, and fundamental limits such as concepts shifting as capability grows. Not this: enumeration specifically (see Enumerative safety).

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/MI for AGI Safety]]
Tier 1: Argues that understanding a network 'at rest' is not enough: much AGI cognition will be interactive and externalised. Examples: a recipe book, Leahy's room-scan bag packing, beavers' hatred of running water, Sydney/Bing using social media as memory. Reflection could also change behaviour without new training data. Vivid but speculative; March 2023 talk.

### [[../Lenses/Interpretability Will Not Reliably Find Deceptive AI]]
Tier 1: Concise list of why interpretability won't be reliable: missed features, no ground truth, long tail, proving a negative. Adds scalability doubts and the risk of obfuscation, with an honest caveat that automated interpretability researchers could change this. A good teaching list, given as inside-view opinion.

### [[../articles/ericjmichaud-on-neural-scaling-and-the-quanta-hypothesis]]
Tier 2: Full text argues a quanta-like scaling story is the worst case for extracting a model's full 'disentangled model'. Ever-sparser mechanisms would need ever-larger cross-layer transcoders trained on ever more data; the author sketches less bad alternative stories. Speculative but concrete; one section of a long post.
