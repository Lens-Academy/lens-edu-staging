Whether an AI accepts correction, modification and shutdown: the shutdown problem, utility indifference, the hard problem of corrigibility, shutdown resistance as observed behaviour. Not this: mild optimization and impact penalties (see Mild optimization, Impact regularization); self-preservation as a convergent drive (see Instrumental convergence).
