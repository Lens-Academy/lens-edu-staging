Whether LLMs will scale to AGI, or a different, possibly far more compute-efficient paradigm (such as brain-like AGI or a 'simple core of intelligence') will replace them, and what that would change for compute, takeoff and safety.

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/byrnes-sympathy-for-both-sides-of-the-egregious-misalignment-debate]]
Tier 1: Reconciles both sides with one claim, 'LLMs won't scale to ASI'; asserted here, argued in Foom & Doom. Both 'true objections' rest on it: LLM alignment evidence is off-topic for ASI. Useful as a short statement of the stakes of the paradigm question; pair it with the Brain-in-a-box lens for the argument.

### [[../Lenses/byrnes-foom-doom-1-brain-in-a-box-in-a-basement]]
Tier 1: Central premise: LLMs won't reach ASI; a 'simple(ish) core of intelligence', brain-like AGI, will, with little compute. Uses the human cortex as existence proof, contrasts imitation learning with learning from scratch (Nicaraguan Sign Language), and guesses one consumer gaming GPU could train it. The author's own research agenda; that LLMs won't scale is stated, not argued, here.
