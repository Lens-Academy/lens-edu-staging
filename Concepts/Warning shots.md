Incidents in which AI visibly causes or nearly causes serious harm short of catastrophe, whether such incidents will come before a catastrophe, and whether they would lead to serious action.

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/AI Control May Increase Existential Risk]]
Tier 1: Argues AI control turns moderate warning shots into hidden or ineffective ones, so fewer incidents spur action. Kulveit's case: civilizational resistance would stop most early rogue AIs anyway, leaving a moderate warning shot that prompts serious action, while labs would sanitise the incidents that control catches. Short and asserted; the excerpt gives no evidence about how societies react to incidents.
