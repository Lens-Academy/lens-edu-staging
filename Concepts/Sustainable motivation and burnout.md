Keeping effort in AI-safety work sustainable: not measuring effort by suffering, not letting urgency crowd out curiosity and play, and avoiding burnout.

A synopsis plus terse bullet justifications (learned helplessness, play as long-term investment, intensity without throwing your mind away); dense and idiosyncratic. ### [[../articles/tsvibt-please-dont-throw-your-mind-away]]
Tier 1: Full post opens with a dialogue: x-risk urgency turns into 'urgent fake thinking' and squeezes out play. ### [[../Lenses/alkjash-pain-is-not-the-unit-of-effort]]
Tier 1: Defining source: treating pain as the unit of effort makes people optimize for visible suffering, not results. Five anecdotes (study, sport, overachievers, webnovels, a friend) and a door-bashing parable; two antidotes: 'if it hurts, you're probably doing it wrong' and 'you're not trying your best if you're not happy'. An edit says these are not mainly truth claims; not about AI safety; self-harm content warning.
