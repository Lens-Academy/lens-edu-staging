### [[../articles/bowkis-automated-alignment-is-harder-than-you-think]]
Section 4.1 asks whether training on easier-to-supervise proxies generalises to hard-to-supervise research tasks. Worked example: predicting outcomes of existing honesty evaluations as a proxy for designing honesty experiments. It argues the proxy is no better than our evaluations, and generalisation cannot be validated without evaluating the true task.
