AI systems that deceive overseers or pursue hidden goals: deceptive alignment, scheming, alignment faking, sandbagging, deep deceptiveness, and the situational awareness that enables them. Not this: measuring it (see Deception and scheming evaluations); detecting it from internals (see Interpretability for detecting deception); honest reporting of beliefs (see Uncertainty, calibration, honesty, and truthfulness).
