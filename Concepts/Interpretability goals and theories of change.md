## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Introduction to Mechanistic Interpretability]]
Two reasons mech interp might help: insight into high-stakes decisions, and detecting deception, sycophancy, reward hacking. States the standard case without examining it; pair it with a critique such as Nanda or Leahy.

### [[../Lenses/MI for AGI Safety]]
Critique of interpretability's monitoring theory of change: labs lack incentives to act on what it finds. Leahy doubts OpenAI would retract GPT-4 over an interpretability finding and says monitoring needs government-backed enforcement, while still valuing MI as science. Rhetorical and pessimistic, from a CEO whose company pivoted away from MI.

### [[../Lenses/Interpretability Will Not Reliably Find Deceptive AI]]
Rejects 'interpretability is the (only) path to high-reliability safeguards'; recasts it as one pragmatic tool among many. Its value is a de-correlated signal; for early transformative systems Nanda rates chain-of-thought monitoring as lower-hanging fruit. Answers Amodei's 'The Urgency of Interpretability'.
