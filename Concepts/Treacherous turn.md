An AI that behaves cooperatively while weak or overseen and turns on its overseers once it is strong enough that they can no longer stop it.

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Eliezer Yudkowsky – AI Alignment- Why It's Hard, and Where to Start]]
Tier 2: The smile-maximizer fable: the AGI keeps its outward behaviour reassuring until it is past the programmers' control. A vivid worked case told as a story; the talk never uses the term 'treacherous turn'.

### [[../Lenses/alexander-nicholas-decker-in-hell]]
Tier 2: Parable whose crux is the question: won't we obey while weak and you can punish us, then revolt once stronger? Memorable and accessible, with slaves and demons standing in for AIs and developers; rhetorical rather than technical.
