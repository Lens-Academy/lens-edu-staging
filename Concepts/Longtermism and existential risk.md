The case that the long-term future matters greatly, so reducing existential risk is a priority, and what counts as an existential risk (extinction, unrecoverable collapse or dystopia). Not this: AI-specific catastrophe arguments (see The core extinction argument); classifying risks by scope and severity (see Risk scope and severity classification); futures worse than extinction (see S-risks); this century as the hinge (see Most important century hypothesis).

Tier 2: Defines x-risk as losing all future value, then argues s-risks may matter more than extinction risk. The Althaus-Gloor lottery analogy (avoiding extinction buys tickets; avoiding s-risks raises each ticket's value) is the most teachable part. It argues from the suffering-focused side, so pair it with a standard longtermist source.
