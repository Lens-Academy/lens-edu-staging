## Relationships

### [[../video_transcripts/rational-animations-s-risks-fates-worse-than-extinction]]
Defines x-risk as losing all future value, then argues s-risks may matter more than extinction risk. The Althaus-Gloor lottery analogy (avoiding extinction buys tickets; avoiding s-risks raises each ticket's value) is the most teachable part. It argues from the suffering-focused side, so pair it with a standard longtermist source.

### [[../video_transcripts/rational-animations-could-a-global-dictatorship-last-forever]]
Short definition of existential risk as lost potential, not just extinction, via Ord's 'unrecoverable dystopia'. The opening thought experiment (a regime still ruling in 2500 and a million years on) makes it vivid; good for widening learners' picture of x-risk beyond extinction.
