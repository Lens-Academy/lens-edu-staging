## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Shut It All Down - Yudkowsky TED Talk]]
Stockfish analogy: you can't predict a smarter opponent's moves, but you can predict who wins. The air conditioner sent to the 11th century makes the same point about unknown physics; accessible but asserts more than it argues.

### [[../Lenses/Without fundamental advances (trimmed, Extinction Risk Foundations)]]
Defining source: misalignment and catastrophe are the default for AIs able to do large-scale novel science. The trimmed lens walks the chain: hard tasks need consequentialism, behavioural training misses goals, control fails, misaligned AI disempowers humans. Long and assertive; it ends with requests to developers and deliberately does not speculate on the 'fundamental advances'.

### [[../Lenses/The AI Book That's Freaking Out National Security Advisors]]
Accessible walkthrough of Yudkowsky and Soares's argument that superintelligence built now would kill everyone. Takes each step in turn (grown not crafted, proxy training, instrumental convergence, indifference, one shot) and weighs critiques such as Carlsmith's. A 2026 video essay, persuasive in tone; its news examples will date.

### [[../Lenses/Deadly By Default]]
Start-to-finish case that extinction is the default, in eleven steps a smart high-schooler can follow. Moves from incentives and opacity to convergent drives, indifference and the intelligence gap ('humans do not lose to beetles'), then 'the trap'. One MIRI staffer's personal, pessimistic take; some 2024 facts are dated.

### [[../Lenses/Risks from power-seeking AI]]
Defining overview: 80,000 Hours' five-claim case (after Carlsmith) that power-seeking AI could disempower humanity. Careful, evidence-heavy and current to 2026, with explicit uncertainty; long, but the claims map cleanly onto a teaching sequence.

### [[../Lenses/Wikipedia Existential Risk]]
Encyclopedic overview of the AI x-risk thesis: the gorilla argument, control and alignment, endorsers and sceptics. Neutral and well referenced, with a Lens footnote correcting the control/alignment framing; wide but shallow on each argument.
