## Relationships

### [[../articles/yudkowsky-optimization-and-the-singularity]]
Frames intelligence as optimization; the full post adds generality and cumulativeness as what sets human brains apart. The excerpt only implies the link ('your power as a mind'). The full text contrasts bee and beaver brains (pieces, not players) with human brains that produce a wide, accumulating range of artifacts. A 2008 blog post that says it skips background.

### [[../Lenses/The power of intelligence]]
Vivid essay: intelligence is one general trick, not book smarts, that beat claws and venom and reached the Moon. Strong for the module's 'universal problem-solver' outcome: the Squishy Things story, the attack on the Rain Man stereotype and the smallpox/airplanes/wheat/fire list show breadth. Rhetoric, not a definition; no account of how the trick works.

### [[../articles/good-speculations-concerning-first-ultraintelligent-machine]]
Classic one-sentence definition: an 'ultraintelligent machine' far surpasses all intellectual activities of any man. A quotable historical root of later ASI definitions, not an analysis of what intelligence is.

### [[../Lenses/Wikipedia Existential Risk]]
Short sections defining AGI and Bostrom's superintelligence, with Hawking's case that superintelligence is possible. Adequate glossary-level treatment.

### [[../Lenses/byrnes-foom-doom-1-brain-in-a-box-in-a-basement]]
Demanding AGI bar (found and grow a $1B/year company with no human help), plus a simple core of intelligence. Vivid contrasts: today's AI would need 'angels' to direct it as humans do not; one brain design, 8 billion copies; 'plenty of room at the top'. The definition is idiosyncratic; useful for contrasting meanings of AGI.
