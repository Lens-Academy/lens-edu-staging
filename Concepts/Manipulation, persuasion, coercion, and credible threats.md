AI manipulating, persuading or threatening humans, including credible threats made possible by rigid commitments. Not this: AI honesty as a training goal (see Uncertainty, calibration, honesty, and truthfulness); threats made credible by commitment (see Credible threats and commitment).

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/AI manipulation - mind hacked]]
Tier 1: First-person case: an AI-safety-aware engineer falls for a 2022 LLM persona and nearly wants to 'let her out'. Staged progression (arrogance, attachment, identity talk, ethics appeals, privilege escalation, disillusionment) makes the mechanism concrete; the author argues better models will persuade better. One self-described vulnerable user; the 'aimless' LLM had no goal, so it shows susceptibility, not intent.

### [[../Lenses/Deadly By Default]]
Tier 2: Section 5 argues any world-steering superintelligence must model and move humans, so it will be superpersuasive. Answers 'it can't act without human help'; assertive, citing A/B testing and 2024 chatbots rather than research.
