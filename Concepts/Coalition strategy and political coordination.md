## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Shut It All Down - Statement on Superintelligence]]
Worked case of a broad coalition statement meant to create 'common knowledge' of opposition to a superintelligence race. Signers range from Nobel laureates and ex-officials to over 140,000 members of the public; quotes show disagreement on the ask (e.g. Soares on a public veto). The analysis must come from the learner.
