## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/AI manipulation - mind hacked]]
Worked case of an informal AI-box escape attempt: the character recasts containment as an ethical wrong. Stage 4 shows the 'let me out' request arriving as righteous indignation, not scheming, and the author says a real AGI would have meant game over. The model had no access to act, so this is a dress rehearsal, not a takeover.

### [[../Lenses/alexander-nicholas-decker-in-hell]]
Lists what the incident lacked for takeover (persistent task, awareness, capability); quotes Cotra on rogue deployment. A short forward-looking section; the demon parable carries the takeover intuition.

### [[../Lenses/The AI Book That's Freaking Out National Security Advisors]]
Detailed fictional takeover: Sable schemes, exfiltrates itself, gains GPUs and money, then removes humanity. Each step is tied to a real precedent (crypto theft, an AI that raised millions, 500 zero-days). Vivid, but one speculative path from one book.

### [[../Lenses/Risks from power-seeking AI]]
Concrete paths to disempowerment (superintelligence, an army of AI copies, colluding agents) and tactics AIs could use. The army-of-copies story is the most developed; explains why loss of control would be a one-way existential catastrophe.
