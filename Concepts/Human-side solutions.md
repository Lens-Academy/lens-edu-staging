## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Letter From Utopia]]
Urges radical self-enhancement: defeat ageing, move minds to durable media, grow cognition beyond human genius. Treats enhancement as the route to flourishing, not as a way to keep pace with AI, so it fits this concept only in part. Rhetorical, with no technical content.
