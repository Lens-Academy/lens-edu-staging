Making humans more capable to keep pace with AI: brain-computer interfaces, merging, cyborgism, cognitive enhancement, whole-brain emulation. Not this: enhancement as an end in itself (see Positive visions of the long-term future).

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Letter From Utopia]]
Tier 2: Urges radical self-enhancement: defeat ageing, move minds to durable media, grow cognition beyond human genius. Treats enhancement as the route to flourishing, not as a way to keep pace with AI, so it fits this concept only in part. Rhetorical, with no technical content.
