## Relationships

### [[../articles/greenblatt+buck-the-case-for-ensuring-that-powerful-ais-are-controlled]]
Sorts control techniques: expensive human oversight, cheap automated monitoring, limited affordances, hardening. The appendices list many concrete techniques; useful as a catalogue, though less structured than later control papers.

### [[../articles/gillen+barnett-without-fundamental-advances,-misalignment-and-catastrophe-are-the-default-outcomes-of-training-powerful-ai]]
Full report critiques AI-assisted monitoring: untrusted monitors and collusion, honeypots, weak monitors, paraphrasing. Argues these patches don't change the picture much against a powerful misaligned AI; a 2024 sceptic's counterpoint, argued rather than tested.
