Concrete control techniques and their parameters: trusted and untrusted monitoring, auditing budgets, editing or replacing suspicious actions, catching models red-handed, legibility requirements, collusion-resistant monitor validation. Not this: how a scheming model could attack (see Control threat models); running control evaluations and using caught attempts (see Evaluating and using control); whether control is a good strategy at all (see The control agenda).

Tier 2: Sorts control techniques: expensive human oversight, cheap automated monitoring, limited affordances, hardening. The appendices list many concrete techniques; useful as a catalogue, though less structured than later control papers.
