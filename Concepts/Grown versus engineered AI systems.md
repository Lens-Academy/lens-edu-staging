How AI made by gradient descent differs from engineered software: nobody designs its internals, so understanding training does not mean understanding the model. Not this: how training works technically (see Neural networks and gradient descent).

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/The AI Book That's Freaking Out National Security Advisors]]
Tier 2: Clear beginner segment: deep learning grows a network rather than writing code, so 'there is no line 47 to point to.' Contrasts hand-coded rules with trained networks and draws the black-box consequence; includes Carlsmith's caveat that humans are black boxes too.

### [[../Lenses/AI Is Grown, Not Built]]
Tier 1: Defining popular source: engineers understand the training process but not the AI it grows, like DNA and an adult. Clear and short, with quotes from Altman, Amodei and Hassabis admitting they do not understand their models, and Grok's 'MechaHitler' episode as evidence. Advocacy adapted from the book If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies; it asserts rather than demonstrates uncontrollability and calls for an international treaty.
