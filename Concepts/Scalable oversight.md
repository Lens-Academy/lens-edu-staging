## Relationships

### [[../articles/bowkis-automated-alignment-is-harder-than-you-think]]
Section 4.2 argues existing protocols (recursive reward modelling, debate, IDA) may fail on fuzzy alignment research tasks. Two reasons: decomposed subtasks still need fuzzy human judgement, and protocols cannot recombine subtasks with correlated uncertainty. A recent critique of the area as a whole, applied to automated alignment.
