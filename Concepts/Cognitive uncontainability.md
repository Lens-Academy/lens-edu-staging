The argument that a superintelligence could act through laws of nature and technologies we do not know, so we cannot foresee its strategies, or even recognise them when shown, though we can predict that it wins.

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Shut It All Down - Yudkowsky TED Talk]]
Tier 2: Q&A answer: an air conditioner sent to the 11th century would surprise its builders, as unknown laws would surprise us. Yudkowsky adds the Stockfish point (you can't predict a smarter player's moves) and says a superintelligence may exploit laws of nature we do not know. One vivid paragraph, spoken, with no further argument.
