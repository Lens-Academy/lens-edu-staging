The claim that capabilities generalize further than alignment, so alignment properties that held before a capability leap fail to carry over once a system starts to generalize far beyond its training.

Uses the evolution analogy (human intelligence applied to physics without being 'trained for' it) and notes it need not involve recursive self-improvement; no argument for why alignment fails to generalize.
