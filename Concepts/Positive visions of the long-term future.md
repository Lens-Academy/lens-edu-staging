Concrete pictures of good long-term futures, with or without transformative AI: cures, abundance, science, and posthuman and transhumanist futures. Not this: keeping options open instead of aiming at a fixed picture (see Better futures, viatopia, and option preservation).

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Letter From Utopia]]
Tier 1: Bostrom's lyrical letter from a possible posthuman future: secure life, upgrade cognition, elevate well-being. Rich, quotable prose for the size of the upside, with cautions ('go easy on your paradise engineering', 'swerve if you smell your wingtips singeing'). Transhumanist, not AI-specific: AI appears only as 'artificial persons you might one day create'; no route to get there.

### [[../Lenses/The Goddess Of Everything Else]]
Tier 2: Poetic allegory ending in a vision of beings freed from Darwinian bonds, spreading over the stars. Only the last section pictures the future (rewritten genomes, a galaxy lit up with consciousness); the rest is evolutionary epic and AI is never named. Good as an emotional close, not a concrete or argued picture.
