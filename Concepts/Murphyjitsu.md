Imagining that a plan has failed and asking why, so as to find and fix the failure modes, including many individually unlikely ones.

## Relationships

Tier 2: One short paragraph uses 'Murphyjitsu' for stamping out many individually unlikely failure modes. Example: data poisoning by a terrorist labeller is unlikely, but a hundred such scenarios together matter. It does not describe the CFAR technique itself (imagining the plan failed and asking why), so use it only as an application.
