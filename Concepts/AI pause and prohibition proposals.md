Proposals to pause, halt or prohibit frontier AI or superintelligence development (moratoria; simple, surgical, regulatory and total pauses; international bans), how they would be enforced, and the arguments for and against them, including backfire effects.

## Relationships

### [[../Lenses/Shut It All Down - Yudkowsky TED Talk]]
Yudkowsky's 'best bad take': an international ban on large training runs, enforced up to risking war over data centres. Includes tracking GPU sales and monitoring data centres, and says a six-month moratorium is too short; the Q&A adds that he does not propose individual violence. A stark position, not a policy design.

### [[../Lenses/Shut It All Down - Statement on Superintelligence]]
Primary text of FLI's call to prohibit superintelligence until scientific consensus and strong public buy-in. Signatory quotes show variants: Russell says it is 'not a ban', Benson-Tilsen thinks the conditions allow too much. Mostly a signatory list.

### [[../Lenses/Shut It All Down - Pause For Thought]]
Best overview of the pause debate: Simple, Surgical, Regulatory Pause, Total Stop and No Pause, with arguments for each. Summarises a 2023 EA Forum debate, including Belrose's backfire effects and Barnett on pauses becoming indefinite, and ends with Alexander's view; dated (GPT-4-era thresholds) but well organised.
