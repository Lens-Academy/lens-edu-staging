## Relationships

### [[../widgets/ai-risk-scope-severity]]
Catalogue of 15 kinds of AI harm, from copyright and job loss to takeover and s-risk, each briefly described. Useful as a broad map that puts near-term harms beside catastrophic ones; it does not sort them into misuse, misalignment or structural categories, and depth per risk is minimal.

### [[../articles/safety-statement-on-ai-extinction-risk-cais]]
Primary evidence of expert concern: the 2023 CAIS one-liner ranking AI extinction risk with pandemics and nuclear war. A short, citable hook on who takes the risk seriously (lab heads, Hinton, Bengio); it gives no argument and no estimate.

### [[../Lenses/Wikipedia Existential Risk]]
Overview of risk kinds: misuse (manipulation, cyber, pathogens), arms races, lock-in, decisive versus accumulative. Also gives survey figures and lists sources of expert concern; breadth, not depth.
