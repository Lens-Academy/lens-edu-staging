## Test:

#### Question: Open
id:: f4e44728-14c0-4cf4-8488-8d6306d3aef7
content::
A government suspects that a new class of AI systems could create a severe irreversible risk, but the evidence is weak. It can deploy immediately, impose a permanent ban, or require a six-month evaluation period that will generate evidence relevant to the risk.

What considerations should determine which option is best? Explain why neither "always be cautious" nor "wait for certainty" is an adequate rule.

assessment-instructions::
Score out of 100.

30 points: The answer considers severity and irreversibility of the possible failure.

25 points: The answer considers the costs and risks created by intervention or delay, including competing risks.

25 points: The answer explains the value of information and whether the six-month evaluation can realistically change the decision before key options disappear.

20 points: The answer explains why a blanket precaution rule and a certainty-before-action rule each fail to handle relevant tradeoffs.

feedback-instructions::
Give 100 to 160 words. Identify which decision dimensions were handled well and the most important missing one.
