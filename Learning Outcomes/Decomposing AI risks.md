content:: People worried about catastrophic harm from advanced AI are not all worried about the same thing: the risks split into several distinct categories that call for different responses. assessment-instructions:: A strong answer identifies at least three distinct categories of catastrophic AI risk, e.g.: (1) autonomous misaligned AI (an AI that pursues goals not in line with human wishes), (2) AI misuse for destruction (e.g. a person using AI to assist bioterrorism or other catastrophic harm), and (3) AI misuse for seizing power / concentration of power (e.g. enabling global totalitarianism). The answer must keep the categories distinct — a key distinction is misalignment (the AI's own goals diverge from human wishes) vs. misuse (a human deliberately directs an aligned-to-them AI toward harm). Award a high score (4-5) for three clearly distinguished categories plus a concrete example; mid (3) for three categories with blurred distinctions or no example; low (1-2) if fewer than two categories or if misalignment and misuse are conflated.
