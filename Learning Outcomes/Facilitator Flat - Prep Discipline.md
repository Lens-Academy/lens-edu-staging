## Test:
id:: 729042fd-6164-4bae-8ceb-203d5995ee35

#### Question
content:: You will be tempted to over-prepare. Reading the entire reading three times, writing 15 candidate questions, drafting an opening monologue — these feel diligent. They mostly aren't. There's a small list of prep moves that reliably improve quality and a longer list that's busywork.

Name three prep moves that actually move session quality, and three prep moves that are busywork. For each "busywork" item — why does it feel useful but actually isn't?

assessment-instructions::
**1** — Treats over-preparation as a virtue. Cannot distinguish high-value from low-value prep.

**2** — Names some prep moves but conflates volume with quality.

**3** — Names at least three high-value prep moves AND three busywork moves, and for each busywork move explains why it feels useful but isn't. A move counts as high-value if it makes the facilitator better able to respond to what participants actually do in the room (e.g. reading the text once with a participant's eye; preparing a few good questions in a deliberate order; one backup plan for one foreseeable problem; a rough session shape and timing — any equivalent move qualifies). A move counts as busywork if it adds volume or polish to the facilitator's own material without improving that responsiveness (e.g. scripting the opening word-for-word; writing out detailed answers to one's own questions; designing elaborate activities; re-reading the text several times — any equivalent move qualifies). Grade whether the student's items fit these two categories, not whether they match the examples above. Fail if the answer's "high-value" moves are mostly volume-based, or if it cannot say what makes the busywork items feel diligent.

**4** — As above, plus recognizes that a small set of prepared questions should do different jobs at different points in the session (e.g. a low-stakes opener, a mid-session deepener, and one that pushes toward application — or an equivalent spread of functions) rather than being several variations on the same theme.

**5** — As above, plus pre-commits to a 20-30 minute prep ceiling for their next session and articulates how they'll resist the pull to do more.
