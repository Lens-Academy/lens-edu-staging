## Test:
id:: 1a2d5016-342d-4016-a6a1-1b9c19e95085
#### Question: Open
id:: 0f6a69a2-7111-46d9-85ae-b01c06f451d2
content::
A new autonomous system performs scientific research substantially better than individual human researchers.

A policy memo concludes: "This establishes that the system presents an existential threat."

Evaluate the inference. Identify the additional facts you would need before reaching the memo's conclusion.

assessment-instructions::
Score out of 100.

25 points: The answer distinguishes scientific capability from dangerous agency, persistent objectives, or misalignment.

25 points: The answer identifies conditions affecting the system's ability to acquire resources, scale, coordinate, or exert strategically relevant influence.

25 points: The answer identifies the role of oversight, containment, human institutions, competing systems, or the ability of humans to respond.

25 points: The answer identifies what would have to make failure catastrophic and irreversible rather than merely harmful.

Credit different threat-model decompositions when they cover the same independent reasoning steps. Do not require the learner to believe catastrophe is likely.

feedback-instructions::
Respond in 80 to 150 words. Name which missing links the learner identified clearly and point to the single most important unsupported jump if one remains. Do not turn the feedback into an argument for or against AI risk.
