## Test:
id:: a1b77f17-45a1-4185-a689-164d910fa8f0
#### Question: Open
id:: 71f901fb-c636-45bf-90fe-e0ba7822ef85
content::
A country deploys AI systems that follow operator instructions reliably. Firms automate most cognitive labor, states become less dependent on citizens for revenue and administration, and political and economic decisions increasingly respond to machine-driven incentives. Humans remain alive, but their ability to redirect these systems steadily falls.

What catastrophe pathway is described here? Explain why it is not primarily a rogue-AI takeover story, and name one kind of intervention that would target this pathway more directly.

assessment-instructions::
Score out of 100.

40 points: The answer identifies gradual disempowerment or an equivalent systemic pathway centered on declining human influence.

35 points: The answer explains that the mechanism does not require autonomous misalignment or AI systems independently seeking power; ordinary incentives and substitution can drive it.

25 points: The answer proposes an intervention aimed at preserving human influence, institutional responsiveness, or resilience, rather than only changing an AI system's internal goals.

feedback-instructions::
Give 90 to 150 words. Identify whether the learner correctly located the mechanism. Below full credit, point out if the proposed intervention targets a different threat model.
