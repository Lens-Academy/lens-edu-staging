An overview of the key organizations, programs, and projects operating in the AI safety space.

The AISafety.com field map is an interactive directory of organizations, programs, projects, and resources in AI safety. It includes entries across categories such as advocacy, blogs, capabilities research, career support, conceptual research, empirical research, forecasting, funding, governance, newsletters, podcasts, research support, resources, strategy, training and education, and video.

The map helps learners discover the landscape of AI safety work: major labs and research groups, policy and governance organizations, funders, training programs, community infrastructure, information resources, and career support options.
