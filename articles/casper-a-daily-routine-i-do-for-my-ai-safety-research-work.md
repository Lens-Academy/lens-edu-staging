I’m a Ph.D student at MIT working in AI safety--mostly interpretability in deep networks. I thought I would very briefly share a list of links that I have bookmarked and go through every weekday. It usually takes me <15 minutes. Since I started doing this, I’ve been much more up to date and informed on current work. 

First is [Twitter](https://twitter.com/). For a long time, I resisted using Twitter because it’s terrible. But after I started following almost exclusively AI safety people and orgs, I’ve found a lot of good papers and ideas. 

Second, I check [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/) and the [AI Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/) :) 

Finally, I look over new titles on arXiv for [AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent), [ML](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/recent), [Computation and Language](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/recent), and [Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CV/recent). More often than not, I find a new paper that I want to click on and check out. 

And as a bonus, I’d of course also recommend [these](https://rohinshah.com/alignment-newsletter/) [three](https://jack-clark.net/) [newsletters](https://newsletter.mlsafety.org/).
