## What this article demonstrates

This body is deliberately ordinary Markdown. When embedded in a Lens, the platform adds article-specific presentation around it:

- source attribution with author and publication date
- a link to the original source
- article typography
- excerpt boundaries and controls
- article media treatment
- widgets embedded in the article body, like the one below
- a callout around elements: the box below holds a question and the widget
