76,210 signatures

Including 5,000 from the same [petition by Ekō](https://action.eko.org/a/sign-the-statement-on-superintelligence)

**Context**: Innovative AI tools may bring unprecedented health and prosperity. However, alongside tools, many leading AI companies have the stated goal of building superintelligence in the coming decade that can significantly outperform all humans on essentially all cognitive tasks. This has raised concerns, ranging from human economic obsolescence and disempowerment, losses of freedom, civil liberties, dignity, and control, to national security risks and even potential [human extinction](https://safe.ai/work/statement-on-ai-risk). The succinct statement below aims to create common knowledge of the growing number of experts and public figures who oppose a rush to superintelligence.

_For corrections, technical support, or press enquiries, please contact [letters@futureoflife.org](mailto:letters@futureoflife.org)_

Statement

**We call for a prohibition on the development of superintelligence, not lifted before there is**

1.  **broad scientific consensus that it will be done safely and controllably, and**
2.  **strong public buy-in.**
