A Tentative Hypothesis

**It will be very helpful if you read this article on [Wanting and Liking](https://slate.com/technology/2009/08/the-powerful-and-mysterious-brain-circuitry-that-makes-us-love-google-twitter-and-texting.html) by Emily Yoffe at Slate Magazine before studying the chart below.** The article probably oversimplifies things a bit - and most likly the chart below is very much an oversimplifyication (e.g. the roles of serotonin and other neurotransmitters are not addressed at all). But this is my best guess as to what's happening in the brain during the experience of the four jhanas - remember it's a tentative hypothesis.

| 1. | Quite secluded from sense desire, _secluded from unskillful states_ -- **one enters & remains in the first jhana**: rapture & happiness born of seclusion, _accompanied by thinking & examining_. | Quite secluded from the dopamine-based wanting system (substantia nigra), _secluded from any other disturbing mental activity_ -- **one enters & remains in the first jhana**: norepinephrine system activation & opioid-based liking system activation born of seclusion, _accompanied by background mental activity_. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2. | With the stilling of thinking & examining, **one enters & remains in the second jhana**: rapture & happiness born of concentration, _unification of awareness free from initial thinking & sustained thinking_ -- internal tranquility. | With the stilling of background mental activity, **one enters & remains in the second jhana**: norepinephrine system activation & opioid-based liking system activation born of concentration, _unification of awareness free from background mental activity_ -- internal tranquility due to opioids flooding the nervous system. |
| 3. | With the fading of rapture, _one remains in equanimity, mindful & clearly aware_, and experiences happiness throughout ones being. **One enters & remains in the third jhana, of which the Noble Ones declare**, 'Equanimous & mindful, one has a pleasurable abiding.' | With the fading of norepinephrine system activation, _remaining equanimous, mindful & clearly aware -- with continued opioid-based liking system activation, one_ experiences happiness throughout ones being. **One enters & remains in the third jhana, of which the Noble Ones declare**, 'Equanimous & mindful due to opioid system activation, one has a pleasurable abiding.' |
| 4. | With the abandoning of pleasure & pain -- _as with the earlier disappearance of elation & distress_ -- **one enters & remains in the fourth jhana**: mindfulness fully purified by equanimity, _neither pleasure nor pain_. | With the abandoning of liking generated by opioid system activation -- _as with the earlier disappearance of wanting generated by dopamine system activation_ -- **one enters & remains in the fourth jhana**: mindfulness fully purified by the equanimity remaining from the prior opioid activation, _a neutral state now that the liking systems have calmed down_. |
| Notes: There are two mesolimbic dopamine systems: the substantia nigra (wanting) system and the ventral tegmental (liking) system.<br>sense desire = _kamachanda_ = activation of the dopamine-based wanting system<br>rapture = _piti_ = activation of the dopamine-based liking system (housed in the Nucleus Accumbens) which generates norepinephrine which gives rise to the "symptoms" of piti<br>happiness = _sukha_ = activation of the opioid-based liking system (also housed in the Nucleus Accumbens) |  |  |
