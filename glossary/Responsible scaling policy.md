An AI company's published policy that ties its safety measures to what its models can do: when a model reaches a set capability level, the company commits to add safeguards or pause until it can. The name comes from Anthropic's policy of 2023. Other companies call theirs frontier safety or preparedness frameworks. Often shortened to RSP.
