    How the score works

    Each risk's score multiplies a weight for its scope (Personal 1, Local 10⁴, Global 10⁹, Trans-generational 10¹¹, Cosmic 10¹³) by a weight for its severity (Imperceptible 0.001, Endurable 0.1, Terminal 1, Hellish 3) and by the average likelihood across your range. The scope weights grow so fast because Bostrom argues an existential catastrophe destroys the whole future, not just present lives. Bars use a log scale, so each step is a large multiple.
