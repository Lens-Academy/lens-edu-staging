summary_for_tutor: "A static horizontal timeline of the verification plan, running left to right from 2026 to 2036+ along a single ruled axis with year ticks beneath it. Seven numbered diamond markers sit on the axis, each labelled above it at a staggered height so the labels do not collide: 1 Chip tracking (2026), 2 Verification R&D funding (2027), 3 Mutual Chip Declaration and 4 Inference-only retrofit (both early 2029, close enough together that their markers nearly touch), 5 R&D verification (2030), 6 Hardware cap & trade (2032), and 7 Verification improvements (2034). Beneath the years the span is divided into three phases: Phase 1: Preparing, 2026-2028; Phase 2: Implementing, 2029-2030; Phase 3: Improving, 2031-2036+. The figure makes the shape of the plan legible at a glance: a sparse preparatory stretch, a dense cluster of commitments in 2029-2030 when the deal is actually stood up, and then slower improvement steps thereafter. Each milestone label is a link to the matching section, and the milestones are the only marked events, so the empty stretches of axis are meaningful rather than incidental."
