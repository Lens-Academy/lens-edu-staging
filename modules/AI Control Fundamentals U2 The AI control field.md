#### Question: Open
id:: 1f07d77a-6981-4703-aecd-dcf56f9453c6
content::
Three pieces of control work:

(a) A PhD student measures how well current models can tell a real deployment task from a synthetic test made to look real.

(b) A team builds the system that records every action of a company's internal AI agents, so that monitors and human reviewers can see them.

(c) An AI company publishes a report on the risks from its own internal agents. A team reads it, checks its claims against information the company shares privately, and publishes its own verdict on the report.

For each: what access to AI developers' systems, or independence from them, does it need? So which kind of organisation is placed to do it? Give a real example of such an organisation where you can.
placeholder:: Take (a), (b), then (c)
force-feedback:: first
assessment-instructions:: Score out of 100. Three pieces of work: 33 for (a), 33 for (b), 34 for (c). For each: 17 for what access to developers' systems or independence from developers it needs, and 16 (17 for c) for a kind of organisation that follows from that, with a real example where possible (a fitting kind without an example earns 10). (a) A PhD student measures how well models can tell a real deployment task from a synthetic test: it needs only access to models, not a company's systems, so a university group, a nonprofit such as Redwood Research or a government institute such as the UK AI Security Institute can do it. (b) A team builds the system that records every action of a company's internal agents for monitors and reviewers: it needs the company's own systems and agents, so mainly the AI company itself, such as Google DeepMind, OpenAI or Anthropic. (c) A team checks a company's report on its own agents against private information and publishes a verdict: it is only worth something if the team is independent of the company, and it needs the company's cooperation to see private information, so an outside evaluator such as METR or a government institute. Either the independence or the access reason earns the full 17 for (c). Do not take off points for naming no research area. Accept other reasoned placements. Give credit for each point whenever the answer shows the idea, in any wording. Model answer, for the feedback, not a grading checklist: "(a) It needs only models, so a university group or Redwood Research can do it. (b) It needs the company's own systems, so the AI company itself, for example Google DeepMind. (c) It is only credible from someone independent who still gets the company's private information, so an outside evaluator such as METR."
feedback-instructions:: Practice question before the field test. Reference answer: (a) anyone with model access can do it, because it needs no company systems: a university group, Redwood Research or the UK AI Security Institute. (b) only the AI company can build it for its own agents, because it needs access to its own systems, as Google DeepMind's prototype monitor over a million coding-agent tasks shows. (c) a third-party evaluator such as METR, or a government team such as the UK AI Security Institute's Control Red Team, because the review is only worth something if it is independent of the company, though it needs the company's cooperation to see private information. Accept other reasoned placements. In 80 to 150 words: name what is right, then the most important correction. Up to two turns. No generic praise.
