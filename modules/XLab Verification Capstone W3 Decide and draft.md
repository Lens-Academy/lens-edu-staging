\## This week's project work

About four hours, after the method check. The target is the draft described in the next lens: in order, holes labelled, first paragraph says what it is, shareable. If you switched brief at the method check, the first ninety minutes go to the new brief's crappy version; the rest to the draft.

Spend the hours in this order:

1. **Structure.** Put every section in its final order, each with at least a heading and a sentence on what goes there.
2. **The section that carries the answer.** The table, the rule set, the ranking, the protocol. Get it to a state where a reader could disagree with it.
3. **Evidence for the load-bearing claims.** The two or three claims the answer rests on: source them or show the calculation. Everything else can be a labelled hole.
4. **The first paragraph.** Reader, question, current answer. Write it last, when you know what the answer currently is.

\## Before meeting 3

- Draft handoff submitted (the next lens).
- Draft link and reader's guide sent to your review partner, with sharing permissions checked.
- The hardest problem you have right now, in one sentence, for the group.
