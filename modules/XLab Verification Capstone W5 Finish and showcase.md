\## This week's project work

About two and a half hours, spent on closing, not opening:

1. **Close what can be closed.** Go through the labelled holes. Any that a source or a calculation settles in under thirty minutes, settle.
2. **Convert the rest.** Every remaining hole becomes a sentence in the limitations section: what it is, why it is open, what would close it.
3. **First paragraph.** Reader, question, answer, how sure. If a reader stopped here, they would know what you concluded.
4. **Sources.** Everything you relied on, in a form a reader can follow.
5. **Stop.** Then run the checklist in the next lens.

\## Before meeting 5

- Final submission (the next lens), before the meeting.
- Your four showcase beats, and one table or figure if it carries the answer.
