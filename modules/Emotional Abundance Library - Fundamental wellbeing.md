%% Library module on Jeffery Martin's research on persistent non-symbolic experience (PNSE), which he calls fundamental wellbeing in public. Sources are Martin himself (Closer To Truth interview, the PNSE preprint, the preprint of the 2023 Finders Course paper, his 2015 slides, the Clearer Thinking podcast), a participant's review (LessWrong), Scott Alexander's reading, and the Headless Way site for the pointing experiment. Left out as low value: Doug Toft's Substack critique (mostly relays an anonymous Reddit post) and the Life Itself summary (secondary, no new facts). Required time about 1 h 55 min by the lenses' own estimates (optional Scott Alexander lens 16 min). The experiments themselves run from ten minutes to six weeks outside the platform. The paper's emotion section and abstract are already in module 2 (lens "Sukha, equanimity and fundamental wellbeing") and are not repeated. %%
