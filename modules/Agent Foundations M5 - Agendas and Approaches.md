tldr:: How agent foundations researchers plan to solve alignment, in five readings by Nate Soares, John Wentworth and Eliezer Yudkowsky, with optional alignment proposals and research agendas.
summary_for_tutor:: Opening page of Module 5 of Agent Foundations for Superintelligence-Robust Alignment, a course by Alignment Ecosystem Development reproduced on Lens. The module covers research agendas and approaches. The site gives no introduction for this module, only the reading list. The readings that follow are 2018 Update: Our New Research Directions (Nate Soares, MIRI), The Plan (John Wentworth), and three Arbital entries: Autonomous AGI and Pivotal act (Eliezer Yudkowsky) and Corrigibility (Nate Soares). The three Arbital entries are marked as the most important. Optional bonus resources follow: three alignment proposals (Physicalist Superimitation, previously called Pre-DCA, by Vanessa Kosoy, explained in a video by Jack Parker, the Universal Alignment Test by plex and collaborators, and Metaethical AI by June Ku), then Agent Foundations for Aligning Machine Intelligence with Human Interests (Nate Soares and Benja Fallenstein), MIRI's Approach (Nate Soares) and The Learning-Theoretic Agenda: Status 2023 (Vanessa Kosoy).

*Module 5 of the [Agent Foundations course](https://agentfoundations.study/#module5) by Alignment Ecosystem Development.*

- 2018 Update: Our New Research Directions – Nate Soares [2018, 41 mins]
- The Plan – John Wentworth [2021, 17 mins]
- **Autonomous AGI** – Eliezer Yudkowsky [2015, 1 min]
- **Pivotal act** – Eliezer Yudkowsky [2015, 9 mins]
- **Corrigibility** – Nate Soares [2015, 9 mins]

Readings in bold are the most important ones.

\## Optional bonus resources

- From the last reading above, either study one proposal deeply or skim several [1–5 hrs]:
    - Physicalist Superimitation (previously called Pre-DCA) [2022]
    - Universal Alignment Test (UAT) [2022]
    - Metaethical AI [2019]
- Agent Foundations for Aligning Machine Intelligence with Human Interests – Nate Soares, Benja Fallenstein [2017, 42 mins]
- MIRI's Approach – Nate Soares [2015, 20 mins]
- The Learning-Theoretic Agenda: Status 2023 – Vanessa Kosoy [2023, 66 mins]
