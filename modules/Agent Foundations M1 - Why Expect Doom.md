tldr:: Why the move to superintelligence looks so dangerous, in five readings by Rob Bensinger, Eliezer Yudkowsky and Nate Soares.
summary_for_tutor:: Opening page of Module 1 of Agent Foundations for Superintelligence-Robust Alignment, a course by Alignment Ecosystem Development reproduced on Lens. The module asks why the transition to superintelligence is so dangerous. The readings that follow are The basic reasons I expect AGI ruin (Rob Bensinger), AGI Ruin: A List of Lethalities (Eliezer Yudkowsky), then a choice between Yudkowsky's two posts Cascades, Cycles, Insight and Recursion, Magic or Rob Miles's Computerphile video AI Self Improvement, then A central AI alignment problem: capabilities generalization, and the sharp left turn and What I mean by "alignment is in large part about making cognition aimable at all" (both Nate Soares). Bensinger and the sharp left turn post are marked as the most important.
