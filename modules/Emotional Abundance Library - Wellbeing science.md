%% Library module, wellbeing science and positive psychology. Profile: claims (happiness is measurable by self-report, has several parts, comes partly from genes and circumstances and partly from what people do, and brief exercises can raise it), main people (Diener, Seligman, Lyubomirsky and Sheldon, critics Brown and Rohrer, White and colleagues, Folk and Dunn), method (self-report scales, panel studies, randomised trials of brief exercises), evidence (early meta-analyses large, corrected ones small, recent network meta-analysis moderate against inactive controls, few preregistered studies), commercial interests (trade books, PERMA workshops, the 40% pie in popular books), experiments (five sourced exercises with SPANE and SWLS). The school says little about the self or how experience is built, so the module does not pad that part. Required about 2 hours 20 minutes by the lenses' own estimates. No learning outcome: the testable skill (reading intervention evidence) belongs to module 3, and the pie lens has three graded questions instead. The original 2005 pie paper and White et al. 2019 could not be imported (importer failures on 2026-10-05), so they are linked and quoted briefly. %%
