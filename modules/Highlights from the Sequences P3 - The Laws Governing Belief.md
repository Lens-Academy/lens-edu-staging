# Lens: Part 3 of 6: The Laws Governing Belief
id:: 25622a08-f80c-4415-a674-c0fb6e411628
tldr:: Your beliefs are your own, but the evidence you have fixes which beliefs are correct.
summary_for_tutor:: Opening page of Part 3 of Highlights from the Sequences. The part is about the rules that connect evidence to belief: beliefs are subjective, but given the evidence there are laws that fix what you should believe. Eleven essays by Eliezer Yudkowsky follow, in this order: Making Beliefs Pay Rent (in Anticipated Experiences), What is Evidence?, Scientific Evidence, Legal Evidence, Rational Evidence, How Much Evidence Does It Take?, Absence of Evidence Is Evidence of Absence, Conservation of Expected Evidence, Argument Screens Off Authority, An Intuitive Explanation of Bayes's Theorem, The Second Law of Thermodynamics, and Engines of Cognition, Toolbox-thinking and Law-thinking, and Local Validity as a Key to Sanity and Civilization.
duration_minutes:: 1
#### Text
content::
This is part 3 of 6: *The Laws Governing Belief*. Beliefs are subjective, but you can't pick them as you like. Given the evidence, there are laws that say what you should believe.
