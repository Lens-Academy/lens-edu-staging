# Lens: Part 1 of 6: Thinking Better on Purpose
id:: 6026610a-0c78-4838-8738-61ac59ba0c0f
tldr:: Humans can think about their own thinking, and this part is about using that to find and fix the flaws in how we reason.
summary_for_tutor:: Opening page of part 1 of Highlights from the Sequences. The part is about the human ability to reflect on our own reasoning, notice its flaws and improve it deliberately. The essays that follow are The Lens That Sees Its Flaws, What Do We Mean By "Rationality"?, Humans are not automatically strategic (Anna Salamon), Use the Try Harder, Luke, Your Strength as a Rationalist, The Meditation on Curiosity, The Importance of Saying "Oops", The Martial Art of Rationality and Twelve Virtues of Rationality, all by Eliezer Yudkowsky unless noted.
duration_minutes:: 1
#### Text
content::
This is part 1 of 6 of *Highlights from the Sequences*, LessWrong's selection of essays on how to think more clearly and act more effectively.

Humans can think about their own thinking. That lets us notice where our default ways of reasoning go wrong, and work on purpose to do better. The essays in this part are:

1. *The Lens That Sees Its Flaws* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
2. *What Do We Mean By "Rationality"?* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
3. *Humans are not automatically strategic* by Anna Salamon
4. *Use the Try Harder, Luke* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
5. *Your Strength as a Rationalist* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
6. *The Meditation on Curiosity* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
7. *The Importance of Saying "Oops"* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
8. *The Martial Art of Rationality* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
9. *Twelve Virtues of Rationality* by Eliezer Yudkowsky
