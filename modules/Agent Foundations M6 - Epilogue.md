*The epilogue of the [Agent Foundations course](https://agentfoundations.study/#epilogue) by Alignment Ecosystem Development.*

Still eager for more? Here are some assorted extras you can peruse.

- [Eliezer Yudkowsky – Why AI Will Kill Us, Aligning LLMs, Nature of Intelligence, SciFi, & Rationality](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41SUp-TRVlg) – Dwarkesh Patel [2023]
- [Alignment Research Field Guide](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/PqMT9zGrNsGJNfiFR/alignment-research-field-guide) – Abram Demski [2019]
- [AISafety.info](https://aisafety.info/) (FAQ with hundreds of answers) – Rob Miles’s Team [updated 2025]
- [AI Safety Chatbot](https://aisafety.info/chat/) (LLM summarizations of content from the Alignment Research Dataset) [updated 2025]
- [AI alignment on Arbital](https://arbital.greaterwrong.com/explore/ai_alignment/) (wiki) – Eliezer Yudkowsky [2015]
- ["Why Not Just..."](https://www.lesswrong.com/s/TLSzP4xP42PPBctgw) – John Wentworth [2022]
- [Intro to Agent Foundations (Understanding Infra-Bayesianism Part 4)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SLQDNRbihsk) – Jack Parker [2022]

\## Other things worth knowing about

[AISafety.com](https://www.aisafety.com/): A central hub for AI safety resources. See especially the job board and the section on funding sources.

[AI Safety Quest](https://aisafety.quest/): Helps new people navigate the AI safety ecosystem, connect with like-minded people and find projects that are a good fit for their skills

[BlueDot Impact](https://bluedot.org/courses): The standard 3 month-long introductory course running two tracks: Alignment and Governance
