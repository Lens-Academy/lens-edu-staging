This course assumes AI Control Part 1. It picks up where Part 1 stopped, in the middle of XLab's module on high-stakes control, and runs to the end of XLab's track.

Five days, each about four to five hours of reading and exercises, each closing with a group meeting:

- **Day 1.** Two guided exercises on high-stakes protocols, collusion in untrusted monitoring and legibility for trusted monitoring, then the shift to low-stakes control.
- **Day 2.** Sandbagging, capability elicitation and exploration hacking.
- **Day 3.** A research-sabotage benchmark and the long guided exercise on exploration hacking.
- **Day 4.** Threat models beyond scheming: reward seekers, satiable preferences, spillway motivations, and how to measure reward seeking.
- **Day 5.** Deals with early misaligned AIs, and where to go next.

The alpha notice from Part 1 still applies: XLab expects the material to change after its paid pilot cohort, and would appreciate issues sent through [their feedback form](https://forms.gle/KkWcHkKh87pygDzw9).
