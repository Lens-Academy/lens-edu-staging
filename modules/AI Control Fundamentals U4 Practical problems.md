**Part 3: Practical problems**

The criticisms in Parts 1 and 2 would hold even if control were done well. The ones in this part are different. They are practical problems: ways control could work less well than it seems, which more research or better practice might fix.

This part covers:
- Control evaluations may overstate safety.
- Optional: the AI may game its own evaluation.
- Catching and retraining an AI may breed better schemers.

For each one, ask how much more work could fix it, and how bad it is until then. The part ends with the unit's three short tests.
