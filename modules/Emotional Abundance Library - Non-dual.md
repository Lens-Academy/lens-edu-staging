Required path about 1 h 45 min by the lenses' own estimates, plus the experiments themselves (2 minutes for the pointing experiment, 26 minutes for the guided session, two weeks of short daily practice for the main experiment). Optional: classical Advaita (IEP), the rest of Spira's talk, Tolle's talk, Ramm's essay on the headless way, Deane et al. on depersonalisation, Welwood on spiritual bypassing.
