\## This week's project work

**Session 1, ninety minutes: the crappy version.** Open a blank document and produce the version you described in the proposal: the table with three rows from memory, the one-page outline, the first paragraph for the reader, the list of what the deliverable will contain. No new reading. If you catch yourself opening a source, close it and write what you would have expected it to say; you will check in the second session.

**Session 2, ninety minutes: the two unknowns.** Your proposal named two things that could sink the project. Find out. A good outcome is an answer; an acceptable outcome is knowing that no public answer exists, which is itself a finding your deliverable can carry. If time remains, start on the brief's sources properly.

Keep a running list of what surprised you. The method check at the start of next week asks for it, and it is the evidence your decision rests on.

\## Before meeting 2

- Proposal submitted (the previous lens).
- A two-minute spoken pitch: question, reader, deliverable, crappy version.
- The crappy version itself, however rough, and one sentence on what building it taught you.
