%% Module 6 has three parts. Part 1 (goal 1) reads each view of the self in its own words: Buddhist not-self and the no-self debate, Hume and Parfit, Ramana and IFS, Dennett and Strawson, Metzinger, Seth and Thompson, then one comparison table (Wright versus Thompson folded into the table and its note, as briefed). Part 2 (goal 2) turns "selflessness" into checkable claims: Lindahl and Britton's six changes, Martin's PNSE self reports and the checks against partners and body, and three brain-study abstracts read as a lesson in the limits of such evidence. Part 3 runs short sourced experiments with a written prediction, a baseline and the NADA-S ratings, each with a stop rule sourced to Britton and Cheetah House. Noting and the Loch Kelly glimpse are optional. Two learning outcomes: recognising views and telling verbal from factual disagreements, and checking a selflessness claim. %%
