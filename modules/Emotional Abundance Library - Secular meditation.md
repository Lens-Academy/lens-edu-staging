%% Library module, secular and pragmatic meditation. Order: the claims and main voices (Kabat-Zinn and MBSR, Harris), the trial evidence (Van Dam, Goyal via Van Dam, Galante), Shinzen Young (most operationalised system, suffering = pain x resistance, one dismantling RCT), Culadasa (stage model, short), Ingram and pragmatic dharma (noting, progress of insight, Dark Night, no perpetual bliss) with Analayo's critique, harms (Van Dam on measurement, Britton 2021), then experiments. Waking Up has no lens of its own: it is Harris's app, his instructions are in the first lens and no trial of the app turned up in our search (2026-10-04). No learning outcome: the module is a profile to compare with other schools, and the one testable skill here (reading an effect size against active controls) belongs to M3. Adverse effects in depth belong to the separate contemplative science library module. Required about 2 hours by the lens estimates. %%
