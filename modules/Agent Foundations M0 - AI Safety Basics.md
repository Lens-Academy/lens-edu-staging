*Module 0 of the [Agent Foundations course](https://agentfoundations.study/#module0) by Alignment Ecosystem Development.*

Skip this module if you're already familiar with the basics of AI safety. Otherwise choose one (or more) of the options below, depending on your preferred learning style.

\## Option 1: Book 📕

*The AI Does Not Hate You* by Tom Chivers is the most accessible and accurate long-form introduction to AI extinction risk. Later chapters cover things which will be of interest to some, but can be skipped.

- The AI Does Not Hate You (part 1 and 2 only) – Tom Chivers [2019, 3 hours]

\## Option 2: Engaging and graphical 🖼️

This option collects some of the most engaging introductions, while covering the core of the concerns.

- The Artificial Intelligence Revolution: Part 1 and Part 2 – Tim Urban [2015, 1:50 hrs]
- The Most Important Century – Holden Karnofsky [2021, 9 mins]
- Four Background Claims – Nate Soares [2015, 15 mins]

\## Option 3: Info-dense 🔍

*Superintelligence* by Nick Bostrom [2014] introduced the field to the world, and has an incredible amount of information. Some people love the style, some really don't get on well with it. It is somewhat outdated as it pre-dates LLMs, but many chapters are still relevant, and it’s available free online as well as in paperback/ebook/audiobook form. The following are the most important chapters.

- Preface [4 mins]
- 5. Decisive strategic advantage [24 mins]
- 6. Cognitive superpowers [27 mins]
- 7. The superintelligent will [21 mins]
- 9. The control problem [36 mins]
- 12. Acquiring values [51 mins]
- 13. Choosing the criteria for choosing [42 mins]
- 14. The strategic picture [57 mins]

\## Option 4: Video 🎥

This track is for people who strongly prefer video, and is supplemented with the Wikipedia article for more coverage.

- AI Safety Intro playlist (all except the 1hr+ videos) – Stampy’s AI Safety Info [2015–2025, 4 hrs]
- Existential risk from artificial intelligence – Wikipedia [updated 2025, 38 mins]
