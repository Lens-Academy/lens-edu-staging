%% Library module for the Emotional Abundance course: self-compassion (Kristin Neff, Mindful Self-Compassion), compassion-focused therapy (Paul Gilbert, threat, drive and soothing systems) and contingent self-worth (Jennifer Crocker). Same profile as the other library modules: claims, people, sources and method, evidence with critiques and commercial interests, experiments with sourced protocols. Order: Neff's own first exercise before any theory, then the three primary voices, then the evidence, then experiments. No learning outcome: the one testable skill (judging what the evidence shows) is practised by the graded question on the evidence page. Required time about 2 hours 20 minutes by the lenses' own estimates, plus the experiment itself. Two optional lenses (about 40 minutes together): the Self-Compassion Scale dispute, and Neff's written case. Ferrari et al. 2019 could not be imported (the importer got only the abstract), so the evidence page summarises and links it. Gilbert's public handout was still in the import queue at review time, so the Gilbert page embeds his 2014 paper and summarises the handout with page numbers. %%
