\## This week's project work

Block ninety minutes before meeting 1. Open a blank document and produce the crappy version from your proposal: the table with three rows from memory, the one-page outline, the first paragraph for the reader, the list of what the deliverable will contain. No new reading. If you catch yourself opening a source, close it and write what you would have expected it to say; you will check next week.

At the end of the session you should have one artifact you could show your group, and a list of the questions that came up while writing it. Both come to meeting 1.

\## Before meeting 1

- Proposal submitted (the previous lens).
- A two-minute spoken pitch: question, reader, deliverable, crappy version.
- One artifact from this session, however rough.
