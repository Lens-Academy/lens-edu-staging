\## Before meeting 1

Two things to bring:

- **Your defended-ranking memo.** You will give it in two minutes: the policy goal, the portfolio you recommended, and the residual blind spot with its owner. The group's job is to find the blind spot you did not name.
- **One mechanism you rated differently from the reference map**, and the evidence that moved you. Disagreement with the map is fine; the meeting is where it gets tested.

Next week the capstone bank opens. If you want a head start, keep the research tips' three questions in mind while you present: who would act on your memo, which corner of it is tractable, and what its crappy version would have been.
