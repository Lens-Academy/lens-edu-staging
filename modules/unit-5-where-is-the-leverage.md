Ordering: the handoff first, because it repositions everything from Unit 4 (the
playbook's plans end with AIs doing most of the remaining safety work, which is where
the alignment door signposted since Unit 1 actually leads). Then the target (preventing takeover is not the
same as a future worth wanting). Then the menu of concrete work. Then the reading stops
and the student takes over.

Day Zero, Revisited is the terminal exercise of the whole course, and its max-time:: 15
reflects that: it is the one time the student is told to read their day-zero paragraph
BEFORE writing, reversing the write-first-compare-after order every earlier revision
used. The tutor cannot see that day-zero text and its brief says so. The Lunch Table
needs a build-time selection of one real, dated, primary-sourced development; house rule,
no fabricated specifics.
