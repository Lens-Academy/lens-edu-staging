\## This week's project work

About three and a half hours, on the revision plan you wrote: three changes, in order, with hours against each.

- Finish change one before opening change two. A draft with one change complete and two untouched is better than three half-made.
- If change one runs over, decide at the halfway point of your hours: finish it smaller, or drop change three. Do not silently let the week absorb it.
- Everything you drop or defer goes into the ten-more-hours note next week. Nothing disappears.

\## Before meeting 4

- Peer review sent to your partner within three days of meeting 3, and pasted into the previous lens.
- Your response to the review you received, and your revision plan (previous lens).
- One review point you rejected, and your reason, ready for the group to red-team.
