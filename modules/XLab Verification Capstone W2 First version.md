\## This week's project work

About four hours, in this order:

1. **Finish the crappy version.** Every section exists, even as a heading and a guess. Do not polish anything.
2. **Chase the two unknowns.** Your proposal named two things that could sink the project. Spend the middle of the week finding out. A good outcome is an answer; an acceptable outcome is knowing that no public answer exists, which is itself a finding your deliverable can carry.
3. **Only then read more widely.** If hours remain, start on the brief's sources properly.

Keep a running list of what surprised you. The method check at the end of the week asks for it, and it is the evidence your decision rests on.

\## Before meeting 2

- Method check submitted (the next lens).
- Your decision, continue, re-scope, or switch, and one sentence of reason.
