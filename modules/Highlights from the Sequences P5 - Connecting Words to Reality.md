# Lens: Part 5 of 6: Connecting Words to Reality
id:: 2c66e927-703b-43fc-a9e7-5ea13c50228b
tldr:: This part is about how we form concepts and use words to talk about them, which you need to know to make sense of confusing topics.
summary_for_tutor:: Intro to part 5 of 6 of Highlights from the Sequences, "Connecting Words to Reality". It covers how we form concepts and use words for them, and how that helps with confusing questions. The essays that follow, in order: Taboo Your Words, Dissolving the Question, Diseased thinking (Scott Alexander), Hug the Query, Say Not "Complexity", Mind Projection Fallacy, How An Algorithm Feels From Inside, Expecting Short Inferential Distances, and Illusion of Transparency. All except Diseased thinking are by Eliezer Yudkowsky. Each essay lens ends with one applied question.
duration_minutes:: 1

content::
This is part 5 of 6: *Connecting Words to Reality*. To understand confusing topics, you need to understand how we build concepts and attach words to them.
