%% Module 5 teaches what the schools say about wanting and where they agree and disagree. Part 1 asks why wanting outlasts getting (neuroscience of wanting and liking, evolution, rising aspirations). Part 2 reads the schools that give advice about wanting: early Buddhism (craving versus wholesome desire), self-determination theory (required: acting from wanting rather than from having to is central to the course's question about desire), Epicurus and the Stoics, and the research on valuing happiness. Part 3 compares them in one table (Maslow, hedonic adaptation and the Yoga kleshas appear only there) and carries the one learning outcome: classifying a case of wanting with three distinctions. Part 4 offers six experiments with sourced protocols. Required time by the lenses' own estimates: about 101 min reading plus 89 min tutor (about 3 hours 10 minutes), plus the 12-minute test. Five optional lenses add about 70 minutes. Ryan and Deci 2000, Nesse 2004 and Kasser 2016 could not be imported (the importer's review was blocked), so those lenses assign exact passages in the linked PDFs.
