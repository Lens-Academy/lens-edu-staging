\## Alpha course

We're currently in the alpha testing stage and running a small paid cohort to calibrate and improve the course ahead of the official launch. The materials are open for anyone to use in the meantime. If you do use them, we'd really appreciate it if you could fill out [this feedback form](https://forms.gle/KkWcHkKh87pygDzw9). Your feedback will help us identify issues, calibrate the course, and make improvements before launch.

This Lens version follows [XLab's current public curriculum](https://aisafetytracks.com/tracks/control). XLab expects the material to change substantially after the paid cohort.

\## How to use this course

Everything is here on Lens: the readings, XLab's own explanations, its exercises, and its interactive pieces. Work straight through each module in order. Every page ends with a Works cited box linking the original sources, including the XLab lesson it was adapted from, if you want to go back to either.

Two things are worth knowing before you start. The estimated times on each page are ours rather than XLab's, and they count the exercises as well as the reading, so they run longer than the figures on XLab's site. And a handful of pages are marked optional: they are genuinely optional, so follow your curiosity rather than completeness.
