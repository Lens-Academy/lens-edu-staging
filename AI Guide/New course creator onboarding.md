# Welcome to Lens Academy course creation

This link gives you edit access to the `Lens Edu` folder in Lens Relay Editor. Please do not share it. It acts as your personal access credential and expires on 31 December 2026.

Lens Relay is our shared course-authoring system. Changes made here sync to the staging version of Lens Academy within seconds.

## 1. Connect your AI to Lens Relay

Follow [[How to connect Claude]].

Your access token is the part after `?t=` in the original link Elias sent you. Save that original link before opening it.

Your MCP URL has this format:

```
https://relay.lensacademy.org/mcp/<your-token>
```

You can use Claude, Claude Code, Codex, or another MCP-compatible AI.

## 2. Learn how courses work

Ask your AI:

> Use the Lens Relay MCP to read `Lens Edu/AI Guide/Course Authoring` and walk me through `Lens Edu/courses/Demo Course`. Explain how each file maps to what learners see on Lens Academy. Answer my questions as we go.

Open [[../courses/Demo Course]] and read the course file first. Then follow its module links from top to bottom. It demonstrates how courses, modules, lenses, learning outcomes, tests, meetings, and surveys fit together.

You can also explore the learner-facing platform at https://lensacademy.org/.

## 3. Start creating

Use your AI through MCP while creating course content. Ask it to:

- Find relevant examples.
- Explain formatting.
- Create or edit files.
- Check your work with the content validator.

Treat the AI Guide and existing course files as source of truth. Do not guess syntax.

Spend roughly 1 to 2 hours exploring the editor, Demo Course, and learner-facing platform. Then send Elias any questions your AI could not answer.

If the editor link or MCP connection fails, let Elias know.
