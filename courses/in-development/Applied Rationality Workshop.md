title: "Applied Rationality Workshop"
description: "A six-week workshop in applied rationality, built on the CFAR Handbook by the Center for Applied Rationality. For people who want to think better and act on it, many of them because they want to work on AI safety. You write a list of your own real problems in week 1 and use every technique on it: planning that survives contact with reality, habits that fire on their own, motivation you can take apart, comfort-zone experiments, inner conflicts, and disagreements with other people. The AI tutor is your practice partner, each week ends with a real-life experiment you report back on, and weekly group meetings run Hamming circles and pair debugging. About three hours a week."

%%
Target audience (people with one or more of these):
- wants to think better and act on it, on purpose
- takes AI risk seriously and wants to work on it, and sees thinking better as part of that
- has real problems to work on: a stalled project, a decision, a habit, motivation, a disagreement

Value prop:
- The CFAR workshop format at home: techniques from the CFAR Handbook, applied to your own bugs list, not to hypotheticals
- The AI tutor as a pair-debugging partner for every technique
- A weekly experiment loop, so techniques get used in real life and reported on
- Group meetings for Hamming circles, pair debugging, Double Crux and a tutoring wheel, or a solo version of each meeting

Links:
- Research notes: https://editor.lensacademy.org/0fd0e1b1/Lens/Research/CFAR-workshops-and-handbook.md
%%
