## How the estimates are made

- **Reading:** page text at 200 words a minute. Excerpts at 200 for essays, narrative, primers and magazine articles; 175 for policy reports; 150 for academic papers, legal texts and technical documents. Videos at their running time. Widgets by what the learner does in them, which is the least certain part. Reading that a page sends the learner to do off the page is counted, as the AI Guide asks.
- **Tutor:** per question at the AI Guide's sizes: about 3 minutes for a quick check, 5 to 6 for explain or apply, up to 8 for multi-part, and written answers judged one by one.
- **When a time changes:** only when the estimate differs from it by more than a quarter or 5 minutes, whichever is bigger, and the gap survives the neighboring reading rate. Gaps that depend on the rate or on a widget guess are listed as "keep" with the reason.
- **Unit totals:** required pages plus the outcome tests' writing minutes. Optional pages are left out, as the platform leaves them out, and the Distiller's choose-one group counts at its average.
