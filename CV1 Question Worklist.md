# CV1 question worklist

Stage 4 of [[CV1 Restructure Log]], step 1: every question in a CV1 lens, with the call proposed for it. Nothing here is built. Built 2026-10-23 from the staging repository, by question id, so a row still finds its question after stage 3 moves it to another page. Outcome tests and the unit feedback pages are left out, as the plan says.

**How to read it.** "Now" is the question as it stands. "Call" applies the rules already agreed: A1, ungrade where the key is on the page and keep the key as a self-check; A2, keep the grade on longer questions with no key in units 3 to 5, which have no outcomes; single-answer choice questions keep their grade. Rows marked "(proposed)" are the case-by-case calls for units 1 and 2 (A10) and need your yes or no. "Brief" is what happens to the feedback brief. "Optional" says whether a learner can tell the question is optional. Comment on any row to change its call.

**What it changes, if every call stands.** Graded questions go from 44 to 21. The 21 shared "XLab writing or reflection" briefs are replaced with briefs written for their questions (A6). First-answer feedback goes from 0 questions to the 52 that have a brief (A13). Four optional questions that say so nowhere get "Optional:" at the start of their prompt.

**Applies to every row.**

- Every question with a brief gets `force-feedback:: first`. The four without one (the welcome note, the two essays' pick-a-recommendation questions, and "Explore AI 2027") need no feedback and stay as they are.
- An ungraded question's rubric folds into its brief, since the tutor of an ungraded question sees only the brief: its elements stay as what to look for, its point weights and caps go, and its model answer stays as reference. Only graded questions mention a score.
- A reworded prompt takes its brief with it in the same edit.
- Edits go around notes from Elias's AI, never through them. The Notes column marks the lenses that have them.
- XLab named in a brief or rubric is decided item by item (A9). The validator warns on it outside the Works cited.
