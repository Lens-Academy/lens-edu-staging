We're witnessing a major revolution in AI. Exciting new models are generating realistic text, creating beautiful images, writing new code, and composing music. These generative capabilities are powered by foundation models. These are artificial intelligence systems trained on massive amounts of internet data including text, images, videos, and more.

Foundation models unlock powerful new possibilities. Instead of one model built solely for one task, foundation models can be adapted across a wide variety of different scenarios: summarizing documents, generating stories, answering questions, writing code, solving math problems, synthesizing audio, and the list never ends.

But foundation models bear serious risks. Because they are trained on data from the internet, they can exhibit bias and stereotypes that are harmful to marginalized groups. They can be used by malicious actors to create disinformation. How these models are built and deployed is determined by a few people with limited transparency. And because they could cost tens or even hundreds of millions of dollars to train, only the biggest tech companies can afford them, which continues to centralize power.

Foundation models are shaping our lives, transforming how we think about technology, and restructuring our collective society. How we interact with computers and each other is changing, from how we search the web to how we draft an email. Soon anyone will be able to write a coherent essay from a loose collection of thoughts or generate synthetic art and music that is hard to distinguish from the real thing.

Spanning industry, government, academia, and the public at large, we should come together to ensure this emerging technology is built and deployed responsibly. We must act now to ensure foundation models develop in ways that benefit society.
