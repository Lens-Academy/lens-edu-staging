THINK:  Thought challenging from Getselfhelp. co. uk THINK:  Is this thought.... True - Helpful - Important - Necessary - Kind  ? As soon as you notice an upsetting thought ask yourself is this thought... True - Helpful - Important - Necessary - Kind  ? Is this thought True? Is there evidence confirming this? What are the facts? Is this thought a Helpful one right now? Is this thought really Important right now? Is this thought Necessary? Do I need to act on it right now? Is this thought kind? is it kind to me? is it kind to others? what IS True? What WOULD be Helpful? what IS Important? what IS Necessary?
