Have you seen this? Look at this. This is a vine, like the plant, like it's just in super fast motion. This is sped up footage. Isn't that crazy? Look how alive it is. It's like an animal.

We don't care about plants at all, right? Like they don't matter. We can just chop them down if they're in the way, burn them, whatever. But plants really have a lot more going on than we usually think. They're just really slow.

And that made me realize, oh, this is how advanced AI is going to see us in the future, isn't it? Like to an AI that thinks at the speed of electronics, we would basically be like trees. The AIs will be like, oh, I guess yeah, the humans do show some signs of intelligent behavior. If you leave them alone for like thousands of milliseconds at a time, you can see them starting to do some stuff, but eh, it's not really relevant. We can just do what we want with them.
