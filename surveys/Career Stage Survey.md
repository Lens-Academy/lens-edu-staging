%% DRAFT (Mark, 30 Sep 2026). A check-in sent to alumni every 3 months after their course (3, 6 and 12 months). Not attached to any meeting, and not automated yet: send it by email or Discord for now. The questions reuse the exact wording and options of the application form (`ais_work_status`, `ai_safety_programs_other`, `ais_connections`) and of the final survey's next-steps section, so every answer compares with the one before. Outcome categories (A1–C) and how we report them: [[../../Lens/Growth/Impact Tracking/Career Outcomes and Attribution]]. Anyone who reports a paid full-time role or fellowship (A1 or A2) also gets the [[Role Attribution Survey]], sent separately. %%
