# CV2 streamlining: log

Companion to [[CV2 Streamlining Proposal]]. The proposal holds the plan, this holds the decisions and what was done. CV1's records are [[CV1 Restructure Log]] and [[AIRF Restructure - Working Preferences]], whose working rules apply here too. Decisions here are numbered B1, B2 and so on, so they never collide with CV1's A-numbers.

**Status, 2026-11-24.** The proposal is agreed apart from one open question (Q1). Stage 3, step 1 is done: the module titles are back in CV1's form, and the meeting headings keep units 6 to 10 in title case (B9). The overview's five box titles also take title case, for consistency, though the platform shows them in capitals (Andreas). Stage 3, step 2 is done: XLab's lesson numbers are back in the 20 Works cited entries, in title case, and the four restored lessons say XLab removed them and link to their last version on GitHub (B10). Stage 3, step 3 is done: link labels describe where they lead in plain words, as CV1's do ("the next page", "the expert review at the end of this unit", "unit 4 of Compute Verification 1"), rather than spelling out page titles (B11), and learner-facing text says "page" where it said "lens". Stage 3, step 4 is done: the red-line question points to "the four questions above" and is "Graded on" its five criteria instead of going through peer review. Andreas raised two structural ideas for stage 4 (Q3, Q4). Next: stage 3, step 5, the objective-box titles.
